## M1 · Demographics — a 100-million market that is urbanizing, ageing and trading up at once

> SO WHAT: So what: Vietnam is not a "young frontier market" anymore — it is a fast-ageing, below-replacement-fertility, 42%-urban market whose commercial centre of gravity is two metros (HCMC, Hanoi) and whose demand base is a middle class doubling from ~13M to ~26M people between 2023 and 2026.
