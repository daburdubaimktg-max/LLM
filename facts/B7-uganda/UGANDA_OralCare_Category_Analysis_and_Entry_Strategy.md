## C1 — The ask

> SO WHAT: The decision this deck asks for: approve AMANNYO — (1) launch Dabur Herbal as the genuine-herbal family tube at the UGX 5,000–8,000 single-tube seat, on the hair wave's distribution (macro W2), with the same Q-mark + batch-QR authenticity dress; (2) run an oral-health education platform — radio + schools — that builds the brand as the teeth-teacher in a market where the habit itself is still forming (toothpaste penetration, not brand share, is the growth engine); (3) hold Meswak as a targeted second SKU for Muslim-segment dukas and Eid windows (14–35% of the population, miswak practice present), not as the lead. No multipack war with Colgate; no mouthwash this cycle.
