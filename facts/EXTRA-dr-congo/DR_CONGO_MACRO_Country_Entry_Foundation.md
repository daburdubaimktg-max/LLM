## M6 · Regulation: border-control country, not dossier country

| T8 · THE IMPORT MACHINERY STACK (all INDEX-DERIVED from official-adjacent sources) | Instrument | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Importer status | Numéro import-export | Approved by Ministry of Foreign Trade, registered with Banque Centrale du Congo; valid one calendar year, renewable by 31 March; online-only via GUICE since 5 Oct 2025 — the distributor partner holds this, not Dabur |
| 2 · Per shipment | DIB (Déclaration d'Importation de Biens, ex-licence modèle IB) | Subscribed with a commercial bank for imports >US$2,500 CIF |
| 3 · Pre-shipment | Bureau Veritas BIVAC inspection | Attestation de Vérification issued at origin (RAK/Mumbai), transmitted to OCC via the single window (verigates datasheet rev.25) |
| 4 · At border | OCC (Office Congolais de Contrôle) | Quality/quantity/conformity control incl. physico-chemical and microbiological analysis |
| 5 · Label law | French labelling | Production date, expiry, origin, production code, quantity, batch, manufacturer — in French; non-compliance → refusal or destruction. RAK export packs need over-labelling or dedicated Francophone artwork |
| 6 · Duty stack | Tariff bands 5/10/20% + 16% import VAT | Cosmetics typically top band — line-level rates UNCONFIRMED this pass ; DGDA chapter-33 pull is T21 queue #4. No pricing model ships until it lands |
