**THE PLAY:** DEEP THINK — interrogating the accident. The deck's founding fact deserves hostile examination: does one marketplace listing at RWF 3,500 really prove a premium slot? Three deflationary readings. (1) It's a price, not a velocity — a listing proves a buyer's judgement, not a consumer's; Sawa Citi may have sold twelve tubes in a year. Answer: partially conceded — which is why the bet's terms measure maintained price AND repeat, and why the Sawa Citi meeting (week 1–3) asks for the sell-through data that converts the anecdote into evidence before launch capital commits. (2) It's a diaspora niche — Kigali's Indian community and health-conscious expats could absorb a marketplace trickle without any Rwandan mass demand existing. Answer: plausible, and priced in — the launch design leads with supermarket + Goodlife doors where the mainstream shopper walks, and the kill-switch's fallback (75g-led mainstream positioning) exists precisely for this scenario. (3) The price is high BECAUSE it's grey — parallel-import economics (small lots, retail-sourced in Nairobi, double margins) force 3,500; a formal route would land cheaper and the "premium" is an artefact of inefficiency. Answer: partly true and GOOD news — formal economics at the same shelf price convert the grey route's cost burden into Dabur's margin (the ~8% contribution line). The synthesis: the accident is a hypothesis with unusually strong provenance (a professional buyer's real money), not a proof — and the entire 90-day plan is engineered to test it before the July gate. Second-order: formalising TEACHES the grey trade that Dabur enforces — the first market where Track-and-Trace visibly separates authorized from parallel stock sets the precedent the whole EAC programme will lean on.

**THE PLAY:** DEEP STRAT — the education seat, engineered for a governed state. Rwanda's version of the programme's recurring education play must fit a state that runs its school system centrally and its health messaging through 30,000 CHWs — freelance brand programmes do not get invited twice. The design: (i) diligence before design — confirm no incumbent programme exists (BSBF unconfirmed is not BSBF absent); (ii) ministry authorship — the module is MoH/MoE curriculum with Dabur as named sponsor, not a Dabur campaign with a ministry logo; in a governed market the inversion is the difference between a decade of presence and a one-term pilot; (iii) CHW respect — the community health worker is the trusted health voice; the programme trains teachers WITH CHW-compatible materials rather than around them; (iv) the measurable — brushing-frequency baseline and follow-up in programme schools, published jointly — the governed state loves data, and a published result is equity no competitor can seize without years; (v) the commercial firewall — no product sampling in classrooms (the governed market would read it as capture); the brand earns the association, the shelf converts it. Works if: the seat is open and the ministries want a funded, authored programme. Fails if: policy forbids brand sponsorship in schools — then the same module deploys through Goodlife/pharmacy counters and CHW cooperative channels as community education, slower but unblocked. Cost: modest; the asset: the only durable differentiation available in a category where everyone imports and nobody owns a story.
