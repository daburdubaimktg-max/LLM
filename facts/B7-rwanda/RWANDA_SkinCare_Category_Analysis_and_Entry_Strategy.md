**THE PLAY:** DEEP STRAT — the one-bottle bridge, engineered. The Vatika coconut body-oil bridge is the deck's only genuinely new commercial idea, and its engineering is deliberately minimal: the SAME 100ml bottle the hair deck launches as R1 appears on the skin shelf and in Kasha's body-care category with dual-use copy («for hair and body» — the cow-ghee tradition never separated them; kwisiga applies to both). One SKU, one registration, one price (1,800–2,500), two facings universes. The economics: dual-aisle placement roughly doubles the bottle's velocity potential at zero incremental supply cost, and the heritage register does the positioning work in both aisles simultaneously. The risks are two: shelf confusion (solved by shelf-talker discipline — the duka nails it to one location anyway) and category-copy drift (the panel clears ONE copy set serving both aisles; no aisle-specific claims that could diverge). Works if: consumers read oil as oil, the way their grandmothers did. Fails if: modern-trade buyers demand aisle-specific SKUs — in which case the bridge splits into two labels at W2 with the velocity data deciding whether the second registration is worth US$1,250. The bridge is the passenger model's purest expression: a product that travels on another deck's economics end to end.
