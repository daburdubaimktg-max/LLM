**THE PLAY:** DEEP THINK — the promo culture, understood before it is fought. A 46% cut on the franchise flagship did not happen by accident; it happens because every actor in the current system is locally rational. The trade wants footfall events; the distributor wants volume against targets; the regional office wants share defence against Parachute's price points; and the multipack is the weapon all three reach for because it moves cases without a strategy meeting. The discipline project fails if it reads as head-office moralising against local rationality — so it must CHANGE the payoffs, not the sermon. (1) For the trade: designed Ramadan/National-Day bundle events with better theatre than a shelf-edge cut (hamper formats, gift-with-purchase, app-exclusive offers) — footfall preserved, price integrity kept. (2) For the distributor: the twin-waterfall arithmetic shared openly — a 46% cut on a QAR 27 pack burns roughly QAR 3 of DISTRIBUTOR margin per unit too; discipline is their margin recovery as much as Dabur's. (3) For the regional office: the share-defence job reassigned to the honest value multipack (R1's designed ≤20% bundles) — Parachute is fought with architecture, not panic. Second-order: the first two quarters after withdrawal will show a volume dip as discount-hunters churn — the plan pre-declares it (the bet measures realised price and contribution, not tonnage) so nobody re-opens the discount tap to chase a vanity metric. Kill-test: if realised price per unit hasn't risen ≥20% by month 12, the governance design — not the thesis — gets rebuilt.
