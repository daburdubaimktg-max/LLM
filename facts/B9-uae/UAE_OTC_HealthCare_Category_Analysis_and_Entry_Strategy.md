## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. UAE OTC health is a US$1.8–2.9bn pool growing 4–8% with the fastest sub-pool — dietary supplements, US$570.6M at 11.5% CAGR — growing at twice the base rate (Credence Research 2024; Grand View Research 2024). Dabur already holds three quiet assets no other Indian entrant has: Honitus is MOHAP-licensed for general sale (the same regulatory lane Prospan used to win the pharmacist's first recommendation), Chyawanprash is an Amazon.ae bestseller at 4.1–4.2★ across 5,000+ reviews, and the RAK/JAFZA base makes duty and logistics moot . The threat is dated and named: Himalaya broke ground on an AED 200M herbal-pharmaceutical factory at Dubai Industrial City on 25 Oct 2023 (3bn tablets + 15M syrup bottles/yr, EDB-financed, paired with a Dubai Science Park R&D centre) — once it commissions, "Made in UAE" + GCC logistics + MOHAP proximity lock the regulated-herbal shelf. The ask: a 24-month "Shifa Protocol" — (1) pharmacy-franchise build on the Honitus template into Life's 600+ doors with Koflet/Prospan-style detailing; (2) re-frame Chyawanprash for Arab shoppers under its own Arabic-lineage name ʿAwāliḥ (عوالح) with "Ayurveda as longevity science" language; (3) own the Ramadan clock (Hajmola = iftar digestion, Chyawanprash + honey = suhoor stamina) — verified unowned in UAE OTC comms; (4) barbell Dabur Honey: hold the AED 15 grocery flank, add a sidr-tier prestige gifting SKU riding hamper curators. Size band: US$9–18M Dabur OTC-cluster retail value by Y3 (C6). Kill-switch (macro T24): if MOHAP/EDE dossier velocity is under 3 SKUs per 2 quarters, escalate to partnership/in-licensing.

The macro's origin doctrine holds: India-origin is neutral-to-positive here — 26 years of Himalaya normalised "Indian herbal science" on UAE pharmacy shelves; Dabur arrives into a lane Himalaya already paved, which is both the gift and the race.
