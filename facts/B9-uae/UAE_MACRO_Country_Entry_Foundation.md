> SO WHAT: So what: the UAE is a federation of diasporas under an Emirati cultural canopy: ~11.3M people of whom Emiratis are ~11.5% and South Asians are ~62% (~7.1M) — meaning Dabur's home-market loyal base is the demographic majority while the prestige codes are set by the Khaleeji minority.
