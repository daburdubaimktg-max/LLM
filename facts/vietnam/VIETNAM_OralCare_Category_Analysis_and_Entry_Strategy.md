## C1 · Executive summary — the play, the size, the ask

> SO WHAT: The play. Vietnam oral care is a US$194m market growing ~7% ruled by one fortress (Unilever's P/S — bought Vietnamese in 1995, still marketed as the protector of "the Vietnamese smile") and one wounded giant (Colgate, which bought the #2 local brand Dạ Lan in 1995, killed it, and has under-earned ever since — an accumulated ~US$11m loss by 2017). The fight Dabur must NOT pick: price, whitening, or "national purpose" — those are owned. The fight it should pick: the herbal-therapeutic gum shelf ( kem đánh răng dược liệu ) that local pharma brands Ngọc Châu and Thái Dương built at VND 45–75k, Korean LG Bamboo Salt premiumised, and Thai Twin Lotus imports validated — a shelf whose exact codes (areca husk for firm gums, salt, clove, "reduce gum bleeding") map one-to-one onto Dabur Red and Meswak, 140 years of apothecary proof behind them . Vietnam's clinical reality feeds it: >80–90% adult caries experience, a third of adults with periodontal pockets, dentist care problem-driven, ~15.8m smokers plus a national coffee-and-tea ritual = a permanent gum-and-stain need-state. Dabur enters as the proof-led Ayurvedic sibling of Đông y at upper-mass VND 55–95k, via Shopee Mall + TikTok Shop + Long Châu pharmacy, RAK-UAE supply under the new UAE–VN CEPA (macro §M3), Tết gift multipack as the seasonal spike. The ask: fund a 2-SKU Wave-1 (clove-salt gum paste + Meswak botanical), ~US$0.8–1.2m Year-1 A&P envelope inside the shared chassis, Y1 net revenue US$0.6–1.0m building to Y3 US$3–6m (INR 26–52 Cr) — gates and kill-switches in C7.

## C3 · Competition — the fortress, the wounded giant, the herbal insurgents

> SO WHAT: So what: Vietnam oral care is the single most instructive M&A cautionary tale in ASEAN FMCG. Both global majors entered by buying the two local champions in 1995; Unilever kept the Vietnamese name and built a national-purpose fortress; Colgate killed its acquisition and has fought uphill for thirty years. The lesson for Dabur is structural: in this category, Vietnamese identity is a moat, foreign identity is a fee — so Dabur must enter where foreignness is an asset (herbal authenticity, like Korean Bamboo Salt and Thai Twin Lotus did), never where it is a fee (mass family paste).

## C4 · Consumer, culture & the clinical reality

> SO WHAT: So what: Vietnamese oral care behaviour is problem-driven, ritual-rich and proof-hungry . The epidemiology says gums and stain, the culture says salt and areca are already medicine, and the coffee-tobacco economy manufactures stain daily. Dabur does not need to teach a single new belief — it needs to upgrade three existing ones (salt cleans, areca firms gums, herbs are safer) with evidence and a better format.
