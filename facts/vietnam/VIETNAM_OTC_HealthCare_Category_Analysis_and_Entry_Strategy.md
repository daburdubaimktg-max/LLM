## C1 · Executive summary — the one screen

> SO WHAT: The play. Vietnam's self-care market is two stacked pools — OTC drugs ~US$1.5–1.8bn and health supplements (TPCN) ~US$0.9–2.4bn depending on definition , both growing 6–11%/yr — sitting on a 2,000-year Đông y culture where >60% of adults have used a supplement and self-medication at the pharmacy counter is the norm (up to ~76% urban). The category's fortress is licensed traditional-medicine cough (Hoa Linh's Bảo Thanh — the #1 herbal cough lozenge by volume and value — plus 65-year-old Bổ Phế Nam Hà and Mega's Eugica): Dabur does not assault it in Wave 1. The open cells the research found are different: (1) the Tết health-gift shelf between VND 270k bird's-nest boxes and VND 1.65m Korean ginseng sets is structurally empty — no herbal-tonic gift owns the VND 300–500k "meaningful health gift for parents" slot, and Chyawanprash is the only credible occupant with no local equivalent; (2) the snack-medicine digestive cell is culturally pre-built — Hajmola is functionally an Indian ô mai (Vietnam's own plum-ginger-licorice snack-that-is-medicine), a one-sentence education job, launchable through the light food lane and TikTok-native by design; (3) throat comfort for voice-workers (teachers, streamers, karaoke, office AC) is claimable by a honey-ginger lozenge without fighting Bảo Thanh's "trị ho" drug claim. The engine: Long Châu (2,517 stores, Mar 2026) + Shopee Mall (85% of TPCN e-com) + TikTok Shop KOC — run compliant-by-design, because the 2025 Kera-candy scandal and the 2026 ad-law have turned sloppy supplement marketing into criminal exposure, which favours a proof-led entrant. The ask: release OTC Tranche-2 per macro §M11 — Decree 46/2026 dossiers + trademark filings by Q3 2026, Hajmola fast-lane pilot from Q1 2027, Chyawanprash gift tin piloted Tết 2027 and scaled Tết 2028. Y1 US$0.6–1.2m → Y3 US$3.5–7m (₹5–10 Cr → ₹30–61 Cr).

## C2 · Market & sub-segment map + the Claim Ladder

> SO WHAT: So what: the money is not in one "OTC market" — it is in eight sub-segments with radically different doors. The two Dabur can win fastest are digestive snack-formats (light food door, zero incumbent in the fun-format cell) and immunity/tonic gifting (registered-supplement door, occasion-concentrated, incumbents are expensive sâm/yến — not herbal jams). The cough/throat pool is the biggest trap: its leaders hold drug licences and may legally say "trị ho" (treats cough) — a claim no food-lane entrant can match. Sell around that wall, never against it.

## C4 · Consumer & culture — need-states, folk physiology, and the Tết gift machine

> SO WHAT: So what: Vietnamese self-care runs on a folk-physiological operating system — nóng trong người (internal heat), bổ (tonification), wind/cold invasion for coughs — that Ayurveda maps onto almost one-to-one (pitta≈heat, rasayana≈bổ). Dabur does not need to teach a new body-model; it needs to speak the local one . Tân Hiệp Phát built a national beverage brand on exactly this ("Thanh lọc cơ thể, không lo bị nóng"). And the buying unit is the family: children buy tonics FOR parents ( hiếu — filial piety), which is why gifting, not self-care, is the entry emotion for Chyawanprash.
