## C1 · Executive summary — the one screen

> SO WHAT: So what. Vietnam skin care is the largest pool of Dabur's four categories (~US$1.27–1.88bn in 2025, scope-dependent, growing ~7–10%) and simultaneously Dabur's hardest fight : facial care is ~90% of category value and it is owned by a three-army occupation — Japanese moisture science (Hada Labo, the fortress), the K-beauty actives wave (Some By Mi, Skin1004, innisfree), and a French derm column advancing through pharmacies (La Roche-Posay). The authentic-botanical story is already held by credible locals: Cocoon (vegan star, 13→184bn VND revenue 2020–22, #1-selling brand across Guardian Vietnam in 2025) and Thorakao (1961, the kem nghệ heritage house still moving ~16,000 turmeric-cream jars a month). The deep-dive changes one thing: the open cell is not the generic "herbal body/barrier" wedge of the first-pass hypothesis — it is "Nghệ 2.0" : the modernization of Vietnam's own turmeric skin canon ( bôi nghệ lên sẹo — dab turmeric on scars), which today exists only in VND 18–77k dated formats (Thorakao, Thái Dương) with zero clinical proof and zero modern texture, while the trust field around it burns — the 2025 Hanayuki scandal (label SPF 50, tested SPF 2.4; criminal arrests) and the endemic kem trộn corticoid-cream meme have made verifiable per-batch proof the single most valuable claim in Vietnamese skin care . Dabur owns turmeric credibly, owns rose water (Gulabari) in a market where nước hoa hồng is an established ritual, and owns aloe-in-balm (Herbolene) in a sun-scarred, ninja-Lead country. The play: a three-SKU heritage-botanical set — turmeric-honey mờ thâm gel-cream (hero), Gulabari rose toner, Herbolene aloe post-sun balm — at premium-mass VND 79–159k, riding the Hair/Oral chassis in Wave 2, with published third-party batch testing as the trust moat. Fem depilatory is a watch-list flanker (the Veet-vs-Cleo price gap is real but small). The ask: no standalone Skin P&L — release Tranche-3 funding only when Hair passes Gate 1; Y1 net sales US$0.4–0.8m, Y3 US$2.5–6m if gates clear. Skin remains the passenger; it is now a passenger carrying luggage worth opening.

## C4 · Consumer & culture — need-states, cohorts, occasions

> SO WHAT: So what: Vietnamese skin behaviour is driven by a triangle no other ASEAN market has in this intensity: (1) a fair-skin ideal (trắng da) softening into "even, glowing, no dark marks"; (2) a sun-hostile daily reality — motorbike commuting under tropical UV, answered by the ninja-Lead full-cover ritual (jacket, mask, gloves) AND booming sunscreen; (3) an acne-and-aftermath cycle (heat, humidity, helmets, pollution → mụn → self-treatment → thâm marks → months of mark-fading). Dabur's entry lives in corner 3 — the aftermath — where the folk prescription is already "bôi nghệ" (apply turmeric) and the shelf translation of that folk prescription has not been modernized in 60 years.
