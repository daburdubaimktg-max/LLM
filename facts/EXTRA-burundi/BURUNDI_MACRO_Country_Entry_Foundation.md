## M3 · The two walls — the FX rate and the EAC tariff

| Wall | Mechanism | Effect on Dabur (non-EAC origin) | Effect on EAC-origin rivals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall 1 — the parallel FX rate | ~80% of imports financed at the parallel rate (+80–160% over official); the rate itself swung 32% in ten days | Hard currency bought at a large, volatile premium; every P&L re-priced at parallel; repatriation the top risk | SAME wall — but their landed cost is lower to begin with (wall 2), so the FX premium bites a smaller base |
| Wall 2 — the EAC CET 35% band | 2022 CET 4th band: 35% on cosmetics/beauty/personal care from OUTSIDE the bloc; EAC-origin goods duty-free under rules of origin | RAK/India/Turkey Vatika, Meswak, Gulabari face 35% + 18% VAT | Movit (Kampala), ex-Godrej Darling (Nairobi), Unilever-Nairobi enter DUTY-FREE — a decisive structural cost advantage |
| Compounding | Wall 1 × Wall 2 | A non-EAC good is taxed 35% AND paid for in premium dollars — the entry's core economic problem stated in one line | EAC-origin goods dodge wall 2 entirely and face only wall 1's premium on a lower base |
