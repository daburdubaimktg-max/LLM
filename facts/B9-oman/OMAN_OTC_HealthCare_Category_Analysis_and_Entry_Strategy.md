## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Oman's OTC pool reads US$288.4M on Statista's broad definition (flagged — ~US$55/capita runs high vs GCC peers; the definitional check is priority one [!] ), with VMS around US$18M and the segment splits paywalled. Three structural facts do the strategic work. First, the regulatory door is already labelled : Oman's Drug Safety Center explicitly registers "Ayurvedic products" as a named category — no other market in this batch names the lane — at an OMR 500 fee, with the CEPA having fully eliminated HS 3004 duties (confirmed, not estimated). The complication is new: June-2025 advertising rules require prior written DSC approval for medicine ads (60 days, silence = rejection, licences valid 3 months) — so a registered Honitus lives on a 90-day creative cadence, while food-lane SKUs (Chyawanprash at LuLu today, OMR 1.135) keep social speed. Second, one company is the channel : Muscat Pharmacy is simultaneously the #1 chain (~66 doors), the only interior network (~33 branches), and "Oman's largest distributor of pharmaceutical and healthcare products" — and it already carries Himalaya, which is both the obstacle and the proof. One agency meeting covers what took three negotiations in Bahrain. Third, the culture has already written the brand : Omani homes steep luban water overnight, treasure sidr honey at OMR 25–30/700ml (a 30× ladder above imported Al Shifa at 1.675), and brew honey-and-herbs for the throat — Honitus's Hot Sip kadha is structurally identical to existing Omani practice , and the deck's one absolute rule is to position alongside the sacred local honey, never against it. Health math sharpens the range: diabetes at 15.7% and vitamin-D deficiency at 87–91% (the best-documented deficiency in the batch) make sugar-free variants mandatory and the immunity register permanently warm. The ask: DSC registration for Honitus (syrup + Hot Sip) with ad assets filed alongside the dossier; the Muscat Pharmacy dual-role conversation (distribution + 66-door detailing in one deal); Chyawanprash energised at its existing LuLu seat with the sugar-free variant leading; Honey held at the import floor without provenance claims. Y3 band: US$1.2–2.4M (C6). Kill-switch: DSC clock ≤4 quarters (macro G4) or the food-lane portfolio continues alone.
