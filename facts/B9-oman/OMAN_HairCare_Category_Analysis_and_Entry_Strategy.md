**THE PLAY:** THE PLAY — "KHAREEF": Dabur already owns Oman's value oil shelf — now own its seasons and its stories: the khareef anti-frizz window no brand has ever claimed, the desalination hair-fall anxiety Vatika Cactus already answers, the empty premium-naturals rung below Indulekha, and the Vatika Luban variant only Oman could inspire


## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Oman hair care is an estimated US$45–55M pool (10–11% of the country's US$472.7M beauty market — the direct Statista page is the named re-run [!] ), and Dabur enters it as the incumbent, not the challenger: Amla trades in four-plus formats at LuLu (100ml at 0.640; an aggressive 300+100ml band at 1.470), Vatika runs oils, shampoos, serums and the Arabic-named Hammam Zaith hot-oil treatment, and the portfolio is already multi-channel (Talabat Mart, Sharaf DG, noon). What the shelf audit exposes is not a distribution job but four unclaimed stories sitting on top of an owned ladder . First, the khareef window : Salalah runs 75–90% humidity from June to September with 1.03M seasonal visitors, and no brand has ever run khareef-coded anti-frizz communications — a seasonal category moment as legible as "monsoon hair care" in India, which happens to be Dabur's home genre. Second, the desalination anxiety : "hair fall since moving to Oman" is a live consumer worry that clinics monetise and no shampoo brand owns — and Vatika Cactus Hair-Fall Control already sits on shelf at 1.115 waiting for the story. Third, the premium-naturals gap : oils ladder from Parachute's 0.23/100ml floor through Amla's mid to Indulekha's ~2.7/100ml premium with nothing between 1.2–2.5 — and H&S Oud, P&G's pan-GCC proof that scent codes carry 2× premiums, has no Oman listing yet [!] . Fourth, the luban option : a Vatika Omani-frankincense variant (Dhofar-sourced, Hammam-Zaith-adjacent) is the most localisable NPD in the batch. The ask: repair the ladder's gaps (missing prices, the OOS 700ml hero), run the two-story platform (khareef seasonal + desal-protection evergreen) through licensed Omani creators, pilot Vatika Oud/Luban at OMR 1.9–2.4 into the empty rung, and let the CEPA lane carry value packs India-direct. Y3 band: US$2.5–4.5M incremental on the base (C6). Kill-switch: story-led velocity +20% on oils in two quarters or revert to distribution maintenance.
