## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Oman oral care is an estimated US$28–38M pool (6–8% of the US$472.7M beauty-and-personal-care market; the direct Statista page is the named re-run [!] ) with the familiar GCC geometry — a full Colgate ladder from 0.700 (Max Cavity 100ml) to 3.300 (Optic White), Sensodyne owning the therapeutic ceiling to 3.420, and a herbal middle in disarray: Himalaya's Complete Care trades at 0.500 on a −39% promo against its own 0.825 list , the price-signal chaos this engagement has now documented in three markets. Dabur's position is unusual and unnoticed: Dabur Red 200g at 0.630 is the cheapest branded paste on the Omani shelf (0.315/100g) — live at LuLu, Talabat Mart and Mumzworld — while Meswak has no Oman listing at all , even as the code it owns is being rented in plain sight: Listerine Total Care Miswak (zero-alcohol, 500ml) sells on noon Oman today, and Colgate Herbal Miswak trades one border away. And Oman adds a twist no other market has: the local sunnah-adjacent oral ritual is not the arak stick but luban — Dhofari frankincense chewed for teeth and gums, with published antimicrobial evidence against S. mutans and P. gingivalis. The ask (three moves): (1) hold and energise the Red floor (family value framing, baqala small formats via the van build); (2) land Meswak through the CEPA lane at OMR 0.400-class pricing — the authenticity answer to Listerine's rented flavour, «أصل السواك» re-cut for Oman; (3) develop the Meswak + Luban paste — a Dhofar-sourced, Oman-first NPD that no multinational can copy credibly and that doubles as a GCC export story. Sugar-heavy diets and dated-but-alarming caries baselines (dmft 4.61 at age six) keep cavity-protection claims commercially live at every price point. Y3 band: US$0.9–1.9M (C6). Kill-switch: Meswak listing + velocity gates per C7; the luban NPD is feasibility-gated (shared RFQ with the Hair deck's W2) and never forced.
