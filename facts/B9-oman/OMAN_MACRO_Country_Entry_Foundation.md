## M3 · Trade, legal & regulatory — the CEPA flip

### T3 · Tariff chessboard — now a two-lane race
| Lane | Duty | Mechanics | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| India direct → Oman (CEPA) | 0% on ~98% of lines [!] [!] confirm HS 33 / 3305 / 3306 / 3004 / 2106 in the immediate basket + RoO thresholds | Indian value-add rules of origin; Indian plant costs; longer sea leg | NEW PRIMARY for freight-heavy, low-value SKUs (hair oils, Chyawanprash, large shampoo) — est. 10–20% landed-cost gain |
| RAK-UAE → Oman (GCC customs union) | 0% (GCC origin, CoO) | ≥40% GCC value-add; 1–3 day road/feeder; consolidation with GCC sister markets | KEEPS speed, consolidation and NPD-response roles — no longer the default |
| India → Oman pre-CEPA (context) | 5% GCC CET | the old cost of India-direct | obsolete on EIF |

### T4 · Legal & registration spine
| Item | Rule | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Agency law (MOCIIP register) | 1995 amendments removed mandatory exclusivity ; agents may be up to 70% foreign-owned; but a registered agency still acquires statutory protections — territorial rights, parallel-import blocking, termination compensation | structure as a distribution agreement kept OFF the agency register ; never let an exclusive agency accumulate by conduct — the classic Gulf trap, avoidable by drafting |
| Cosmetics regime | conformity notification (DoC + certified lab report + free-sale cert + MSDS + Arabic/English GSO ISO 22715 label) via DGSM/MOCIIP; import permits issued within ~2 hours, 7 days a week | the fastest cosmetic door in the GCC — hair, oral-cosmetic and skin SKUs enter at notification speed |
| Pharma / health supplements | MOH Directorate General of Pharmaceutical Affairs / Drug Safety Center; GCC central-registration participant but national registration still required; food-supplement lane faster | Honitus-class claims need MOH; Chyawanprash/Honey stay food-lane for mass retail [!] [!] herbal timeline (typ. 6–12 months) |
| Halal | assumed default; no mandatory cosmetic cert | halal/vegetarian logos are quiet positives; alcohol-free (the Vatika platform) lands well — displayed, not shouted (M4 tone rule) |

> SO WHAT: So what. The CEPA does three things at once: it neutralises RAK's tariff edge in exactly one GCC market (re-run the lane economics per SKU — big-cube value SKUs go India-direct, fast-turn NPD stays RAK); it hands Dabur an India-cost base against competitors still importing European or GCC-made product; and it eases Indian professional mobility for merchandising and regulatory staff. Combined with the 2-hour cosmetic import permit, Oman is operationally the easiest entry in the batch — the constraints are all demand-side (wallet, tone, geography), not regulatory.
