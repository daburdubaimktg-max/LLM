**THE PLAY:** THE PLAY — "HUNGRY WATER": Amla and Vatika are already distributed everywhere that matters — this is extraction, not entry. Weaponise Bahrain's own desalination hair-damage press narrative to reframe oiling as protection, close the price-pack ladder, and test the oud-coded premium H&S just proved at 2×


## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Bahrain hair care is a US$43.27M pool growing 3.97% (Statista 2025 — the one category in this market with a direct public size [!] ), and Dabur is already its best-distributed Indian brand: Amla in four sizes at LuLu plus Sharaf DG, Bahrain Pharmacy, the Alaujan webstore and Ninja q-comm; Vatika oils from BHD 0.590 and Vatika shampoos running −30% promos. Distribution is not the problem — silence and price-pack drift are. Three market facts convert that installed base into growth. First, Bahrain's own press wrote Dabur's ad : the Daily Tribune's "Is Bahrain's Desalinated Water Causing Your Hair to Fall Out?" carries the "hungry water" narrative (chlorine + calcium stripping cuticles) that makes the pre-wash oil ritual a protection story — the single best comms hook in the category, unclaimed. Second, P&G proved the premium ceiling : Head & Shoulders' Oud Collection sells at BHD 4.390 — exactly 2× its Classic — proving Khaleeji scent codes carry a 100% premium; Dabur's hair-oil heritage plus an oud/bakhoor-coded Vatika variant attacks the same wallet from below. Third, the hijab shelf is empty : Sunsilk Hijab Recharge exists in SE Asia but shows no GCC listing — no mainstream hijab-specific line is confirmed on Bahraini shelves [!] . The ask (extraction, three moves): (1) "Hungry Water" campaign re-framing Amla/Vatika oiling as pre-wash protection, with the price-pack ladder repaired (the 300ml price hole closed, promo depth disciplined); (2) Vatika Oud/Dukhoon variant test at BHD 1.9–2.4 — under H&S Oud, above the value tier; (3) hijab-fresh scalp positioning piloted on an existing Vatika SKU before Unilever imports its SEA range. Y3 band: US$1.5–3M incremental on the existing base (C6). Kill-switch: if the protection campaign doesn't lift oil-segment velocity ≥20% in two quarters, revert to distribution maintenance and let the OTC/Oral waves carry the chassis.
