**THE PLAY:** THE PLAY — "AL-JISR": bridge the UAE MOHAP dossier into NHRA's Health Product lane, land Honitus at BHD 0.95–1.10 — 45% under Koflet, 65% under Prospan — and win the pharmacist's mouth at Nasser, in the densest pharmacy market in the region, where no Honitus has ever been listed


## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Bahrain OTC is an estimated US$115–135M pool (derived by summing Statista's indexable segments — analgesics US$26.8M, cold & cough US$21.5M, other OTC US$41.8M, VMS estimated [!] ) and its most relevant slice is also its fastest: cold & cough grows +6.26%/yr to US$29M by 2029 . The structure mirrors the UAE with three Bahrain twists. Same: Himalaya is the Ayurveda incumbent — Koflet (BHD 1.995), Septilin and Liv.52 hold a dedicated range at Nasser Pharmacy, the #1 chain, pre-validating "Indian herbal in a GCC pharmacy" and occupying it first; Prospan anchors herbal efficacy at an estimated BHD 2.7–3.0; the pharmacist is the demand engine (Bahrain's OTC dispensing is notably liberal and its pharmacy density — one per ~3,900 residents — is the region's highest). Different, and all in Dabur's favour: Honitus and Hajmola appear on no Bahraini retailer at all (pure whitespace, and an awareness debt); the UAE MOHAP registrations become a dossier bridge into NHRA's Health Product lane (no automatic recognition, but NHRA runs reliance-style review and the documentation pack — CTD-lite, halal certs, heavy-metal COAs — transfers nearly whole [!] ); and the RAK 0%-duty stack lands Honitus at BHD 0.95–1.10 — undercutting Koflet ~45% and Prospan ~65% while still funding the pharmacist incentives that decide this market. Health context sharpens the portfolio: diabetes at 11.3% of adults (fastest growth globally, +154% since 2005) makes sugar-free Chyawanprash near-mandatory , and 49.4% adult vitamin-D deficiency keeps the immunity register permanently warm. The ask: one registration wave (Honitus + Chyawanprash through the Health Product lane, NHRA ad pre-approval built into every asset), a ~100-door pharmacist programme led at Nasser/Boots/YMH, Chyawanprash and Honey into LuLu on the existing hair-care trucks, and the Ramadan/winter clock inherited from the macro calendar. Y3 band: US$0.9–2M (C6). Kill-switch: if NHRA registration exceeds 3 quarters or the ad committee blocks traditional-use claims, run the food-lane portfolio (honey + claim-silent Chyawanprash at LuLu) and cap investment — the Malaysia G1 discipline, re-derived.
