**THE PLAY:** THE PLAY — "ASL AL-SIWAK": Dabur Meswak is already on LuLu's shelf, unmarketed — claim the miswak code Listerine is now renting, at Colgate's mass price with the heritage story Himalaya charges 2× for, and cross into the pharmacy door Himalaya left open


## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Bahrain's oral-care pool is small — an estimated US$40–50M total, BHD 8–11M in toothpaste (pro-rata of IMARC's US$1.6bn GCC figure; the Statista Bahrain page exists but is paywalled [!] ) — but it is the cheapest fight in the batch, because Dabur is already on the shelf and doesn't know it as a business : Dabur Original Miswak Herbal 120g and Dabur Herbal Basil 150g are live LuLu Bahrain listings today, with Meswak Gold at Dukakeen — unpriced-in-plan, unmarketed, un-detailed. Around that dormant beachhead, three facts define the opportunity. First, the miswak code has been validated by the enemy : Listerine sells a Miswak variant in Bahrain (Sharaf DG, Talabat bundles) — a multinational renting the heritage Dabur owns outright. Second, the price geometry is a gift : the herbal benchmark (Himalaya Complete Care) sits at BHD 1.19/100ml while Colgate's mass anchor sits at 0.74/100ml — and the RAK cost base lets Meswak land at BHD 0.590–0.690, Colgate's price with Himalaya's story , still leaving an estimated 55–62% pool for trade and A&P. Third, the demand base is clinical, not just cultural : pooled EMR data (including Bahrain) shows 61% caries prevalence at age 12 and 70% at 15 — cavity protection is a live mother-concern, and sunnah-siwak is a live faith practice whose Saudi Ramadan sales spike ~500%. The ask: one wave on the macro's shared chassis — relaunch the existing LuLu listings with shelf-edge price-per-100ml framing and the «أصل السواك في معجون» ("the origin of siwak, in a paste") claim; extend Herb'l Charcoal at BHD 0.9–1.2 under Colgate's 3.59 charcoal ceiling; enter ~100 priority pharmacy doors where Himalaya crossed and Dabur hasn't; and own the Ramadan miswak moment the category ignores between Ramadans. Y3 size band: US$0.8–1.8M (C6). Kill-switch: if LuLu velocity doesn't triple within 2 quarters of relaunch, Bahrain oral reverts to passive distribution and the effort moves to the OTC deck's pharmacy build.
