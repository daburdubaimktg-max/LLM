## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Bahrain skin care is, surprisingly, the biggest of the four pools — US$75.13M at 4.13% CAGR, per-capita US$49.70 (Statista 2025 [!] ) — 1.7× the hair market. But the per-capita number is the warning, not the invitation: this is a premium-skewed market whose growth lives in the pharmacy dermo tier (CeraVe at BHD 7.53, La Roche-Posay at 12.75 — three to five times mass pricing), and the macro's verdict stands: Dabur plays the value floor, Wave-3, no prestige plays . Within that floor, the audit found two embarrassments and one gift. Embarrassment one: Dermoviva True Radiance Turmeric face wash is already listed at LuLu — at BHD 2.690, Himalaya-parity pricing that throws away the RAK 0%-duty cost edge and buys no velocity (Himalaya's own 150ml runs 1.31 on promo channels). Embarrassment two: Gulabari is listed at Sharaf DG and out of stock — a supply failure, not a demand one. The gift: the fairness/whitening category is alive and unembarrassed in Bahrain — LuLu's own category label reads "Fairness/Whitening cream", Glow & Lovely runs five-plus variants at 2.69–2.89, and even the legacy Fair & Lovely name still trades at 2.400 — yet Dabur should still take the radiance (نضارة) lane, because the portfolio's fairness-language exposure is global (the UAE deck's R1 doctrine), and being the clean-language value player in a legacy-language category is a positioning, not a sacrifice. The ask (three fixes, no NPD): (1) reprice Dermoviva to BHD 0.990–1.290 — undercutting Himalaya's regular price ≥55% while still clearing an estimated 10% brand contribution on the RAK stack; (2) attach the hard-water moisture claim (the Hungry Water platform's skin extension — same chemistry, same press logic); (3) restock Gulabari and reposition it as a toner ritual at BHD 0.990–1.190, escaping the food-grade rose-water shadow (Rabee sells a full litre for 1.690). Y3 band: US$0.7–1.4M (C6). Kill-switch: this deck runs entirely on the macro chassis's marginal cost — if repricing doesn't triple Dermoviva velocity in two quarters, the SKUs revert to passive listings and no further skin investment is made in Bahrain.
