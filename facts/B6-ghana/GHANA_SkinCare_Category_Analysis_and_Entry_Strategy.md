## C1 · Category frame — a big market with a wound in the middle

> SO WHAT: So what. The entry's one unbreakable law, set before any SKU is named: the clean register — no fair, no bright, no light, no white, no glow-as-euphemism, no before/after shade imagery, ever, in any language. Not as compliance (the ban is contested and half-enforced) but as position: in a market where the biggest names carry contested words on live packs and the FDA publishes seizure lists, the house whose every label survives the anti-bleaching discourse untouched owns the only ground that cannot be taken — and Forever Clair (C3) has already proven at US$10M scale that this ground SELLS. The programme's radiance fence, tested in ten environments, meets its hardest terrain and does not bend: here even "radiance" is retired — the register is moisture, repair, health .
