## M1 · Engagement frame — what kind of market is this?

> SO WHAT: So what. The macro mode is FOUND-AND-BUILD : nothing to take over (no torn bands, no monopolist resellers — the Batch 5 takeover species do not apply), everything to establish — registration, distribution, price identity — with one warm institutional door (diaspora retail) and one unresolved structural fork (the duty question, M3) that between them decide the entry's economics before any marketing decision is taken.

> **REPUTATION BET:** Foundation verdict. ENTER — lane A (import + national distributor), India-direct economics as plan of record with the Nigeria-fork option checked internally at W0; registration filed inside the strong-cedi window; Melcom met with one four-category sheet; every price authored here re-based on the G1 walk.
