**THE PLAY:** THE PLAY — "DAYA TAHAN": register Honitus into the RM10–20 gap between Woods' and Prospan with a visible KKLIU line; unlock Chyawanprash with halal + sugar-free for the sunnah-wellness shelf; run Hajmola as buka-puasa candy through 99 Speedmart's 2,500 doors


## C1 · Executive summary

> SO WHAT: The play. Malaysia's OTC pool is US$889M growing 4.6% (Statista 2024), wrapped in a supplements pool of US$645–920M and a national herbal industry the government itself targets at RM19–28bn of GDP by 2027 (MIDA) — this is a state that wants traditional medicine to win, and regulates it seriously: NPRA registration with a printed MAL number, Medicine Advertisements Board approval with a printed KKLIU number on every ad, and JAKIM-recognised halal as the commercial passport to the Malay 60%. Dabur's position today is invisible: Honitus at RM13.50 and Chyawanprash at RM33.50/950g sell through Indian groceries (MADRAS, Brickfields) with no discoverable NPRA registration [!] QUEST3+ — diaspora cash, zero Malay-majority reach. The market has already run the experiment that matters: Himalaya Koflet lozenges sit in Watsons at RM14.50 , proving Indian herbal cough sells in Malaysian modern trade, and the "sunnah wellness" shelf (madu kelulut, habbatus sauda, kurma) proves Malay shoppers pay premiums for heritage-Islamic health formats. The ask (three moves): (1) register Honitus as a Traditional Medicine (MAL…T) and price at RM15.90–17.90 — squarely in the empty gap between Woods' Peppermint (RM10.30, the 100-year "Cough Specialist") and Prospan (RM20.50) — with KKLIU-approved "herbal + madu, tak mengantuk" advertising; (2) rebuild Chyawanprash as the halal, sugar-free "daya tahan" tonic (15.6% adult diabetes makes sugar-free near-mandatory) seeded through sunnah-adjacent agent networks and TikTok Shop, not pharmacy-first; (3) run Hajmola in the food lane through 99 Speedmart (2,542 doors) as buka-puasa candy — fastest cash, lowest regulatory load. Two key accounts (BIG CARING 500+ doors + Watsons 700+) put ~1,200 pharmacy doors within two negotiations. Size band: US$4–8.5M cluster retail by Y3 (C6). Kill-switch: if NPRA registration exceeds 3 quarters or JAKIM certifier recognition fails, run the food-lane-only portfolio (Hajmola + honey + unregistered Chyawanprash) and cap investment.

> SO WHAT: So what. The KKLIU number is not bureaucracy — it is shelf language : Blackmores prints "KKLIU 1369/2024, valid until 31.12.2026" on its own product page because the number itself signals legitimacy to Malaysian shoppers scarred by krim tiruan and fake-supplement scandals. Dabur's fastest credibility move in Malaysia is the same one it made in the UAE with MOHAP: get registered, then advertise the registration . And the halal flip (the macro's biggest China-inversion) is decisive here: without a JAKIM-recognised certificate, Chyawanprash simply does not exist for the Malay 60% — with it, the product reads as sunnah-adjacent heritage wellness. Dabur India already holds Jamiat Ulama halal certification; whether that certifier is on JAKIM's recognised list is the single most important open fact in this deck [!] JAKIM senarai badan pensijilan halal luar negara .
