**Subject:** Parallel country-entry research sprint - kit attached, please start today (deadline 7 Jul)

Hi all,

We're running deep CPG market-entry research across ~40 countries in parallel. Everything you need is in the attached **COUNTRY_ANALYSIS_HANDOVER_KIT.zip** - the methodology, the skills, the project instructions, and a filled-in prompt for each batch.

**Your set-up (about 5 minutes):**
1. Unzip and read `0_START_HERE_HANDOVER.md` first - it explains the whole flow.
2. In your Claude account, create a new **Project**. Paste `1_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the Project's custom-instructions field.
3. Upload as Project knowledge: the files under `skills/country-analysis/` (SKILL.md + its references), all eight `skills/core/` skills, and the regional expert(s) for your batch (the tracker table in `3_RUN_PROMPTS_BY_BATCH.md` tells you exactly which).
4. Open `3_RUN_PROMPTS_BY_BATCH.md`, go to your batch, and use that ready-made prompt. Run **one country per chat**, swapping the country name as you move down your batch.

**What each country produces:** five HTML reports - one Country-Macro foundation plus four category decks (Hair, Oral, OTC Health, Skin). Do the macro first, then one category per pass.

**Two non-negotiables:**
- **Public data only.** Never paste real Dabur P&L, margins, distributor lists, A&P or NPD pipeline into this environment - those stay in our secure enterprise tools. Work in public ranges.
- **Depth over breadth.** The whole point is deep, board-grade reports - ~6,000-9,000 words and 20+ data tables each, with named brands and sourced numbers. If the model tries to squeeze all four categories into one answer, stop it: "one category per pass, full depth." One deep report beats four thin ones.

Batch ownership is in the tracker - please confirm your batch back to me. Anything unclear, the handover note answers most of it; ping me otherwise.

Thanks,
Nishant
