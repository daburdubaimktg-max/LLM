THE THIRTY-EIGHTH CORRIDOR FINDING — GATE-BIASED. The CAR oral file contains zero country-specific data — no brand shares, no prices, no penetration figures, no oral-health epidemiology; the research note declares the section «100% proxy» and this deck honours the declaration in every cell. What the file DOES contain is a structural peculiarity no prior oral market presented: the only economically visible decision-maker in the category is the PK5 wholesale stockist — and he belongs to the one community in the country for whom the miswak tree carries personal, devotional meaning (Muslim traders of Chadian/West African origin run PK5 — CAPTURED; siwak practice is standard across Sahelian Muslim communities — UNCONFIRMED for CAR specifically, flagged). The temptation is obvious and the finding names it as a TRAP: a Meswak sell-in to PK5 would likely succeed for reasons that have nothing to do with sell-through — the gatekeeper's affinity would buy the first carton, the Christian-majority street would never ask for the second, and the enthusiastic order would masquerade as market demand in exactly the telemetry the seed protocol trusts most. The finding's instruction extends the sensor discipline deposited at #188: where the channel's own taste aligns with the product, first-order telemetry (wholesale orders) is BIASED and must be discounted; only second-order telemetry (stall-level sell-through, repeat coin purchases) reads true. With that correction installed, the deck runs the full C1–C18 spine against the macro's pass-shaped pre-read — and confirms it: PASS at entry, with one strictly-governed exception — a single Meswak mini-tube corner permitted on the hair seed's carton IF the seed ships at all, read on stall sell-through only, both endings pre-written.

DEEP THINK · The objections, answered

"If the verdict is a pass, why does the deck exist at full depth?" Because a pass that cannot show its work is indistinguishable from neglect — and because this particular pass produced the queue's most transferable oral instrument.
