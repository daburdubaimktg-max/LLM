Central African Republic — Skin Care: Category Analysis & Entry Strategy

Fencing in the dark: the category's biggest demand pocket is untouchable and unmeasured at once — so the deck sizes by jobs it can see, holds its one SKU as a priced option, respects the domestic soap floor, and closes the set at zero fixed cost
Prepared for the Dabur International board · 7 July 2026 · Public-web evidence only · Routing: africa-consumer-insight-expert · Research base: CF_FULL_NOTE.md §8; macro #187 + #188–190 standing orders · Whitening fence: 23rd market — drawn through unmapped territory · Gulabari: the null geometry's second member · HUSACA's soap floor: respected, never entered

THE FORTIETH CORRIDOR FINDING — FENCED-BLIND. Every fence market before this one let Dabur measure what it was refusing: Kinshasa's lightening economy had documented manufacturers and export studies; Mexico's had mercury alerts with ppm figures; Brazil's clareador segment had market data. The CAR presents the fence's hardest configuration: the lightening pocket is real — and completely unmeasured. Regional science is unambiguous (women's usage 25–77% across country studies; a Pan-African Medical Journal case-control literature ties voluntary depigmentation to leg erysipelas in Central Africa at OR≈4.29 — CAPTURED, regional grade), the supply base is identified (Cameroon's «unifying» ranges and Sivop-class imports feed Central African markets — CAPTURED for the supply side), but no CAR or Bangui prevalence study exists in either language — the note's skin gap #1, confirmed by search. The deck therefore cannot do what every predecessor did — state the category's size, subtract the fenced pocket, and plan the remainder — because subtraction needs a number and none exists. The finding names the discipline this forces: when the fence must be drawn through unmapped territory, draw it by JOBS, not by numbers — claim only the demand the plan can see and name (body moisture in a palm-oil culture, the stretch-mark frame, the baby cohort, the soap-adjacent lotion step), refuse every job that borders the fenced pocket's vocabulary, and let the PK5 audit map the territory the desk cannot. One consequence follows immediately and the deck owns it: skin ships NOTHING in Y1. The macro's seed carries two SKUs (Amla, the balm) and skin's candidate — a petroleum-jelly-class moisturiser at 300–500 XAF — enters the file as the programme's held-and-priced instrument instead: fully specified, fully priced, both endings written, activated only at the re-review if the seed graduates. The set closes at zero fixed cost with its discipline intact.

The sizing discipline's reasoning, stated once in full. The temptation in every fenced market is to size the total category, estimate the fenced share, and present the remainder as addressable — arithmetic that looks rigorous and is actually two guesses multiplied. Here even the first guess has no public basis: no total-category figure exists to start from. The jobs method replaces the arithmetic entirely: identify demand behaviours that are DIRECTLY evidenced (palm-oil body care, soap purchase, the birth rate), attach a candidate product to each, and let the audit price each candidate against its incumbent. The output is not a market size — it is a short list of priced, testable product-cells, which is what a seed-scale entry actually needs. Market-size numbers serve boards ranking countries; the macro's order-of-magnitude flag already does that job, and this deck declines to launder its precision upward.

One paragraph on what the missing map means beyond marketing. The absence of any CAR prevalence study is itself a public-health datum: a practice documented as widespread across every neighbouring research literature is invisible here because nobody has funded the looking. When the first study eventually appears — a Bangui dermatology thesis, an NGO survey — it will define the national conversation the way the 2016 Wiley study defined Kinshasa's, and every ruling in this deck's fence table is pre-committed to re-open on that day (the G3 standing watch). The deck's posture is thus not «the problem doesn't exist here» — the regional science makes that reading untenable — but «the problem is unmapped, and an entrant who markets into unmapped territory cannot know what it is touching». The fence at its strictest setting is epistemics, not squeamishness.

DEEP THINK · The objections, answered

"A deck whose verdict is 'ship nothing' — why does it run to full depth?"
