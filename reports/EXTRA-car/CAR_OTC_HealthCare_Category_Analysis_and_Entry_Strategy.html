Central African Republic — OTC Health Care: Category Analysis & Entry Strategy

Where paper trust is dead, the product must prove itself on the skin: in a market flooded with falsified medicines, the balm's heat is its own certificate — and the world's deepest honey culture needs nothing in a jar from anyone
Prepared for the Dabur International board · 7 July 2026 · Public-web evidence only · Routing: africa-consumer-insight-expert · Research base: CF_FULL_NOTE.md (§7 — the set's strongest CAPTURED local signal); macro #187 + #188–189 standing orders · Honey verdict #33 (ABSTAIN-JAR) honoured throughout · Foods-not-medicines lane; the AMM registration question queued, never assumed

THE THIRTY-NINTH CORRIDOR FINDING — SELF-PROVING. The CAR OTC environment destroys the entry asset every prior market let Dabur lean on: paper trust. The formal pharmacy layer is a thin crust (Pharmacie du Centre, Notre-Dame, Bimbo; Centrapharm wholesale — CAPTURED) over a flood: «pharmacies sauvages» line Avenue de France a dozen deep with daily queues, and falsified/substandard medicines proliferate through them (Africanews/Allo Docteurs 2023 — CAPTURED). In that environment, a certificate is a rumour, a claim is a stranger's promise, and a sealed box proves only that someone sealed a box. What survives is the product that needs no believing: the sensory format that demonstrates itself at first use. A camphor-menthol balm heats the chest within seconds of application — no literacy, no dosage, no leap of faith, no counterfeit-proof paperwork required, because the proof is in the user's own skin and the fake that cannot replicate the sensation exposes itself at first rub. The finding names the selection rule this market imposes: where trust infrastructure has collapsed, enter ONLY with formats whose efficacy is self-evident at first use — the format IS the certificate. The deck builds the balm case on that rule (one Tiger-Balm-class SKU, sealed and batch-coded, through pharmacy dépôts and PK5, inside the foods-not-medicines lane), honours honey verdict #33's abstention in the world's most honey-saturated market, retires Chyawanprash with its memo, and treats the tradipraticien economy — institutionalised here as nowhere else in this queue (FNTCA federation; CERPHAMETA at the University of Bangui — CAPTURED) — with the courtesy the programme owes the systems that were here first.
