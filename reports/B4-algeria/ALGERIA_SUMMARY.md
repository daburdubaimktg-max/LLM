## Cross-cutting Algeria findings (portable to Tunisia/Libya)

1. **Read the direction, not the text.** The regulatory decade table (every closing gate faces importers, every opening door faces
   producers) outlived every individual rule change. In institutionally churning markets, plan against the policy's vector — Libya will
   demand the same discipline with less text to read.
2. **The two-price economy is the real market map.** Official-vs-parallel FX (~80% gap) decides who wins before any brand acts: the local
   manufacturer is the only untaxed actor. Check the FX architecture FIRST in every remaining B4/B5 market — it may pre-decide the lane as
   completely as it did here.
3. **Structural asymmetries beat cultural insights when both are available.** The set's strongest cards were mechanical: the Signal/Veet
   asymmetries (a variant cannot out-story a brand; a DAPS-exiled incumbent cannot defend its own mass market), the jelly manufacturing
   gap, the crowded-word meaning-migration. Culture aimed the cards; structure made them trumps.
4. **Transplant mechanisms, never rituals — now demonstrated both ways.** Morocco's swak-beldi failed the Algerian evidence test (no
   dual-heritage oral concept was manufactured); Morocco's akker fassi PASSED it (Tlemcen's Chedda) and was extended on evidence. The
   method is the finding: every neighbour pattern gets tested, and both outcomes are strategy.
5. **In claims-restricted markets, format is the advertising.** The ARAV wall made Dabur's sensory formats (chatpata tablets, honey-thick
   syrups) and its paperwork (dose, seal, halal, decree compliance) the only legal communication — and they suffice. "Restraint is the
   licence" generalises to every enforcement-state market ahead.
6. **The blank page is an asset when the price is right.** Zero footprint (hair, OTC, skin) meant no grey anchors, no fake-was history,
   no discount-bin perceptions — Lebanon's lesson inverted. The corollary: the one accident found (oral's 280) must be absorbed by FORMAT,
   never by a visible hike.
7. **The made-here claim converts enforcement into moat.** The crackdown state polices counterfeits and suitcase trade FOR the compliant
   local producer; Nivea's resellers' authenticity-anxiety copy is the import tier confessing the problem a tolled line doesn't have.
   "مصنوعة هنا — بلا ما تسقسي واش أصلية" is the set's most portable line.
