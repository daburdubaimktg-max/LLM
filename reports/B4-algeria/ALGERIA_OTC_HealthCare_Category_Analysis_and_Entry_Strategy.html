C1 — Category verdict & thesis

Verdict: BUILD-NARROW — "enter as food, not as medicine." Algeria's OTC terrain splits into three lanes with wildly different physics. Lane 1 — médicament: ANPP registration + state PPA pricing; Saidal's Toplexil at 116 DZD/150ml sets a floor even more brutal than Morocco's 14.70-MAD jail — no Dabur SKU ever registers here. Lane 2 — complément alimentaire: a $500M/yr, import-driven, publicly-acknowledged grey zone being tightened in real time (Apr-2026 draft decree: authorized-ingredient lists, dose ceilings, stronger controls [!] Journal Officiel status) with a hard advertising wall (ARAV TV interventions; the 8-Apr-2022 inter-ministerial instruction banning unjustified health claims; a ministerial move to ban supplement TV ads outright) — and de facto pharmacy/parapharmacy-gated distribution. Lane 3 — foodstuff: honey and food-framed products sell FMCG-open through épiceries, herboristes (~2,700 achab shops + 688 ambulant traders; Sétif alone 445) and Ouedkniss with zero pharma gatekeeping. The strategy reads directly off the lanes: Hajmola enters through lane 3 (a digestive-tasting food/candy at coin price — the claims live in the eating, not the labelling), Honitus enters through lane 2's proven umbrella (honey cough syrups already sell at 700 DZD (Bronchonet) against Toplexil's 116 — a 6× umbrella pre-built by local brands), and the jar of Dabur Honey stays home (sidr and daghmous terroir at 5,000–5,600 DZD/kg — the never-out-honey law, fourth market running). The vehicle: a wellness-specialised toller (BPN Biophytonutrial or Biomax class — multi-certified, halal, digestive lines running) — a SECOND partner track, because supplement plants are not shampoo plants, sharing the set's importer and wholesale spine.

Two proofs anchor the thesis. First, the Indian route is proven: Bhargava Phytolab — a century-old Indian phyto/homeopathy house — already sells Gas Off and IBS Off on Algerian pharmacy e-commerce; an Indian natural-health brand CAN register and shelve here. Second, the honey-cough association is pre-built: Bronchonet (250mg honey), Toux Kid and Puressentiel already occupy the honey-syrup shelf, and Biopharm's own Rynza runs a tulsi-licorice-turmeric-ginger composition — Algerian pharma R&D already speaks Ayurveda's ingredient language without naming it. Dabur arrives not as an exotic but as the branded original of a formula logic the market has partially domesticated (strategist read).

C5 — Positioning space & the open territory

Three axes organise the space: legal lane (medicine / supplement / food), price altitude (116-DZD state floor → 700 honey umbrella → 850–1,400 immunity band → 3,900 gummy premium), and trust register (state-generic / French-science / local-halal / terroir-natural). The occupied cells: Saidal owns medicine-cheap; French imports own para-science-premium; local labs own halal-functional mid; the achab owns loose-natural. Two cells stand open. Open cell one: food-lane branded digestive — nothing branded owns the post-meal occasion at épicerie coin prices; the entire digestive shelf lives behind pharmacy glass at 350–3,900. Open cell two: heritage-natural cough at the umbrella's middle — Bronchonet priced the honey syrup at 700 but carries no story; Honitus at 550–650 undercuts it WITH the story (the grandmother's zaâter-and-honey, dosed). Both cells reward exactly what Dabur is: format-led, heritage- storied, claims-light (strategist read).

Why the food lane is the strategic centre, not the fallback. It is tempting to read lane 3 as the consolation prize (no claims, no pharmacy trust, coin margins) — the deck argues the reverse. In a 48M-person market where supplements are pharmacy-gated and modern trade is ~3%, the épicerie/achab food lane is the ONLY route to national physical ubiquity — the same ubiquity that made Hajmola a subcontinental institution without ever being a medicine. The lane's constraint (no claims) is survivable precisely because Hajmola's category logic never depended on claims: the product IS the occasion, bought by taste memory and habit loops, advertised by the jar on the counter. Honitus needs the para channel's trust theatre; Hajmola needs the opposite — to be too ordinary to regulate, as familiar as the mint by the tea glasses. Two heroes, two lanes, two trust systems — and the wholesale spine carries both (strategist read, opinion).
