M1 · Demography — old, urban, two-speed

Russia is demographically the opposite of Batch 8's Africa: population ~143.4M (2026 est.) and shrinking, median age 40.7, urbanisation 75.4% (~108M urban dwellers) Worldometer/UN-derived, 2026 [!] Rosstat's own mid-2026 estimate runs closer to 146M including disputed territories — cite Rosstat direct before any board use. This is a replacement-and-persuasion market, not a cohort-growth market: no youth bulge will carry a brand that fails to convert existing habits. The consumption map is brutally concentrated — Moscow city ~13.1M and >22M in the wider agglomeration, St Petersburg ~5.6M, then a tier of ten-plus "million-cities" (Novosibirsk, Yekaterinburg, Kazan, Nizhny Novgorod, Chelyabinsk, Samara, Ufa, Rostov-on-Don, Krasnoyarsk, Perm) that anchors regional distribution citypopulation/Worldometer 2026.

M2 · Economy — a war economy normalising downward, with a paid-up consumer

The macro picture is stranger than its headlines: three years of labour-shortage and defence-sector wage inflation delivered real disposable income +8.2% (2024) and +7.4% (2025) — a genuinely strong run — now forecast to decelerate hard to +2.1% in 2026 Rosstat; Min-Economy Sept-2025 forecast via Interfax/TASS, Jan 2026. Consumer sentiment improved through 2025 (HSE index, Q2-2025: 26.6% would spend rather than save a windfall, +2.1pp) HSE CSI Q2-2025. Inflation has been squeezed from 9.52% (2024) to ~5.3% (May 2026) by a punishing rate path — the CBR key rate began 2025 at 21% and stood at 16% by December 2025 cbr.ru. The entrant-relevant reading of that rate: any Russian distributor's working capital costs mid-teens per year, so trade credit is expensive, stock turns are king, and a marketplace consignment model (FBO) quietly shifts inventory financing onto the entrant — a term to negotiate knowingly, not discover later.

M3 · THE ENTRY LANE — the file's priority section: a risk map, not advocacy

Legal starting point, stated once and precisely. India is not a sanctioning state. Rupee-denominated trade with Russia is legal under Indian law and RBI policy (July-2022 rupee-invoicing framework; August-2025 RBI circular allowing category-1 Indian banks to open Special Rupee Vostro Accounts for foreign correspondent banks without prior approval) RBI circulars 2022/2025. Nothing in this section questions legality. What it maps is practical friction and second-order exposure — the things that decide whether a legal lane is a usable lane: payment settlement, corridor logistics, insurance, marking compliance, counterparty risk, and the reputational calculus of a publicly listed Indian company operating visibly in a sanctions-reshaped market. The board should read this section as a risk register with mitigations and kill-switches, and decide with open eyes.

Parallel imports — the misunderstood clause, read correctly for an Indian entrant. Russia legalised parallel import (import without trademark-holder consent) for goods from "unfriendly" countries (Minpromtorg Order 2701, July 2023, amended 2024). Two facts matter. First, India is not an "unfriendly" country — the mechanism neither helps nor constrains Dabur, whose correct lane is ordinary authorized import. Second, cosmetics and perfumery were removed from the parallel-import list in 2025 as substitution matured, and total parallel-import volumes fell ~45% in 2025 ($20.9bn Jan-Nov vs $37.9bn prior year) Minpromtorg/TASS/legal commentaries 2025 — meaning the informal Western competition on shelf is thinning, not thickening. The twist this file must own: the Dabur products already on Wildberries and Ozon most likely arrived through third-party resellers exporting from India/UAE/CIS — informal but not "parallel import" in the legal sense. The entry job is to audit, then convert or de-conflict those sellers (M9), not to pretend the shelf is empty.

M4 · Regulation — two tracks, both hard gates, both EAEU-wide

The regulatory frame is mature and legible — this is not a paper-thin market like the batch's African notification lanes, nor an opaque one. Cosmetics run under TR TS 009/2011 ("On safety of perfumery and cosmetic products"): most SKUs need a declaration of conformity; a defined list — including peroxide-based whitening agents and fluoride concentrations above 0.15% — requires full state registration instead EAEU technical regulation; Schmidt & Schmidt compliance literature. Supplements — Chyawanprash, honey-as-wellness, single-herb lines — register as БАД (BAD, biologicheski aktivnye dobavki — biologically active food supplements) via Rospotrebnadzor's СГР (SGR — State Registration Certificate): indefinite validity, RUB 55,000–95,000 per SKU, 20–45 working days including lab testing, checkable in the public EAEU registry [!] agency-sourced figures — confirm on a live filing. BAD claims are restricted to structure/function wellness language — supports immunity, general tonic — and may not claim to treat, cure or prevent disease; Chyawanprash's ~40-herb blend faces a compositional review against permitted-ingredient lists, the single most likely source of registration friction or a reformulation demand.

M5 · Health & culture — pre-built cognitive slots, and one hard reframe

Russia is culturally pre-adapted to almost everything Dabur sells — through its OWN traditions, which is both the gift and the trap. The Soviet-era herbal-tonic tradition made элеутерококк (eleuterokokk — eleuthero/Siberian ginseng), родиола розовая (rodiola rozovaya — rhodiola, "golden root"), женьшень (zhen'shen' — ginseng), лимонник (limonnik — schisandra) and мумиё (mumiyo — shilajit) household pharmacy words aif.ru/kp.ru/farosplus, 2024-25 — a population that does not need the adaptogen concept explained, only the question "why an Indian multi-herb jam versus the RUB 77–117 single-herb tincture already on the shelf" answered. Ayurveda has decades-deep niche recognition (Soviet-era Indian cultural exposure, the post-Soviet yoga/wellness subculture, a live specialist e-tailer layer). The banya tradition keeps natural oils and honey inside a mainstream ritual [!] no market-sizing found — qualitative hook only. The pharmacy (аптека, apteka) functions as a quasi-primary-care channel with ~70% trust in counter staff pharmvestnik/nafi. And the mass-skin need-state is REAL, not aspirational: −20°C to −35°C winters make barrier creams a universal purchase with a beloved Soviet archetype (Детский крем, Detsky krem — "children's cream") already in the culture's hands.

The hard reframe, binding for every Batch-5 Russia deck: China-style whitening-as-beauty-ideal does not exist as a Russian category frame. What exists is corrective pigmentation care — against веснушки (vesnushki — freckles), пигментные пятна (pigmentnye pyatna — age/sun spots), постакне (postakne — post-acne marks). Any Dabur SKU adjacent to tone must speak ONLY that dermatological grammar; "whitening/fairness" language is not merely off-brand here, it is culturally illegible and legally reviewable. The programme's lightening fence thus takes its fifth environmental form in Russia: NOT-A-CATEGORY — the fence holds by reframing, not by policing. And the halal flip lands mid-way in this batch: positive and quietly useful in Tatarstan/Bashkortostan/North-Caucasus routing and for the Muslim-Turkic CIS later in the batch, but NOT the national Russian frame — never lead with it in federal-level copy.

M7 · Retail — a marketplace-first country, with a drogerie and apteka second storey

Russia's answer to the programme's standing channel question is unusually clean: Wildberries (~35% of online orders) and Ozon (~28%) together handle 77% of all online orders and 53% of order value; the top-4 platforms (adding Yandex Market and MegaMarket) control 80% of orders and >RUB 11.2 trillion combined turnover; top-3 marketplace GMV reached RUB 8.59tn in 2025, +32.2% sostav/Kommersant/retail.ru 2025 [!] all platform figures. For an entrant without a distributor network, the marketplace IS the national shelf — and Dabur is already on it (M9). Modern trade is a second wave, not a first: X5 Group (Pyaterochka, Perekrestok, the Chizhik discounter) is #1 at RUB 4.64tn revenue 2025 (+18.8%), overtaking Magnit (~RUB 3.5tn), whose dedicated beauty banner Magnit Kosmetik (~8,000 outlets, rebranding М.Косметик) is the obvious modern-trade personal-care target once a distributor exists. The drogerie tier is led by Золотое яблоко (Zolotoye Yabloko — "golden apple", RUB 155.5bn, premium) and — more relevant to Dabur's tier — Улыбка радуги (Ulybka Radugi — "rainbow's smile", RUB 41.9bn, +29%, ~1,908 stores concentrated in the North-West/Volga regions) retail.ru/Forbes 2024-25. Pharmacy: top-10 chains hold 56.9% of a RUB 1.245tn market — Apteka Apreli (~9.1%), Rigla (~7.9%, 6,766 outlets), Planeta Zdorovya (~5.6%) — with apteka.ru (Katren group) as the ordering aggregator pharmacy trade press, 2025.
