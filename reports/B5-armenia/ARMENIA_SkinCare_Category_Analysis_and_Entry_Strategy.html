C3 · Teardown — the champion, the tiers, and the two selves

Nairian — the champion, read with full respect. Everything the programme's heritage grammar reaches for, a local company already built: its own Damask rose farm (supply-chain truth the membership gate would demand), the manuscripts (the sisterhood substrate, commercially claimed), export validation (Hwahae lists the Rose Toner in Korea), and domestic pride distribution. The teardown's read is strategic humility: Nairian is not a competitor to beat but a BOUNDARY to respect — any Gulabari move that reads as contesting the Armenian rose story loses twice (against the champion's authenticity, and against the market's loyalty to its own). The entry's whole design is therefore ALTITUDE SEPARATION: Nairian is the estate rose — premium, single-origin, Armenia's own; Gulabari can only be the everyday rose — the subcontinent's accessible, multi-use gulab jal, priced in a different band, telling a different country's tradition, never once using the word «հայկական» (Armenian) near a rose.

The French dermo tier. Actively merchandised premium (the Vichy campaign, the LRP account) — the pharmacy's skin vocabulary here is FRENCH-clinical, which shapes the register available to Herbolene: plain, functional, ingredient-listed — never pseudo-clinical, because the genuine clinical tier is present and priced above.

K-beauty. Younger, digital, mall-anchored (Erborian) — the routine tier is more developed than Azerbaijan's, less than a hub market's. Not contested; its buyers meet Gulabari only if the everyday-rose positioning wins its gate.

The EAEU mass tier. Nivea and Nevskaya presumptively on-shelf via the corridor, prices uncaptured — the Детский anchor resists capture for the fourth consecutive market and the discipline holds: no Herbolene multiple is set off an estimate; the G1 photograph rules.

The corridor self — Herbolene's head start. Unique in the batch's skin arc: the lead SKU already MOVES on the EAEU rail (WB/Ozon/Beloris/Sima-Land at 176–389 RUB), positioned in Russian retail exactly as the entry would position it here («vazelin dlya kozhi» — cosmetic petroleum jelly with aloe and vitamin E). The grey-to-white thesis applies at its smallest scale: the demand experiment is part-run; the native founding (AMD price, pharmacy shelf, HY label) completes it.

Self-teardown. Dabur skin in Armenia: one SKU with a running rail and a real seasonal case (Herbolene), one SKU with a beautiful story that is OWNED locally by someone else (Gulabari), a fence with no local language evidence either way, and a set behind it that has already built every piece of machinery this file needs. The honest weaknesses: the everyday-rose differentiation may be too subtle for a shelf glance (the gate exists for exactly this); the mass tier's price floor is unmeasured; and winter demand, while real, is seasonal — the Herbolene P&L carries a summer trough by design.

THE OCCUPIED-KINSHIP CASE — the heritage grammar's newest state

The grammar's record now spans: three forms born (UZ), a full null (KG), the richest substrate gated (AZ), sisterhood re-run (AM OTC) — and this file adds the state none of those anticipated: KINSHIP WHERE THE LOCAL BRANCH IS CORPORATELY OWNED. The rose-water family tree genuinely includes both countries (Persian-lineage distillation, the shared vard/gulab root), but Armenia's branch is not a diffuse tradition a foreign kin can honour into welcome — it is a living company's brand equity, farm-backed and export-proven. Kinship framing against a CULTURE risks appropriation; kinship framing against a COMPANY risks something more prosaic and more dangerous: reading as a me-too competitor wearing borrowed poetry. The case's law, written for the programme: where heritage is corporately owned, the foreign kin does not claim the family — it claims its OWN house at a different address: Gulabari as India's everyday gulab jal (the multi-use tradition — a splash for skin, a spoon for desserts, a sprinkle for occasions — sold in volumes and at prices the estate toner never touches), differentiated by altitude, origin and USE-BREADTH, with the Vardavar etymology admired publicly and claimed never. The DIFFERENTIATION GATE tests the design: a 12–15 cohort (shared recruiting with the OTC gates' spec) meets the everyday-rose framing beside a Nairian toner; the kill read is confusion («սա Nairian-ի էժան տարբերա՞կն է» — is this the cheap Nairian?) dominating; the pass read is category separation (different product, different country, different use). Fail → Gulabari defers to a year-2 file and the skin entry ships Herbolene-only — a launch that loses a SKU, not a thesis.
