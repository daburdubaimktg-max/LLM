C3 · Teardown — the umbrella's residents

Splat — the umbrella's architect and the entry's teacher. The premium-herbal position at 2,880–2,900 ֏ was built on an 18-herb narrative, organic lines, and a no-advertising trust model (the founder's letter). Two lessons taken, one refused. Taken: the story premium is real here, and conviction-led content beats media weight in this market's premium tier. Taken: herbal does NOT mean cheap in Armenia — the tier structure inverts the Central Asian instinct. Refused: copying the no-ads model — Splat had fifteen years and a Russian home market to compound trust; a new entrant borrows the LESSON (content over noise) while still buying the introductions.

Colgate — the mass metronome. 1,190–1,400 ֏, ubiquitous across marketplaces and supermarket storefronts. Its whitening SKU at the mass price (1,190) shows whitening is a default claim here, not a premium one — which repositions where Red's "ayurvedic whitening" reads: as story-tier, not claim-tier.

The therapeutic bloc — Sensodyne, Lacalut, Paradontax. The pharmacy's clinical shelf, 1,790–2,850 ֏, German-pharma and gum-repair cues. Not Dabur's fight; its per-ml prices simply hold the umbrella open.

Foramen and the unnamed domestic plant — the local flank. The flank is treated with the programme's standing respect — and the manufacturer is explicitly filed as a POSSIBLE PARTNER lead (contract packing, local goodwill, a "made-with-Armenia" option years out) before it is ever filed as competition.

Лесной бальзам — the presumptive analogue. Russia's 5-herb value paste moves on the same corridor and its presence on Armenian shelves is likely but UNPROVEN [!]. It matters because it is the closest structural analogue to a value-herbal Dabur play — and its price point, once captured, decides whether the umbrella has a value-herbal floor or whether Red's mid-tier entry stands alone.

Azerbaijan REFUSED Red by reflex-check: the miswak monopoly was the better asset and the range followed the market. The Splat lesson, expanded — what the umbrella's architect actually teaches. Splat's Armenian position is worth one more paragraph because it is the market's own case study in everything the entry attempts. A challenger brand with an ingredient story entered a Colgate-anchored market and built a tier 2.5× above the anchor — without local advertising, on packaging conviction, pharmacy presence and imported trust. Three transferable mechanics: the COMPOSITION AS HERO (Splat's pack reads like a formulary — Red's 40-botanical list is the same genre, told from a different tradition); the TIER DISCIPLINE (Splat never chases promos downward — its price IS its claim; the founded-price doctrine arrives at the same law from the opposite direction); and the CHANNEL FIT (premium herbal lives where advice lives). One non-transferable: Splat's Russian-market halo did the introduction work that Red's card programme must buy retail — which is exactly what the ~4–6 index voice-and-card line in the waterfall prices. The umbrella's architect, studied rather than fought, hands the entry its manual.
