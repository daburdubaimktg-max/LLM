The missing habit, designed for. No pre-wash oiling exists here; the deck treats that as a COST, not a challenge line: Amla's launch content is education-first (the how-to, the mask-vs-oil distinction, the twice-a-week frame), budgeted as its own line, Facebook-and-Instagram carried per the macro's doctrine, and its success metric is honest — repeat purchase in the corridor-shopper cohort, not viral reach. The rosemary trend is ridden where it appears (an Amla-rosemary content pairing), never bought outright.

The identity register, handled with the care it demands. Hair carries cultural weight here — thick, dark hair as an ethnic-pride marker, a rich traditional vocabulary around it (the "140 words" claim noted and BARRED from citation per the refusals table) — and the deck's register rules convert that weight into discipline rather than decoration. The strength/thickness/shine registers are used because they land on values the culture already holds; no asset ever EXPLAINS those values back to their owners (the fastest way a foreign brand condescends), no folklore is cited without a primary source, and the double gate's cultural-tone reviewer holds a specific brief: does this line speak TO an Armenian woman about her hair, or ABOUT Armenian women to no one? The first passes; the second dies in review. The rule costs nothing and prevents the one creative failure mode this market would not forgive.

The voice week, Armenian edition. The cannibalisation question, answered before it is asked. The native layer will take some of its volume from Dabur's own corridor flow, and the deck refuses to hide the arithmetic: a corridor sale earns the company its Russia-market margin minus platform economics; a native sale earns the Armenia P&L its contribution AND buys everything the corridor cannot — shelf equity, review base, price identity, a distributor relationship. The founding is therefore accretive even at high substitution rates, and the only genuinely bad outcome is the one the doctrine already bars: a price war between Dabur's own two layers. The tolerance band exists precisely so the two coexist by design — the corridor as the long tail's server, the native layer as the brand's home. One brand, two doors, one price logic — written down before launch so no quarterly review ever discovers the question fresh.

The two-window fit, hair edition. The macro's calendar gives hair a specific pair of occasions: the January summit fits the GIFT formats (a Vatika mask-and-oil set in New Year livery — Gaghant Baba's table is where premium personal care changes hands here), and Vardavar's July water festival fits the one thing no other batch market wanted — a summer hair story (post-sun repair, the wet-hair day as a light-hearted content hook, never a product claim). Both windows obey the standing law: the January set books Poti freight by early November or waits a year; the Vardavar content line costs almost nothing and ships regardless. Hair takes the windows the wellness deck cannot use — the set's calendar divides by category rather than competing within it.

DEEP THINK · The grey-to-white thesis, interrogated. The deck's premise — that formalising an existing pass-through demand is cheaper and safer than creating demand — has one honest vulnerability per leg. LEG ONE (the demand is real): the Ozon node proves merchandising judgment, not volume — a taxonomy entry can serve three hundred customers or thirty thousand, and the deck never learns which until the platform partnership or the native velocity answers; the founded architecture is therefore sized to the NATIVE ladder, not to imagined corridor volume. LEG TWO (nativising captures it): the corridor shopper may simply be a Russian-platform loyalist whose habit does not transfer to SAS shelves at any price — the corridor-shopper panel at G1 exists precisely to hear her say so before the listings commit. LEG THREE (the corridor stays benign): the May-2026 dispute showed the corridor's rules can change in days; if it ever TIGHTENS inbound, the native layer becomes the only layer — which is the quiet second argument for building it now, and the reason the thesis survives its own interrogation: every failure mode of the pass-through makes the native foundation MORE valuable, not less.
