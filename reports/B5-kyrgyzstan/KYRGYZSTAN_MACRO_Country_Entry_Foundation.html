M1 · Demography — small, young, two sub-markets

Population ~7.0–7.3M (Worldometer 7.30M [!] stat.gov.kg), median age ~26.5 — as young as Uzbekistan, a tenth the size. Urbanisation is LOW (~35–38%) — the rural majority shops bazaars and small formats, not modern trade. Bishkek (~1.2M metro) is the beachhead and a linguistic island: Russian is the home language for ~56% of its residents while Kyrgyz dominates nationally (~71%) [!] WVS-derived. The structural read every prior market lacked at this sharpness: the north–south divide is load-bearing — the north (Bishkek, Chuy, Issyk-Kul) is Russified, secular, Kazakhstan-oriented; the south (Osh, Jalal-Abad, Batken) is more observant, Uzbek-influenced, Fergana-oriented, with ~700k Uzbek speakers. The file treats Kyrgyzstan as TWO sub-markets sharing one paper: Bishkek-first launch leaning Russian; national scale-up Kyrgyz-forward; the south routed with the observant-market discipline the batch built in Uzbekistan.
