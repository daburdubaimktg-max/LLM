C1–C2 · Category frame — the climate is the brief

Kyrgyzstan's skin category is organised by physics before preference. The mountain-continental climate delivers long, harsh, dry winters at altitude and intense UV on high ground in summer — a need-state pairing (barrier repair + photoprotection) that local retail already serves in one bundled logic: Akhromin, live on neman.kg, markets itself simultaneously as an «отбеливающий» cream AND a UV-protective one [neman.kg product page via search index]. The CIS-wide «защитный крем от мороза» winter category (vaseline/petrolatum films with sea-buckthorn or mink-oil actives, marketed for outdoor work to −40°C) is documented in Russian/Belarusian sources but NOT directly confirmed on KG shelves [!] search neman.kg/bimed.kg for защитный/зимний крем — a gap the G1 walk closes in an hour. The frame's plain conclusion: winter utility is the category's native language, and Herbolene — petroleum-jelly base, herbal actives, halal-certifiable from the RAK/Turkey hubs — speaks it without translation.

C3 · Teardown — five presences and a null

Akhromin — the incumbent that frames the shelf. A Bulgarian-lineage budget whitening cream, live at Neman, bundling whitening and UV claims in one jar. Its teardown value is diagnostic: it proves the pharmacy shelf sells skin FUNCTION (protection, correction) rather than skin ritual, and it embodies the whitening-language reality the fence must answer. It is not displaced — it is simply not competed with: Herbolene sells barrier, not bleach.

Nevskaya Kosmetika / Детский крем — the loved floor, price unknown. The CIS's default gentle cream is confirmed regionally but uncaptured locally [tut-beauty.by/dionashop.kz — BY/KZ prices only]. The teardown holds it as the REFERENCE COMPETITOR for Herbolene's value story: the winter jar must justify its premium over the children's cream in actives and format, and the exact premium is unpriceable until G1. The honest line: the deck's most important competitor is the one it cannot yet price.

K-beauty via KORSHOP/Korlux — the hub, not the trickle. Fifteen years of Korean-cosmetics wholesale FROM Bishkek's own Dordoi market, stocking Ekel, Farmstay, Skin House, Holika Holika, Dr. Jart, Elizavecca, Heimish, Medi-Peel, Deoproce, serving the CIS [korshop.one; optlist.ru]. The strategic read: Bishkek's beauty trade is more sophisticated than its wallet suggests — routines, serums and the pigmentation sub-frame arrived years ago at wholesale scale. Herbolene does not fight this tier; it sells beneath it, in the winter-utility register K-beauty does not occupy. But the deck notes the asymmetry for the record: in a market this small, the K-beauty hub is the only skin player with genuine distribution muscle, and any Dabur skin ambition beyond the balm eventually meets it.

Chinese mass via Dordoi-Junhai — present as a category, anonymous as brands. Cosmetics confirmed as a Dordoi wholesale category in the China-goods zone; no named brands surfaced [optlist/saby]. The bazaar's floor tier — the price band below which no branded entry should chase.

Gulabari — the null, recorded properly. No .kg listing found; regional supply is Kazakhstan/Russia cross-border e-commerce only (omshankar.kz, ganga.kz, ayurveda-shop.ru). And beneath the absence, the deeper null: no Kyrgyz rose-water tradition — culinary, cosmetic, or bridal — was confirmed in Russian/English search, with the nearest analogue (gyulab) belonging to Azeri-Persian tea culture, not Kyrgyz custom [!] T18 #7 runs the Kyrgyz-language check before this null is treated as final. The heritage grammar built across this batch — MEMBERSHIP (Vatika/usma), SISTERHOOD (Ibn Sina), KINSHIP (attar/gulob) — finds nothing to hold here: no shared family tree, no local lineage to honour, no formulation truth to claim. The teardown records the grammar's FIRST NULL RESULT and its consequence: Gulabari enters, if at all, as a plain product (a gentle toner at a fair price on the e-pharmacy shelf), stripped of every heritage register, sized to a follow line, and never spending a som of media. A grammar that can say "nothing applies" is a grammar, not a reflex — the null is the instrument working.

Self-teardown. Dabur skin in Kyrgyzstan today: zero footprint, zero KGS price, one genuinely strong card (the climate case), one modest card (Gulabari as plain follow), a fence question the market poses daily in its own retail language, and a set behind it that has already built the spine, the terms, the reviewer bench and the audit. The weakness the deck owns: skin is the set's least differentiated entry — the balm competes on utility against a loved children's cream and anonymous frost jars, and its winning margin is the herbal-actives story plus the pharmacy brief, not any moat. The plan is sized accordingly: skin is the set's smallest wave, priced to earn its place, not assume it.

The fence, classified — the record's first repeat environment

The Uzbekistan deck completed the fence's six-environment record — unregulated ×2, unverified, policed, not-a-category ×2, precautionary — and Kyrgyzstan now does something none of the six did: it REPEATS a class. This is an unregulated environment with live, dominant whitening language — Akhromin sells the claim at the national pharmacy chain, and the classifieds trade it in four figures of listings — which places KG in the record's unregulated class alongside its earlier members. The deck marks the taxonomy's maturity: the record has stopped extending and begun CLASSIFYING, which is what a finished instrument does. The position itself is unchanged and restated once: Dabur sells no whitening product and makes no whitening claim in Kyrgyzstan, not because a regulator forbids it (none does) and not because the retail language forbids it (it invites it), but because the company's own law — set globally, held in six environments, now seven — does. Herbolene's copy sells barrier, repair and winter; any «отбеливающий» adjacency in creative, listing titles or pharmacy briefs is BARRED, and the counter card says plainly what the balm does not do, because in a market whose shelf language runs the other way, the unprompted clarity IS the brand's differentiation.

Kyrgyzstan's NISM administers halal standards aligned to Malaysian and Turkish models, extending explicitly beyond food to cosmetics — while the same source calls the cosmetics halal market "at an early stage" [nism.gov.kg; gkstandart.com [!] framework status + the 80% vs ~90% Muslim-share census reconciliation]. The deck's position repeats the set's standing law rather than inventing a new one: Herbolene's RAK/Turkey-hub lineage makes the certificate near-free to carry, so it rides on-pack — a quiet answer for the southern shopper who checks — and appears in no headline, no card lead, no media line. The lead inversion, completed on evidence

Russia led with rose heritage against a winter floor; Uzbekistan split the lead (rose builds, balm reaches); Kyrgyzstan completes the arc — the balm leads BOTH build and reach, and the rose follows unadvertised, because here the evidence runs one way: the winter is documented, the rose tradition is not. The inverted-lead doctrine's final form, stated for the batch: the lead goes to whichever product the market's own evidence sells — heritage where heritage is rooted, utility where climate writes the brief — and the doctrine's job is to stop the programme's affection for its best story from overruling the market's plain need. Gulabari is the programme's best skin story; in Kyrgyzstan it is not the market's, and the deck files the distinction as the arc's closing lesson.

The audit's set compilation — the receipts, assigned. The macro's reputation bet promised the batch's first quantified extension-cost audit; this deck, closing the set, assigns the compilation. Four category waterfalls each carry a corridor line (~7–10 index) and a paper line (~0.5–2), all AUTHORED, all falsifiable against invoices within one quarter of each wave landing. The compilation protocol is one page: for each category, the realised corridor + paper + incremental-negotiation cost against the macro's claimed ~10–15 relay index; one row per category, one honest total, filed to the batch owner with the year-end review. Two disciplines govern it. First, attribution: costs the entry would have paid ANYWHERE (pharmacy margins, counter cards, the mini-gate) are excluded — the audit measures the extension, not the market. Second, symmetry: the compilation files whether the receipts flatter the claim or embarrass it, because the Caucasus builds will be costed off this curve and a curve bent by wishfulness mis-prices three markets. If the extension mode is as cheap as the batch believes, this page proves it; if it is not, this page is worth more than the belief was.
