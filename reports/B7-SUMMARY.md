## 3. Cross-batch threads (the East-African playbook)

1. **Authenticity is the scarcest attribute** — counterfeit-taxed markets (KE/TZ) reward trust
   infrastructure Dabur builds (QR, TANOGA recordation); the governed market (RW) rewards being visibly ON
   the state's own rails (S-Mark, Track-and-Trace). Same thesis, two executions.
2. **Radio + mobile money + traditional trade** is the demand engine everywhere — but the mix rebalances:
   TZ = radio-trio nation; RW = radio-ONE + the batch's first launch-viable MoMo cashback mechanic (eKash).
3. **The LUP law flexes with the wallet**: TZ (71% < $3.65) = sachet-first; KE/UG = sachet + small pack;
   RW = **sachets illegal** → small-rigid-pack architecture (bottle + counter tray + wallet cashback doing
   the sachet's three jobs) — a template later markets with plastic regulation will inherit.
4. **Whitening/Fem exclusion doctrine** hardened batch-wide, peaking in RW (presidential attention) and TZ
   (criminal penalties + highest measured prevalence). Rwanda's negative-check vocabulary protocol (born
   from the uruhu false-friend catch) upgrades all prior decks at next revision.
5. **Indian-origin champions everywhere**: CCIL (TZ, rival-shaped), Sulfo (RW, partner-shaped), Kenyan
   trade houses — the diaspora commercial network is the batch's hidden distribution map.
6. **G5 tolling/hub study** carries the batch's manufacturing question: Orbit (KE) is the named candidate;
   TZ jelly tolling and RW are riders, not drivers.
