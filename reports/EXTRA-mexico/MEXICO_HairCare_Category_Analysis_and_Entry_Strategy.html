THE BANNER. The corridor diagnostic returns its twenty-ninth finding: JOB-KEYED. Mexico has no deep oiling ritual (coconut oil is marketed as a treatment/mask here, not daily grooming), no diaspora to seed one, and its "natural herbal heritage" seat is OCCUPIED by a marketing machine — Tío Nacho (Genomma Lab), built on royal jelly and Mexican herbs across ~500,000 points of sale, with Grisi (1863, Manzanilla Grisi among Mexico's first shampoos) holding the deeper heritage register. Yet the Vatika estate has sold for a DECADE at 4× origin prices — because Mexican shoppers buy hair products by the JOB, and Vatika's jobs are already normalised in the market's own vocabulary: henna's job («tapar las canas» — ammonia-free grey coverage) is a mainstream import category (B NATURE, Botica Moderna, Walmart, Mercado Libre, with consumer-press explainers); the treatment-mask job is exactly how coconut oil already sells; and the rizos method is a forming category whose retailers already import solutions on merit (Skala's presence proves it). JOB-KEYED corridors prescribe: name the job, not the tradition — every SKU enters keyed to a job the market already pays for (Vatika Henna → canas; Amla/coconut oils → the mascarilla/tratamiento slot; the curl-fit SKUs → the rizos method), with the Indian heritage told as INGREDIENT SPECIFICITY (the Grisi lesson: generic "natural ancestral" is owned; named actives are not). The buyer question (macro W0 #1) calibrates everything; the ORS adjacency stays SEALED; and the grey estate's MXN$435 anchor collapses to honest official prices — the graft's first visible act.

C4 · THE CORRIDOR DIAGNOSTIC — TWENTY-NINTH FINDING: JOB-KEYED

The neighbours fail cleanly, and their failures draw the finding. Not RITUAL-KITTED (Brazil): there is no ritual — no calendar, no named steps, no kit culture; transplanting the cronograma playbook would manufacture a corridor that doesn't exist. Not PEER-PROVEN (Canada): no same-origin peer operates here (Marico absent), and the local-heritage incumbents share the REGISTER, not the origin — the screen's variant, confirmed as the macro predicted. Not TAUGHT-BUT-UNCLAIMED (France): the henna education isn't orphaned — it is actively harvested by import brands; what's unclaimed is only the BRANDED CENTURY behind the ingredient. What Mexico presents is a corridor made of discrete, pre-taught, separately-priced JOBS: grey coverage, weekly treatment, curl method — each with its own vocabulary, price band and shelf, none joined by a ritual or a heritage narrative. JOB-KEYED corridors prescribe: (1) key every SKU to one named job and speak the job's own vocabulary («tapar las canas» does more work than any heritage line); (2) let ingredient specificity carry the differentiation — "la henna original de la India, de la casa que la embotella desde 1884" beats "ancient natural wisdom" in a market where Grisi owns the second phrase's register; (3) refuse the jobs the structure refuses — anti-caída belongs to the machine that can afford per-execution claim authorizations, and daily oiling belongs to no one. The finding's series contribution: the corridor taxonomy now spans rituals (Brazil), calendars (Spain), vocabularies (Italy), rivals (Canada) and JOBS — the most granular corridor unit yet, and likely the Africa queue's most common shape after NATIVE-SPROUTED soil.
