Third, the silence is absolute and audited — one immunity claim in one Instagram caption is a COFEPRIS event, and the per-execution workflow exists precisely because the muzzle's enforcement is procedural. The finding's series value: it completes a triptych of flagship-weather answers the Africa queue will reuse — Canada taught filing-toward-a-dividend, Brazil taught holding-before-a-wall, Mexico teaches speaking-through-the-culture when the label must stay mute.
