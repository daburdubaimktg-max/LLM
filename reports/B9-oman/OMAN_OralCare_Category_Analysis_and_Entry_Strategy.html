C1 · Executive summary

The play. Oman oral care is an estimated US$28–38M pool (6–8% of the US$472.7M beauty-and-personal-care market; the direct Statista page is the named re-run [!]) with the familiar GCC geometry — a full Colgate ladder from 0.700 (Max Cavity 100ml) to 3.300 (Optic White), Sensodyne owning the therapeutic ceiling to 3.420, and a herbal middle in disarray: Himalaya's Complete Care trades at 0.500 on a −39% promo against its own 0.825 list, the price-signal chaos this engagement has now documented in three markets. And Oman adds a twist no other market has: the local sunnah-adjacent oral ritual is not the arak stick but luban — Dhofari frankincense chewed for teeth and gums, with published antimicrobial evidence against S. mutans and P. gingivalis. The ask (three moves): (1) hold and energise the Red floor (family value framing, baqala small formats via the van build); (2) land Meswak through the CEPA lane at OMR 0.400-class pricing — the authenticity answer to Listerine's rented flavour, «أصل السواك» re-cut for Oman; (3) develop the Meswak + Luban paste — a Dhofar-sourced, Oman-first NPD that no multinational can copy credibly and that doubles as a GCC export story. Kill-switch: Meswak listing + velocity gates per C7; the luban NPD is feasibility-gated (shared RFQ with the Hair deck's W2) and never forced.

C4 · Consumer & culture — two sacred mouths

Oman's oral tradition runs on two parallel practices. The siwak practice — arak sticks from the souq, the sunnah grammar shared across the GCC — anchors the same authenticity question Bahrain's deck exploited: who is the original, when Listerine sells the flavour? And the luban practice, which is Oman's own: Dhofari frankincense chewed daily for teeth, gums and breath, steeped overnight as "luban water," graded from medical-green Hojari down — a living ritual with published antimicrobial evidence (S. mutans, P. gingivalis) that no toothpaste anywhere has ever bottled. The clinical backdrop makes protection claims commercially serious: the national baselines, dated as they are (the last clean survey is from the 1990s [!] MOH refresh), showed dmft 4.61 at age six with only 15.5% caries-free — early-childhood decay of the kind sugar-heavy Gulf diets sustain — so the mother's cavity anxiety is as real here as the heritage codes.
