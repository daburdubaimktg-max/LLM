Every governance action in this deck is therefore designed as a GIFT first: labels they need, claims that protect them, terms that reward them.

DEEP THINK · Three objections answered

Objection 1 — "Six small doors is not a market; this plan confuses evidence with scale." Correct on arithmetic, wrong on function. The six doors are not the revenue plan — they are the PROOF LAYER (category demand, pharmacy acceptability, community endorsement, format learnings) that makes the revenue plan (Navarro's 79 stores, pharmacy e-tail platforms, eventually counters) pitchable with data instead of promises. The believer network is the pilot Dabur never had to fund — adoption keeps the data flowing and the story honest. Scale was always year 2's job; year 1's job is to be adoptable AT scale, papered and dossier'd.

Objection 2 — "The honey dossier invites the scrutiny it claims to pre-empt." The scrutiny arrives either way — a market that tested 47 import samples and published the results is not waiting for Dabur's permission. The choice is scrutiny-with-dossier versus scrutiny-without: the first is a trust story ("they published before we asked"), the second is a scandal template ("Indian import, honey-based, no documentation"). Verdict #28's falsifier accepts the residual risk honestly: if the dossier exists and the lens still condemns, the learning is that no honey-based import can hold this market — worth knowing at dossier cost, not at brand cost.

Objection 3 — "PRESSED means hurry; adoption means patience. Which is it?" Both, on different clocks — and the deck keeps them separate deliberately. The REGULATORY workstream (move 1) runs at maximum speed because its deadline is external: the EU's botanical drift closes windows without consulting entry plans. The COMMERCIAL workstream (moves 2–4) runs at relationship speed because its asset is trust, and trust compounds slowly and defaults instantly. The two clocks meet only once: the papers must exist BEFORE the Navarro pitch, because the pitch's best line is «papeles en regla». Speed where the clock is the world's, patience where the clock is the community's.
