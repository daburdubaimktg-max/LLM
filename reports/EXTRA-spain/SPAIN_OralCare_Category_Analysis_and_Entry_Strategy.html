The wing that arrived first: Dabur's strongest Spanish retail evidence is TOOTHPASTE — routed through the wrong door

THE BANNER. Spain inverts the European pattern: everywhere else in this directive, hair oil led Dabur's accidental footprint and oral care trailed. Here oral care arrived first — Dabur Herbal Toothpaste Red (100ml×12) and Dabur Meswak (100g) are both confirmed listed directly on amazon.es (B01ASEORWY; B010FOWYHC, third-party seller "Masala Wala" — the same operator captured on amazon.it: the pan-EU grey file's second confirmed market), with Meswak cross-listed on Ayurveda-vertical and import sites at €7–10 per tube [!] re-validate — every direct fetch 403'd. But the corridor diagnostic returns its eighteenth finding, CROSS-ROUTED: the demand pool that PRAYS with miswak (2.41M Muslims, UCIDE 2023; the Pakistani Raval; the Moroccan-led majority) shops in halal verticals that sell generic miswak sticks and other brands — while Dabur Meswak sits in the Ayurveda/wellness lane serving a different, smaller buyer. Demand found a door; supply found a door; they are not the same door. The prescription is the cheapest in the eighteen-finding series: re-route, don't re-build — cross-list Meswak into the halal verticals (Belleza Halal, Halalaya, HalalMarketEU — zero Dabur today), sign the Raval bazaars that already sell the stick, govern the grey price to the EU ladder's Iberian rung (€4.79-class vs today's €7.27–9.20 [!]), and keep the wellness lane open — it is the door that worked by itself. Around this: Spain's own heritage-toothpaste culture (Kemphor 1918; Licor del Polo, 19th-century; Lacer's pharmacy fortress) is treated as Italy taught — parallel traditions, never provers to attack — and the fluoride question is held by the no-argument law's fifth application: sin flúor stays a label fact, never a campaign.

C4 · THE CORRIDOR DIAGNOSTIC — EIGHTEENTH FINDING: CROSS-ROUTED

The prior findings fail cleanly: this is not CHANNEL-PRICED (the price is wrong but that is a symptom), not TRANSLATOR-HELD (no translator exists — the double absence carries from hair), not CEILING-PROVEN (no living herbal prover measures this seat; Kemphor measures the NATURAL niche, not the devotional one). What Spain oral presents is new in the series: demand and supply each independently found a channel, and the channels don't touch. The devotional pool buys sticks in halal verticals; the toothpaste sits in wellness verticals selling to a different buyer. CROSS-ROUTED corridors prescribe the series' cheapest fix: a relationship action, not a demand action. No education budget, no story to build, no incumbent to displace — a cross-listing outreach (three named halal platforms), a bazaar-counter terms sheet (the Raval doors already selling sticks), and the discipline not to break the wellness lane that self-built. The eighteenth finding also carries a warning the macro pre-figured: ask-before-assume — the Ayurveda-lane routing may be DELIBERATE portfolio policy (Meswak as wellness brand, not devotional brand, in non-Muslim-majority markets). The internal question is assigned, the cross-listing move gated behind its answer.
