SPAIN — HAIR CARE: CATEGORY ANALYSIS & ENTRY STRATEGY

The aftercare seat: argan owns the story, the salon owns the ritual — and the treatment economy's product periphery is open
Prepared for the Dabur International board · 7 July 2026 · Public-web evidence only · Inherits the Spain Macro (NETWORK-DORMANT; CONNECT-AND-ACTIVATE; the four-pool discipline) · Gap rule in force · All platform prices [!] re-validate at G1 (Spanish session)
THE DECK IN ONE PARAGRAPH: Spanish hair care is the directive's hottest hair market — €2.0bn in 2024 after +8.9% growth, with treatment (+12.1%) and styling (+10.3%) leading — and its structure resists every prior playbook. The miracle-oil story seat is NATIVELY OCCUPIED: argan arrived through the Moroccan corridor (880k Moroccan residents, a ferry-hop supply chain, an import-and-repack industry at $15–25/litre FOB, OGX on mainstream shelves) and reads to Spanish shoppers as domestic heritage, not exotica — Amla cannot out-story an oil the market considers its own. Meanwhile the category's growth engine is not a shelf but a SERVICE: the keratin/alisado brasileño economy — a salon-treatment boom spanning Madrid and Barcelona, explicitly crossing between the Latin-diaspora and Afro-diaspora client bases, with its own product ecosystem (Kativa-class kits) and its own calendar (treatments lasting 3–6 months). The corridor diagnostic returns its seventeenth finding: AFTERCARE-SHAPED — the open seat is the treatment economy's product periphery: pre-treatment preparation, post-keratin maintenance, between-treatment oiling — a POSITION IN A RITUAL'S CALENDAR rather than a rung on a price ladder. The prescription: enter Amla/Vatika as the treatment economy's aftercare oils (the macro's habit-transplant thesis, made operational as a measurable pilot), hold the diaspora floor separately at its own honest prices (the grey €8.17/100ml repriced into coherence), and never fight argan's story — complement it in the ritual's OTHER slots. The four-pool discipline governs throughout: the LatAm pool hears category performance, the diaspora pools hear recognition, and nobody hears heritage theatre.

C4 · THE CORRIDOR DIAGNOSTIC — SEVENTEENTH FINDING: AFTERCARE-SHAPED

Run the diagnostic against Spain's structure and the prior findings all fail to fit. The bridge is not merely built (Italy) — it is OWNED, by an oil the market naturalised through proximity and community; entering as "the other traditional oil" against native argan is a story contest Amla loses by geography. The middle is not taught-unclaimed (France) — the education engine is a SERVICE whose operators (salons) are businesses, not shelves. What Spain actually offers is a seat defined by TIME rather than price or shelf: the keratin ritual's 3–6-month calendar creates product moments the service cannot fill — the week-before preparation, the post-treatment sealing weeks, the month-three maintenance — and no oil brand OWNS those moments in Spanish retail today. AFTERCARE-SHAPED corridors prescribe calendar entry: position the product into the ritual's named slots (aceite pre-tratamiento; el aceite del mes tres), sell WITH the service economy (salon partnerships, kit adjacency) rather than against it, and price to the ritual's economics (a €200-class treatment makes a €9.99 maintenance oil an obvious protection purchase). The finding also finally operationalises the macro's LatAm thesis: habit-transplant is TESTED here, in the ritual the 4.25M brought with them — measurably, honestly, and without one word of heritage marketing.

DEEP THINK · Three objections answered

Objection 1 — "The aftercare seat is imaginary: salons already sell aftercare." They sell the CHEMICAL aftercare (sulphate-free shampoo, mask) attached to the treatment brand's own range. The OIL slot — the between-washes, month-three maintenance slot — is demonstrably unbranded: the Usera scene's wholesaler-of-the-month oil is the evidence. Dabur is not inventing a slot; it is NAMING one the ritual already generates. The attach-rate gate (8%) makes the claim falsifiable within two quarters — if the seat is imaginary, the number will say so and the deck's own kill-switch executes.

Objection 2 — "The LatAm pool without heritage marketing is a contradiction: what does Dabur SAY?" It says what the stylist says: calendar, protection, price-vs-treatment arithmetic. The four-pool discipline's point is that heritage is a REASON for one pool and a DECORATION for another — and decoration costs credibility. Amla's thousand-year line appears once, as provenance (the card's last sentence), never as the pitch. The pitch is €9.99 protecting €200. Performance vocabulary, heritage footnote — the inversion of every other market's copy, and the first deck where that inversion is the DESIGN.

Objection 3 — "Direct specialist relationships don't scale: Dabur becomes a door-to-door company." Ten doors in two districts is not a distribution model; it is a PROOF model — the recognition doctrine executed where no translator exists to execute it through. The scale answer arrives in year 2 with either a community-appointed consolidator (move 2's fails-if branch) or the herbolario file. The double absence made direct signing possible; nothing requires it to stay the only mechanism. What must NOT happen is the reverse error: appointing a generic Spanish distributor over the community's heads and converting the EU set's only translator-free market back into a translated one.
