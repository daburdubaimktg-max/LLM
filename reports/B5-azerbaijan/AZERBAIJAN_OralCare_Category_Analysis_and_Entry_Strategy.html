The tier decision, argued

The re-tier is the deck's one aggressive call, so it carries its argument. FOR: the grey 4.13–4.80 prices were set by resellers optimising clearance, not positioning — they price miswak as if it were generic herbal paste, below even Splat's herbal rung, wasting the only named-ingredient story on the shelf; the twin-pack sets at 6.00 "was" prices sold well enough to be re-listed, suggesting headroom; and Kesh King's hair lesson (the Ayurvedic register clears at a premium when it is the only one on the shelf) transfers within the same shopper population. AGAINST: miswak's local recognition is unevidenced; the whitening grammar may out-pull any herbal story; and a +50% lift on a grey-taught price can simply stall velocity. The design that holds both: Miswak Gold leads at the Splat rung (~7.5), the classic paste-and-brush set becomes the FIGHTER at ~4.5–5 (continuity for the shoppers who found the brand first), and wave 1's velocity read arbitrates before modern trade widens the bet. If the lift stalls, the fighter carries the listing and the tier retreat costs one quarter and no credibility — the experiment is priced, reversible and dated. The whitening culture, read one level deeper. The 39% cluster is not arbitrary: Azerbaijan's consumer culture is image-forward (the salon economy hair documented), tea-drinking is near-universal (staining pressure daily), and smoking prevalence among men is high by European standards [!] inferential pairing, flagged — three structural drivers that make tooth appearance a daily, visible, social variable. The read matters for the range because it ranks the bridge SKU's ceiling: charcoal-miswak is not a flanker here, it may be the range's long-run volume leader, and the deck's architecture deliberately leaves it room to outgrow the lead (if Q2 data says the market wants the whitening story more than the heritage story, the range follows the market — the no-reflex rule cuts both ways). What the read does NOT do is move the launch order: the heritage lead defines the word first, because definitions precede extensions everywhere the programme has measured.

The register line, drawn once. In Sudan or the GCC the miswak word carries Prophetic-tradition equity; in secular Azerbaijan (~20–33% high-religiosity across dated polls [Eurasianet/CRRC [!] fresher wave]) that register is not commercial space, and the deck does not enter it — no Sünnə framing, no faith adjacency, nothing to triple-gate because nothing approaches the gate. The miswak story here is INGREDIENT HERITAGE: a cleaning twig used for centuries across the East, now in a paste — true, warm, and entirely secular. The batch's faith-register arc closes at its lightest-touch endpoint: from triple-gated Kyrgyzstan to a market where the right touch is none at all.

The rented monopoly — the tempo doctrine, argued

The deck calls the miswak position RENTED, and the rent is worth pricing. Eyüp Sabri Tuncer operates a full misvak line in Turkey with brand equity, halal-adjacent heritage, and — decisive here — the same PTA lane and dizi-halo advantages the macro documented for every Turkish house. If EST enters, it enters cheaper (tariff), warmer (familiarity) and louder (an existing creative library). Dabur's defence is not a moat, because none exists; it is TEMPO plus POSITION: own the word's first Azerbaijani telling (the voice week), hold the twin-pack value formats the grey market proved, and occupy the herbal tier's lead shelf slot at Bravo before a second miswak brand gives the buyer a comparison to run. The honest arithmetic: a category of one earns its premium only until the second entrant, after which the first teller of the story keeps the lead IF the story was told well — which is why the voice week's oral assets are written as the category's definition ("misvak nədir" — what miswak is), not as a product ad. Definitions are hard to displace; advertisements are not. The quarterly EST watch joins the standing watch list beside the PTA expansion.
