The regulatory build — the standalone dossier, in flux

The macro's mode shift lands hardest in this deck: EAEU papers carry ZERO legal weight (confirmed — Azerbaijan is not a member and is not joining), so the wellness file's registration is a standalone Azerbaijani-language build. The system is two-agency and — as of one month before this deck — reorganising: the Analitik Ekspertiza Mərkəzi reportedly stopped accepting supplement-expertise applications on 1 June 2026, its function folding into AQTA with examination quoted at 15 business days from payment and samples; AQTA's food-safety certificate then licenses import and sale (facility registration fee cited at 150 AZN; consultants quote 1–1.5 months all-in with complete documents) [pharma.az; afsa.gov.az; emze/pharmex/ismayilzade [!] the handover is UNCONFIRMED — verify with AQTA or counsel before committing any timeline]. Chyawanprash files as food supplement/functional food, never as a drug; the honey line rides food-import paper. The deck's sequencing law, inherited from Uzbekistan and sharpened here: the dossier clock starts at W0 addressed to whichever authority the W0 agent confirms — and no Novruz promise is made until the paper's real cadence is measured, not quoted.

The occupied-story posture — the agreeing-frame protocol's second application, amended

Kyrgyzstan taught the protocol's first form: where the culture already believes in food-as-medicine, the premise is free and spend moves to respect. Azerbaijan agrees with the premise MORE — naftalan is a more dramatic natural-therapeutic claim than anything Ayurveda makes — but adds a twist the protocol must absorb: the narrative REGISTER that premise usually funds ("ancient Eastern healing wisdom") is not free here, because Azerbaijan tells that story about itself. A foreign brand reciting Eastern-wisdom copy in Baku is not exotic; it is a copy of the house style, and reads as such. The amendment, recorded for the programme: an agreeing frame pays out in whichever story slot the local culture has NOT claimed for itself — here, the laboratory: ingredient science, measured actives, named botanicals with named properties. Amla enters Azerbaijan as one of the most vitamin-C-dense fruits measured, standardised in a 40-botanical preserve — a sentence Solgar's shopper can evaluate — and the word "ancient" does not appear in year one's copy at all.

C5 · Consumer decode — the fair shopper, the pharmacy reader, the gift buyer

The fair shopper / terroir loyalist. Buys her honey from a known beekeeper or the fair, by region and season, at 25–40 AZN/kg. She is NOT the target — the deck says so plainly. Her trust structure is generations deep and a branded jar has nothing to offer it. The value lane's design never argues with her; it serves the shopper below her price line.

The supermarket household. Buys groceries at Bravo/OBA, wants honey for tea and breakfast, balks at 30-AZN jars and has no beekeeper uncle. A branded 500g at ~8–10 AZN with visible quality paper (lab-tested, origin-declared) is the offer — everyday honey, honestly priced, no terroir theatre. This is the volume hypothesis gate 3 tests.

The pharmacy reader (the Solgar shopper). Buys D3+zinc+C on the biofactors reflex each autumn at 18–25 AZN a bottle. Chyawanprash meets her as the food-format alternative: one spoon, whole family, amla's vitamin-C density named, composition printed — a preserve that does what her capsule stack does, told in her own science register. Price ~18–22 AZN/500g [!] AUTHORED — inside her demonstrated spend, at family-jar economics her bottles can't match.

The Novruz gift buyer. The year's peak gifting decision-maker, already buying şəkərbura and honey-adjacent sweet boxes. The Dabur wellness box (honey + preserve, festive AZ-Latin livery) enters HER arithmetic — a 10–25 AZN considered gift [!] — and the box format reframes both products: gifted wellness escapes the everyday price ladder entirely. The box is also gate 3's fallback home if the value lane dies.

The trust panel, worked — gate 3's afternoon. Two cells, twelve to fifteen shoppers each, one supermarket-adjacent room in Yasamal and one in Sumgait [!] design AUTHORED.
