FOCUS · One line: open the Caucasus turn with the batch's most structurally distinct market — a stable-manat, oil-financed, Baku-concentrated consumer economy reached NOT by inherited EAEU paper but by a national registration build on Dabur's own Turkey/RAK supply geography — where the entry's first decision is not a price or a brand but an ORIGIN (which hub ships, at which tariff, under whose preferential paper), where the strongest cultural hooks the batch has seen for two Dabur brands (gülab for Gulabari, Novruz gifting for the wellness jar) wait on category evidence, and where the standing instruments must prove they can be DECLINED — the remittance calendar retires, the halal register stays on-pack, and the extension audit becomes a comparison baseline rather than a mode.

M1 · Demography — the one-city market, stated honestly

Azerbaijan is ~10.45M people, ~59% urban [Worldometer 2026], and economically it is far more concentrated than its population map suggests: Baku metro (~2.39M, ~22.9% of population) plus four satellite/secondary cities (Sumgait, Absheron, Ganja, Shirvan/Mingachevir) produced 77% of national economic output in 2023 [Baku Research Institute]. The regional GDP-per-head gap runs to 8× between richest and poorest regions, softened to 6–7× by fiscal transfers [BRI]. The programme has met two-tier markets before — Kyrgyzstan's north–south divide governed that set — but Azerbaijan is a different shape: not two sub-markets sharing one paper, but ONE market and a long thin tail. The never-blend rule's application here is therefore vertical, not horizontal: Baku-tier and regional-tier are priced, packed and channelled separately (premium jars and routines in Baku modern trade; value packs and sachets where the 8× gap bites), and no Baku velocity number is ever extrapolated to Ganja.

The instrument that retires — the remittance calendar, declined on evidence

Three consecutive markets ran remittance-gearing decodes and two run live promo-calendar experiments; Azerbaijan is where the instrument is formally DECLINED. At 3.87% of GDP (2023, trending down; the 2022 spike was a one-off Russian-relocation effect), remittances are not a structural driver of household FMCG timing here — oil-financed public wages and pensions are [WB; BRI]. The macro records the decline explicitly because a maturing instrument set is tested as much by what it declines as by what it applies: the promo calendar that earned its keep in Naryn and Osh would measure noise in Baku. What replaces it as the household-timing hypothesis: the public-sector pay cycle and the Novruz gifting window (M8) — both named, neither yet instrumented, both G1 questions.

M2 · Economy — oil-financed stability, honestly framed

The consumer economy runs on a stable peg (1.70 since 2015), easing inflation (→5.0% forecast end-2026), and oil-rent-financed public spending. Non-oil GDP crossed 50% by one definition in 2025 (sources conflict on the base — flagged, held directional only [!]), but 91.5% of merchandise exports remain mineral fuels [azernews/AZERTAC, 2025], so the honest macro read is: a Brent shock transmits to Baku discretionary spend through the fiscal channel faster than the "diversifying" GDP mix implies. For pricing architecture the peg is the batch's best news since Kazakhstan: AZN price lists can hold for a year without FX re-papering, and the two-anchor rule's arithmetic stays put once set. Statistical opacity is the counterweight — the BRI flags that poverty data is partly withheld [BRI] — so segmentation leans on retail-footprint and e-commerce-penetration proxies over official income statistics, with wide error bars declared.

M3 · Entry lane — THE MODE SHIFT and THE ORIGIN FORK

The mode shift, stated. Azerbaijan is not in the EAEU and has no customs union with Russia. Every piece of inherited paper the last three markets rode — the SGR, the TR CU conformity marks, the corridor's one-rotation logistics — stops at this border. What travels instead is the METHOD Uzbekistan built for exactly this case: the national-build estimate (registration authority mapped, dossier scoped, local agent engaged, 2–4-month clock started before any commercial commitment), applied now to a second national system. The Kyrgyzstan set contributes its discipline (walks before claims, permit questions first) but none of its paper.

The origin fork — the file's single most consequential open question. Azerbaijan runs a simplified 0/5/15% tariff; finished consumer goods — cosmetics, toothpaste, packaged supplements — typically sit in the 15% band [trade.gov CCG [!] HS-line confirmation via customs.gov.az]. The Azerbaijan–Turkey PTA (in force 1 March 2021) eliminates tariffs on 15 product lines, and in February 2026 the two governments agreed to EXPAND its scope [WTO.az; Trend.Az Feb 2026]. Whether toiletries, cosmetics or food supplements are on the list — today or in the expansion — is UNCONFIRMED, and everything commercial hangs on it: Turkey-origin (or Turkey-repacked, subject to rules-of-origin) Dabur SKUs could enter tariff-free while RAK-UAE-origin goods pay the full 15%. That is the difference between pricing beside Turkish brands and pricing above them. The fork is W0 question #1, it is answerable from public documents plus one customs-broker conversation, and NO price architecture in the four category decks ahead is final until it is answered. The permit-question-first law, evolved for its fourth market: ask whose ORIGIN backs the tariff before asking whose paper backs the listing.

T3c · The home lane — Middle Corridor / BTK mechanics

For the first time in the batch, the supply geography is the entering company's own ground. The Baku–Tbilisi–Kars railway (commissioned 2017; Georgian section modernised for $250m, completed May 2024, capacity lifted 1M→5M tonnes/yr) runs Turkey→Georgia→Azerbaijan entirely outside Russian territory and EAEU customs procedures [Jamestown/Caspian News, Oct 2025]. 2025 volumes grew +39% YoY in block trains (296 through September), +20% in TEU (103,134); the Port of Alat is expanding toward 500,000 TEU; and the Ankara Agreement (Jan 2025, Azerbaijan–Turkey–Uzbekistan) coordinates the corridor's container flow. The read: Dabur's Turkey hub ships to Baku on a growing, politically de-risked, Russia-independent lane — the exact inverse of the Russia-set's sanctions-shadowed logistics file. The macro prices no specific freight rate (none captured [!]) but fixes the structural fact: if the PTA fork resolves toward Turkey origin, lane and tariff align on the same answer, and Azerbaijan becomes the batch's first market where supply strategy and trade policy point the same way.

M4 · Regulation — the second national build, scoped

Dietary supplements (qida əlavələri/БАД — Chyawanprash's likely classification) require mandatory pre-market state registration with the Analitik Ekspertiza Mərkəzi (Center for Analytical Expertise) under the Ministry of Health: manufacturer dossier in Azerbaijani, composition with quantities, dosage form, shelf-life, and for imports a certificate of origin + conformity + HACCP/GMP/ISO [emze.az; pharmex.az; ismayilzade.az]. Cosmetics route separately: ingredient review, Azerbaijani-language instructions, labelling compliance, and an AQTA (Food Safety Agency) certificate. Budget 2–4 months and a local registration agent — Baku consultancies (EMZE, PharmExpert, Ismayilzade) specialise in exactly this for foreign FMCG entrants. The UZ build's sequencing law applies verbatim: the registration clock starts at W0, BEFORE any commercial commitment, so the paper and the Neman-equivalent negotiation (here: Zeytun and the modern-trade chains) converge rather than queue. Two source conflicts are disclosed rather than resolved: the category pass reports the supplement-expertise function REPORTEDLY handed from the AEM to AQTA on 1 June 2026 (unconfirmed — the dossier is addressed to whichever authority the W0 agent confirms [!] T13 #4's first answer), and the timeline estimates diverge — consultancies quote 2–4 months, the category pass ~1–1.5 months for supplements; the calendar plans on the SLOWER figure and celebrates the faster one if it materialises.

M5 · Culture — the batch's richest hook file, held to category evidence

Novruz Bayramı (spring equinox) is the calendar's commercial summit: deep gifting customs, reconciliation visits, the groom's-family gift sequence peaking on Axır Çərşənbə (the last Tuesday before the holiday), and an edible-gift culture (şəkərbura, paxlava, dried fruit, jams) into which a wellness gift box fits without invention [advantour; azerbaijan.az]. Tea culture is near-universal and ritualised (armudu glasses, the guest-must-drink norm) — and inside it sits the batch's single strongest brand hook: gülab (rose water) is a live, named, prized domestic tradition, added to tea for aroma, folk-credited for stomach and heart benefits, ambient enough that some Baku teahouses smell of it [Baku Magazine; azernews]. After Kyrgyzstan returned the heritage grammar's first null, Azerbaijan presents its richest positive candidate yet: Gulabari would enter not as a foreign concept but as a RE-ENTRY into a tradition the shopper already owns — the kinship form's strongest possible substrate, pending the skin note's category evidence and the full gate. Naftalan (crude-oil balneotherapy, ~15,000 medical tourists/yr, folklore back to Nizami Ganjavi) is not a Dabur category asset but is the agreeing-frame protocol's second application: a culture whose own natural-therapeutic reference point is MORE dramatic than anything Ayurveda claims does not need persuading that natural substances heal — the KG lesson (premise free, spend moves to respect) arrives with the posture question already half-answered.

M6 · Media & language — Latin script, Russian's Baku niche, the counted and the missing

Azerbaijani (Latin script) is the sole official language and the mandatory dossier/labelling language. Russian remains a Baku urban register — 81% of Baku residents report speaking it (CRRC 2013 base, dated, directionally re-cited [!] newer Caucasus Barometer wave) — but nationally only ~30% are fluent and the young skew English-as-L2. The design consequence inverts the Central-Asian sets: AZ-Latin leads every asset, Russian is a Baku-secondary layer, and nothing ships in Cyrillic. The counted: Instagram 4.32M users (40.8% of population, June 2025, NapoleonCat); 9.23M internet users (89%); 6.73M social profiles (64.9%, Jan 2025, DataReportal via snippet — direct fetch 403 [!]). The MISSING, named as the media plan's gap: no Azerbaijan-specific TikTok/WhatsApp/Telegram counts surfaced — the regional pattern (WhatsApp for commerce, TikTok for Gen-Z beauty) is hypothesis only, and the media annex cannot finalise until T15 #6 runs. Turkish dizi spillover is structural: dramas air un-subtitled, and the awareness halo that carries LC Waikiki and Koton carries Dalan and Arko too — the competitive set Dabur prices against arrives pre-advertised.

M8 · E-commerce — a partnered duopoly, and the flagship that may have arrived first

The platform layer is neither Kaspi's monoculture nor Uzum's national build: Trendyol (Turkish, ~2M Azerbaijan customers, Baku office since Oct 2024) and Birmarket (ex-Umico, domestic, ~2M customers, the "Bir" ecosystem with BakıKart transit payments) — and the two are PARTNERED, with Trendyol embedded as a storefront inside Birmarket [Türkiye Today; bir.az/AZERTAC [!] both 2M claims overlap in a 10.4M market; no AZ-specific GMV split found]. The commercial read: two doors, one increasingly shared room — a listing strategy negotiates both but plans one catalogue.

The inverted flagship check — CONFIRMED, not hypothesised. Kazakhstan's file was organised by an ABSENCE (no Amla on Kaspi); Azerbaijan's opens with a documented PRESENCE: the category research pass found 24 Dabur SKUs already retailing on Umico/Birmarket — Vatika, Amla, Maha Bhringraj, Miswak — fulfilled by third-party resellers (DR LAILA COSMETICS, REYKHAN BOUTIQUE, AVENA BEAUTY), with real AZN prices: Amla hair oil at 8.18–9.50 AZN/200ml, Miswak toothpaste at 4.13–6.97 AZN [!] listing-level re-pull from an AZ session; seller stock depth unknown, plus a 403-blocked Trendyol Amla trace. This is a grey import layer, not a managed launch — no official distributor stands behind the listings — and it settles the two-anchor question before G1: a GREY anchor exists, in public AZN, set by resellers Dabur has never met. The two-anchor rule's third species is therefore live, and the entry's first pricing decision is already written: ratify the grey band (if it is healthy), reprice it via an official listing takeover (if it is torn), or knowingly undercut it (if the fork's tariff answer allows). What the grey layer proves either way: demand exists at four-to-ten AZN price points without a som of Dabur marketing — the entry starts from evidence, not hope.

M10 · Synthesis — the 19th honey verdict, the calendar, the batch position

The honey file — verdict #19, and the taxonomy's first VALUE-LANE case

Azerbaijani honey is mountain-flora national pride (~240,000 registered hives, state subsidies [FAO]) with a price structure the batch has not seen: local honey retails across a 20–60 AZN/kg band (the two research passes bracket it at ~25–30 core) — ~$12–35/kg at the peg [search-synthesised [!] direct lalafo.az/honey.az scrape] — and the passes CONFLICT on branding: one found a branded domestic player (honey.az, "I'm Honey") with varietal SKUs and corporate gift boxes; the other reads the market as unbranded/provenance-driven with the Baku Honey Fair (state-attended, prestige) as its real institution. The conflict is itself a verdict input — a market mid-transition from provenance to brand is exactly where a value import tests best — and gate 1's scrape resolves it. Every prior market's verdict logic ran one way: the local jar was cheaper and prouder, so the import abstained or sold a narrow ritual niche. Azerbaijan INVERTS the price leg: local honey is premium-priced, branded honey gifting is an established behaviour, and the year's peak gift window (Novruz) is documented. The 19th verdict is therefore the taxonomy's first SELL-TEST (VALUE LANE): a hypothesis, not a commitment — Dabur honey may be able to enter BELOW the local jar as the honest-value import in a market whose own honey has priced itself into the gift tier — gated on three checks before a single jar ships: the direct price scrape (is 25–30 AZN/kg real at retail?), the duty answer from the origin fork (15% vs PTA), and the trust question (does an import undercut or merely look cheap beside terroir pride — a G1 panel question). The taxonomy's width now spans ABSTAIN to VALUE-TEST across 19 verdicts, and the instrument's rule holds: the verdict is derived from the local price structure, never from the last market's answer.
