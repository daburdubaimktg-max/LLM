M5 · Culture — the twin-heritage market, and NO halal file

Georgia is ~84% Orthodox; the halal file does not exist here, per the kit brief's final application. What stands instead is the batch's richest heritage architecture: 8,000 years of winemaking with the qvevri method UNESCO-listed (2013) and the supra feast with its tamada as the core social institution — a culture whose native logic is "ancient tradition = superior quality"; the 11th-century Ustsoro Karabadini medical almanac as the folk-medicine touchstone, with herbal chacha infusions still a living home-remedy practice; jara wild-hive honey holding national Intangible Cultural Heritage status; the tea heritage (the USSR's garden, world #4 at peak, now a craft revival); and a native wellness vocabulary of waters and baths — Borjomi ("the Caucasian Vichy") and Tbilisi's own name meaning "warm place," anchored by the 1,500-year Abanotubani sulfur baths.
