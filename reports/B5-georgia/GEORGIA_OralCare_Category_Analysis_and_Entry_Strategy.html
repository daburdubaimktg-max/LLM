The pharmacy-density decode — why 595 pharmacies change the category's physics. Georgia's pharmacy density (two or three per block in Tbilisi's core) does more than route distribution; it re-writes the shopper's relationship with the category. The negotiation's fine print therefore asks for full-network distribution rather than pilot branches, trading launch-window depth for breadth — and the deck notes the trade explicitly, because a pilot-branch listing that LOOKS like progress would quietly surrender the category's real physics.
