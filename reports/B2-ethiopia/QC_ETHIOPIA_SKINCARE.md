## 2. Deck-specific conformance

- **The strategic spine:** Skin = **BUILD/trailer**, "natural glow, never bleach." Two poles + a hole: petroleum-jelly occlusive mass (Vaseline)
  + toxic whitening pool (Glow&Lovely/Caro — 64.2% ever-used, 49.7% adverse, 8.9% ingredient literacy). **Dabur owns the hole** with the
  **double-substitution proposition** ("natural care and glow — without the petroleum, and without the lightening chemicals").
- **Pre-positioned for the ban:** Ethiopia is a UNEP/Minamata mercury-elimination target and hasn't banned lightening (unlike Ghana/Rwanda/Kenya);
  the natural non-bleach brand is where demand flees when enforcement tightens — a regulatory threat converted to a moat Unilever's own equity
  (Vaseline-petroleum + Glow&Lovely-whitening) forbids it from crossing.
- **The excise wall + two levers:** cosmetics carry a **100% federal excise** (HS 3304) → ~200–240% landed tax → imported finished retails >5× FOB
  (structurally uncompetitive). **Local toll-manufacture is the base case** (~25–35% cheaper; Mekab/Repi/Cosmar); **rose water (HS 3307) escapes the
  excise** — making **Gulabari the most tax-efficient imported SKU** and a deliberate product-mix arbitrage lever.
- **Hero = Dermoviva** (occlusive + even-tone substitution) + **Gulabari** (rose-ritual trial + 3307 tax lane) + **Dermoviva Baby** (high-births
  white-space); **Herboline** = natural umbrella flag; **Fem "bleach" handled with tongs** (reframe turmeric-glow/de-tan or park).
- **Trailer-to-Hair-van:** skin rides Hair's Alfarag spine + Herboline flag + sachet shelf; converts Hair reach into basket at near-zero
  incremental distribution cost — an asymmetric bet + a pre-positioned option, which is why it's the trailer not the spearhead.
- **Depth finding:** Dabur skin catalogue-present (Alfarag) but **shelf-absent** (no priced .et listing); Gulabari cross-border + off Alfarag's list.
  The gap is activation/merchandising + adding Gulabari to the book, not cold access.
