  **Hobby fighter-shampoo pincer**; Amla Kids white-space.
- **Enter underneath Sunsilk:** Sunsilk (local factory, bespoke range) owns "made for Ethiopian hair" (formula) + "real natural hair"
  (authenticity, LEAP 30/40) — so Dabur enters on the **oil/ritual territory shampoo structurally can't claim**, then converts to a trio.
  Don't chase the diaspora premium (Cantu/Shea Moisture ETB 2,000–6,500, <5% reach). **Replace the artisanal butter tier** on hygiene + trust.

  finished-goods excise stack (the 10%↔100% classification risk), halves FX demand, and aligns with import-substitution — the reason to
  **enter finished to seed, convert to toll-fill before scaling** (Sunsilk's pre-emptive move).
- **Channel re-derived:** **win kiosk + salon/braider and you own the market** (braider = fattest margin + trusted recommender); modern trade +
  e-com = Addis halo; Merkato 2–5% thin pass-through → brand-build via direct/hybrid overlay.
