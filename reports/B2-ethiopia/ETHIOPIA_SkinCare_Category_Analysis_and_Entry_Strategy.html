THE ASK: Skin is a BUILD / trailer — do not lead with it; attach a natural-herbal, halal, value-tier skin house to the Hair van's route-to-market. Ethiopia's skin shelf has two poles and a hole: a petroleum-jelly/lotion occlusive mass (Vaseline ቫዝሊን the most-scanned brand, genericised, ~53 distributors/~100k outlets) driven by real dry-highland + high-UV need, and a large-but-toxic whitening pool (Glow&Lovely, Aichun, Caro — mercury/hydroquinone/steroids sold OTC; 64.2% of Bahir Dar women ever-used, 49.7% report adverse effects, only 8.9% know the active ingredient). Dabur owns the hole — the "even-tone / natural radiance / herbal nourishment (ተፈጥሯዊ ብርሃን)" territory — with a double-substitution proposition no one else can make: "natural care and glow — without the petroleum, and without the lightening chemicals." The whitening tier is the biggest single volume pool and the biggest reputational landmine in the whole Dabur-Ethiopia portfolio: Ethiopia is a named target of the UNEP/Minamata mercury-elimination drive, and the natural non-bleach brand is where fleeing demand lands when the ban tightens — Dabur must be pre-positioned on the right side of that line. Two hard economics: (1) cosmetics carry a 100% federal EXCISE (HS 3304), stacking to a ~200–240% landed tax — the heaviest of any category — so an imported finished lotion retails >5× FOB and local toll-manufacture is the base case, not an option; (2) rose water (HS 3307) escapes the 100% excise — a genuine tax-arbitrage lever making Gulabari the most tax-efficient imported SKU. Hero = Dermoviva (occlusive substitution) + Gulabari rose-ritual trial + Dermoviva Baby white-space; Fem "bleach" = handle with tongs (reframe turmeric-glow or park). Dabur skin is catalogue-present via Alfarag but shelf-absent [!] confirm. Category verdict (macro T18): BUILD / trailer. Dual cue: halal east + "natural/plant" fasting-congruent highlands.

C1 — The ask (executive summary)

Macro anchor (§M4, §M7, T18): Skin is ranked BUILD / trailer — no distinctive ritual as strong as hair/oral, a crowded cheap-lotion + toxic-whitening field, and the heaviest tax stack of any category. Attach to the Hair van (same Alfarag spine, same Herboline natural flag, same sachet shelf); own "even-tone / natural," never bleach; sequence to local-make; served from RAK/Turkey.

The play in one screen. Four truths shape the skin strategy. First, the category has already migrated from "fair/white" to "healthy / even-tone / glow / multivitamin" — even Unilever renamed Fair&Lovely to Glow&Lovely — and that migration is Dabur's open door: own the natural-glow / even-tone / herbal-nourishment territory with a proof-led botanical story (telba/nug/qibe/rose — naturals Ethiopians already legibly trust) that out-authenticates Vaseline's chemistry and Glow&Lovely's rebrand, while stepping entirely around the mercury/hydroquinone landmine. Second, the double substitution is the wedge: Dabur can replace both the petroleum-jelly occlusive and the toxic whitening cream in one line — "natural care and glow, without the petroleum, without the bleach" — in a market that already believes in plant oils and is starting to fear the chemicals. Third, the economics make local-make the base case: cosmetics carry a 100% federal excise (HS 3304), stacking to ~200–240% landed tax, so an imported finished lotion retails >5× FOB and is undercut by grey/local product — while rose water (HS 3307) escapes the excise, making Gulabari a deliberate tax-arbitrage lever, and toll-manufacture cuts the shelf ~25–35% (no 35% duty on finished value, a lower excise base, no surtax). Fourth, skin rides Hair, it does not fund its own build: same distributor spine, same Herboline flag, same sachet-first shelf — convert Hair-van reach into incremental basket. The move is a trailer: get Dermoviva + Gulabari onto priced local shelves (catalogue-present, shelf-absent today); lead Dermoviva (occlusive substitution) with Gulabari rose-ritual as trial and Dermoviva Baby as white-space; enter HQ/mercury/steroid-free by design (the trust wedge vs the adulterated Merkato trade); own "የተፈጥሮ ብርሃን" (natural radiance) in real Amharic + Afaan Oromoo; and commit a local-make path. Do not lead skin, do not touch the whitening claim, and handle Fem "bleach" with tongs. The LEAP lesson (C13): Vaseline's "even tone" (31/40) proves the safe pole — Dabur out-authenticates it with real botanicals. Reputation Bet (C18): by 31 Dec 2027, an EFDA/UNEP mercury-lightening action tightens, and a natural, halal, "even-tone-not-bleach" Dermoviva positioning — launched as Hair's trailer, priced local-make or via the rose-water 3307 lane — out-performs any skin-led or whitening-adjacent entry on trial and trade acceptance.

C2 — Category frame, size & the two poles

Ethiopia's skin market is an occlusive mass + a toxic whitening pool, with thin modern-facial penetration. True branded formal spend per head is single-digit USD (personal care ~US$19/person; the Statista ~US$1.8bn skin figure looks implausibly large vs income and likely captures whole-body/informal — directional only). The 2024 float ~doubled import cost, pushing the whole category toward local-make economics.

C5 — Whitespace & the strategic bet (DEEP THINK)

Two poles, one hole. Occlusive mass (Vaseline/Nivea): petroleum-based, imported, float-exposed. Whitening tier (Glow&Lovely/ Caro): toxic, grey, a regulatory time-bomb. Imported premium (CeraVe/K-beauty): price-locked, no herbal/halal story. Local makers: birr-priced but no brand or natural narrative. The hole is the natural-herbal, halal, value-to-mid, even-tone (never-bleach), eventually-local-made skin house — currently unoccupied.

C14 — Cost-to-profit value-chain waterfall (Module 7 — the excise wall, local-make & the 3307 arbitrage)

The economic spine — and the heaviest tax in the portfolio. Cosmetics (HS 3303/3304/3305) carry a 100% federal EXCISE (Excise Proc. 1186/2020, "except sunscreen & shampoo"; unchanged by the 2023 amendment), stacking Duty → Excise 100% → VAT 15% → Surtax 10% to a ~200–240% landed tax on CIF. This makes imported finished skin care structurally uncompetitive and local-make the base case — with two levers: the rose-water HS 3307 lane (escapes the 100% excise) and the lower excise base of local manufacture.

So what: the 100% cosmetics excise makes imported finished 3304 skin care uncompetitive (retails >5× FOB, undercut by grey) — so local toll-manufacture is the base case, not an option (Mekab/Repi/Cosmar partners; ~25–35% cheaper, from avoiding 35% duty on finished value + a lower excise base + no surtax; note the excise also applies domestically, so the advantage is real but bounded). And rose water (HS 3307) escapes the 100% excise entirely — making Gulabari the single most tax-efficient imported SKU and a deliberate product-mix lever (confirm the 3307 vs 3304 classification before locking the line plan). Sun care is likewise excise-exempt. The FX parallel gap (~+18%) applies to every CIF/raw-material line; local-make shrinks the FX-exposed base to inputs only.
