## 2. Deck-specific conformance

- **The strategic spine:** Oral = **BET**, "the mefakia in a tube." The incumbent isn't Colgate — it's a **free twig that is botanically Miswak**
  (mefakia = *Salvadora persica*, the exact plant of Dabur Meswak). Only ~12% brush → **penetration-build (convert stick→tube), not share-steal**;
  peer-reviewed local evidence ("mefakia works") lets Dabur **ratify, not insult, the stick and upgrade the ritual**. Lowest-friction entry
  narrative in the programme.
- **The fluoride switch (regulatory):** a **fluoride-free herbal paste stays in the fast EFDA cosmetic notification lane** (and is exactly what
  Alfarag already sells); fluoride ≥1500 ppm / anti-caries tips into the heavy therapeutic lane. Enter herbal-light, bridge fluoride later — the
  regulatory ease and consumer sweet-spot coincide.
- **Macro correction incorporated:** Ethiopia is **not** 100% import-dependent — **Unilever makes Signal locally at Dukem (2019)**; locally-made
  Signal ~ETB 65 vs imported herbal ~230–266 (3–4×). This **strengthens** the local-make case (proven on ET soil) and reframes it as "join the
  localisers." Two-scenario waterfall shows **local-make ~50–60% cheaper**, escapes excise/FX, import-substitution-aligned; Festac (Nigeria) +
  Dukem are the templates.
- **Depth finding (the crux):** Dabur Herb'l is locally listed (DeliverAddis ~800 / Jiji ~300), **but the halal hero Miswak and Ayurvedic Red are
  grey/cross-border only** — the two SKUs Dabur is built to win with are invisible to a non-importing shopper. The fixable right-to-win gap =
  **local listing + priced availability of Miswak/Red**, not permission (Alfarag has it).
- **LEAP lesson named:** "Nature's own toothbrush" (28/40) is a strong idea but **Presence is the gap** — English/Arabic only, invisible in
  Amharic. The highest-ROI fix is the mefakia-continuity line in **real Amharic + Afaan Oromoo**.
- **Festac school-CSR clone** = the category-and-brand habit-formation flywheel for a 12%-brushing market — the most defensible first move.
