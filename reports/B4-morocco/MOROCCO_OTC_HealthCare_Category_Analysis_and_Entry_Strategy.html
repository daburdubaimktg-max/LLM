C1 — Category verdict & thesis

Verdict: BUILD — "the pharmacy is a jail; the kitchen is a market." Morocco's OTC category is split by law into two economies, and the split IS the strategy. Lane A (médicament): pharmacy-monopoly, state-fixed PPV — Toplexil 150ml at 14.70 MAD nationally, local champions at 13.80–21, even Strepsils capped at 30–66 (Décret 2-13-852; med.ma/Sahha 2026). No brand builds a P&L in that jail, and the 2025 price-reform direction is further CUTS. Lane B (complément alimentaire): a 2–3 billion MAD, largely informal market (Medias24/Consonews Apr 2026) with pricing freedom, multi-channel access (paras, GMS, hanout, online — legal today), and a proven 7–10× price umbrella: Puressentiel honey-plant cough syrup 125ml at 105 MAD, Dietaroma propolis-honey-thyme at 160 — versus Toplexil's 14.70. The market has even priced the honey premium INSIDE the jail: Strepsils Miel & Citron carries a 2× PPV premium over Strepsils Original (61.70–66 vs 30). Dabur's entry writes itself: Honitus enters as a certified honey-herb complément at 89–119 MAD — the grandmother's عسل و زعتر و لويزة in lab-dosed format, priced under the French naturals — and Hajmola enters at the hanout as the candy that names the occasion nobody branded: الثقلة, the Friday-couscous heaviness, against an incumbent that is a ritual (mint tea), not a brand. The binding discipline: nothing ever registers as a drug — the day a Dabur SKU takes a PPV, its economics die.

Two structural warnings shape everything downstream. First, the supply lane switches: unlike hair and oral, Dabur Egypt does NOT manufacture the healthcare portfolio (its 10th-of-Ramadan plants make hair/skin, expanding to baby/oral — Zawya 2022) — Honitus and Hajmola ship from India/UAE, so the 0% door is RAK-GAFTA, not Egypt-Agadir [!] re-validate Honitus/Hajmola origin with Dabur International, with Moroccan white-label tolling (Sembaco, Cosmetiklab — IMANOR-certified) as the long-run hedge. Second, the lane itself is under political attack: the USFP parliamentary group tabled a bill (Apr 2026) to restrict supplement sales to pharmacies only — not passed, pharmacy-lobby-driven, and the single most important kill-switch this deck monitors (strategist read).

Against this stands the category's honest ceiling: out-of-pocket health spend is discretionary in a value-seeking market, and the 99-MAD syrup is a considered purchase — the volume engine is the 2–3 MAD coin unit, exactly as the format law predicts.

C5 — Positioning space & the open territory

Map the space on two axes: legal lane (PPV drug ↔ free supplement/candy) and cultural register (French-science ↔ Moroccan- kitchen). Three quadrants are occupied: PPV×pharma (Sanofi, locals — a jail with tenants), free×French-science (Forté Pharma, Arko — the para shelf), PPV×honey (Strepsils Miel — premium proven but capped). The fourth quadrant — free lane × kitchen register — is empty: no brand sells the Moroccan grandmother's own pharmacopoeia (honey, zaatar, louiza, mint, ginger) in certified, dosed, halal-marked formats at accessible-premium prices, in Darija. That quadrant is Dabur's natural home — it is the same seam the hair deck found (system-at-mass-price) and the oral deck found (heritage at family value), expressed in health: the kitchen canon, formatted. The French naturals validated the price roof; the canon supplies the story; the complément lane supplies the legal door; the hanout supplies the second front no para brand can reach (strategist read).

Why two fronts, not one. The para front (Honitus at 99) and the hanout front (Hajmola at 2–3) are not a portfolio accident — they are mutually insuring. The para front carries margin (33% contribution) but is exposed to the USFP bill and to French-naturals price response; the hanout front carries velocity and political immunity (candy is not capturable by a pharmacy-monopoly bill — Halls is the standing proof) but needs the brand legitimacy the para front builds. If the bill passes, Honitus survives INSIDE the pharmacy at its same price with pharmacist-ally margins; if the French naturals price-war, Hajmola's velocity P&L is untouched. One brand story — the kitchen canon, formatted — runs both fronts with different formats, which is precisely the architecture the incumbents cannot copy: Sanofi cannot leave the jail, Forté cannot reach the hanout, Halls has no health story, and the attar has no certificates. The deck's verdict is BUILD rather than WEDGE because nothing here displaces an incumbent — both positions are constructed on empty ground (the kitchen quadrant, the الثقلة occasion), and construction, not conquest, is the risk profile the P&L prices (strategist read, opinion).

The waterfall is in C11 (T13): RAK-GAFTA landed ~22 on a 99 shelf → ~33% brand contribution — the richest margin structure of the four Morocco decks, because the umbrella (not the duty lane) does the work: even the India-origin 30% MFN downside only cuts contribution to ~26%, making OTC the least duty-sensitive category in the set (hair/oral live at 38-MAD shelves where duty is decisive; Honitus lives at 99 where story is decisive). The umbrella's anatomy, and why it holds. The 7–10× gap between Toplexil and the French naturals is not an accident waiting for arbitrage — it is two different products in the shopper's mind: the PPV syrup is "the medicine the pharmacist hands me" (functional, state-priced, trust delegated to the system), while the 105–160 syrup is "the natural care I choose for my family" (elective, story-priced, trust earned by the brand). Price alone cannot collapse that gap; only a brand holding BOTH trust codes could — which is why Honitus's proposed 99 sits deliberately just under the naturals' floor rather than splitting the difference at, say, 59: at 59 it would read as neither medicine nor premium care, the dead zone where neither trust code applies. The corollary discipline: Honitus's creative never disparages the 14.70 syrup (the pharmacist is an ally, and the medicine works) — it claims the DIFFERENT occasion: the nightly family care and the first-sniffle response that precede the pharmacy visit. The two lanes are complements in the household's own sequence, and the brand that names that sequence honestly wins both trust codes over time (strategist read, opinion).
