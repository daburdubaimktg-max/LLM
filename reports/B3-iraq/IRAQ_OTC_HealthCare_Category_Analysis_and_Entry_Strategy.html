THE ASK: OTC is a BUILD, not a hero — but a build with a genuine wedge. Iraq is one of MENA's most self-medicating, pharmacy-first markets (self-medication ~63–92% in cited studies; the pharmacist (صيدلاني), not the doctor, is the decision-maker — 76%+ see self-medicating patients "often/very often"). The OTC map has two poles and a hole: an MNC analgesic/cough/antacid mainstream (Panadol بنادول = ~72% of pharmacist analgesic recs; Strepsils, Gaviscon) and an Iranian/local herbal floor (Barij Essence باريج, Dineh, state SDI Samarra, the عطّار herbalist trade, Hemani black-seed) — culturally close but QC-thin and un-trusted. Dabur's wedge = the trusted, halal, herbal-credible value-to-mid health house — the QC/branding rigour of the MNCs with the natural/faith grammar of the floor. The winning lane is the immunity + digestive + rehydration triad (Dabur Honey, Chyawanprash, Hajmola/Pudin Hara, glucose/ORS) — the fast-growing tail (vitamin imports +107% YoY 2024) where MNCs are weak and the Iranian floor is un-trusted — not a head-to-head analgesic war vs Panadol, nor a price war vs state SDI. Two structural keys: (1) win the classification game — engineer claims so hero SKUs enter via the fast food/supplement/cosmetic lane (importer-controlled, 30–50% retail margin), reserving the 12–24-month MoH/Kimadia medicine lane for the few genuine OTC-drug plays; (2) own the Arbaeen (أربعين) health-kit — the ~21.5M-pilgrim, 80-km-on-foot annual endurance moment (foot-balm, ORS, honey, tonic) that no competitor owns. Category verdict (macro T17): BUILD. Halal is a positive/baseline (China-flip). The e-com finding is stark: Dabur's OTC core is absent from every organized Iraqi platform — pure greenfield [!] confirm.

C1 — The ask (executive summary)

Macro anchor (§M8, §M11, T17): OTC is ranked BUILD — a patient, portfolio, pharmacist-led compounding play, not a quick hero. Enter via the food/supplement lane; queue medicated SKUs in parallel; served two-door from RAK (Umm Qasr / federal) and Turkey (Ibrahim Khalil / KRG). Halal baseline + COSQC CoC + FX + bureau selection are the gates.

The play in one screen. Four truths set the OTC strategy. First, the pharmacist is the market: with weak GP gatekeeping and self-medication running 63–92% in cited studies, the صيدلية counter — not a TV GRP or a doctor's script — converts trial, so the entire commercial model is pharmacist margin + education + trust seals. Second, the demand is bifurcated and Dabur should pick the tail, not the trunk: MNCs own the analgesic/cough/antacid trunk (Panadol alone ≈72% of pharmacist analgesic recommendations), while the fast-growing immunity / vitamin / herbal tail (vitamin imports +107% YoY 2024) is contested only by an un-trusted Iranian/local herbal floor — that tail is Dabur's lane. Third, regulation is a portfolio design problem, not a barrier: the classification fork (medicine vs supplement vs cosmetic) differs ~10× on time and control, so we claim-engineer at source — Honey, Chyawanprash, Hajmola and glucose enter as food/supplement in months; Honitus, Gripe Water and the analgesic balm queue on the slower medicated track in parallel, never gating the launch. Fourth, Iraq hands Dabur two unique cultural on-ramps: the honey + black-seed (حبة البركة) Prophetic-medicine grammar that pre-sells a herbal immunity house, and the Arbaeen pilgrimage — 21.5M people walking 80km, a tailor-made annual health-kit activation (foot-balm, ORS/glucose, honey, tonic) no MNC or Iranian brand owns, run under strict Ashura tone discipline (somber/CSR, never a festive sale). The move is a build: appoint a compliant scientific bureau (the single highest-leverage decision — it holds the registration, the MoH relationship, the margin and de-facto brand ownership); lead with the food/supplement triad; win the pharmacist counter; own the Arbaeen kit; and price the value-herbal middle — above the Iranian floor, below the MNC mainstream. Reputation Bet (C18): by 31 Dec 2028, ≥3 Dabur OTC SKUs (Honey/Chyawanprash/Hajmola-class) are MoH-registered and reordering through pharmacy in ≥3 governorates, an Arbaeen health-kit CSR activation has run at least once, and ≥1 medicated SKU (Honitus-class) has entered the MoH dossier queue — or Dabur's herbal health portfolio stays a diaspora/grey curiosity while the Iranian floor keeps the tail.

C2.2 — The lane fork (the single most important design choice)

Every SKU falls into one of three regulatory lanes that differ ~10× on time, cost and control. Claim-engineering at source — what the pack says it is — decides the lane, the speed, and who holds the margin.

Never wait for the drug registrations to start selling. This is the deck's load-bearing move.

C3 — Competition & brand-by-brand teardowns (Module 1)

The floor has three tiers. MNC OTC mainstream: Haleon Panadol (بنادول) — the paracetamol category-generic; Strepsils (سترپسلز), Otrivin; Reckitt Gaviscon (جافيسكون), Eno; Sanofi; Bayer — trusted, efficacious, foreign, premium, zero heritage story. Regional/Iranian herbal floor: Barij Essence (باريج اسانس) (Kashan, est. 1992, ~200 herbal SKUs), Dineh, Goldaru, Ghaem Darou — cheap, culturally proximate via the Shia-south land trade, but QC-thin. Local Iraqi: state SDI Samarra (أدوية سامراء) — 135 products, Kimadia-tender-dominant, patriotic, low price; Pioneer, Asia Pharma, Ninevah, distributed through scientific bureaus. Prophetic-medicine adjacency: Pakistan's Hemani (halal/ESMA, black-seed + honey) already farms the exact faith-coded wellness turf Dabur would enter; India's Zecuf herbal cough syrup lists an Iraq dealer — proof Indian herbal OTC has a beachhead to leapfrog.

C5 — Whitespace & the strategic bet (DEEP THINK)
