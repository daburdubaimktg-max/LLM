## 2. Deck-specific conformance

- **The strategic spine:** OTC = **BUILD**, a patient two-speed regulatory greenfield. Iraq is one of MENA's most self-medicating,
  **pharmacy-first** markets (self-med 63–92%; pharmacist = decision-maker, 76%+ see self-medicating patients often). The map has
  two poles + a hole: MNC analgesic/cough/antacid mainstream (Panadol ≈72% of analgesic recs) and the Iranian/local herbal floor
  (Barij, SDI, عطّار, Hemani) — culturally close but QC-thin. **Dabur's wedge = the trusted, halal, herbal-credible value-to-mid
  health house.**
- **Pick the tail, not the trunk:** own the **immunity + digestive + rehydration triad** (Honey, Chyawanprash, Hajmola, glucose/ORS)
  — the fast-growing tail (vitamin imports **+107% YoY 2024**) where MNCs are weak and the floor is un-trusted; **never** a
  head-to-head analgesic war vs Panadol nor a price war vs state SDI.
- **The classification game is the whole strategy:** claim-engineer hero SKUs into the fast **food/supplement/cosmetic** lane
  (importer-controlled, 30–50% retail margin); reserve the 12–24-month **MoH/Kimadia medicine** lane for genuine OTC-drug plays;
  queue Honitus/Gripe Water in parallel from day one — never gate the launch. Claim/lane firewall threaded through C8/C13/C18.
- **Own the Arbaeen (أربعين) health-kit:** the ~21.5M-pilgrim, 80-km-on-foot annual endurance moment (foot-balm + ORS + honey +
  tonic) that no MNC or Iranian brand owns — the single most defensible first move, under strict Ashura tone (service/CSR, never sale).
- **Two-door supply re-derived:** RAK→Umm Qasr (federal/south), Turkey→Ibrahim Khalil (KMCA/north); treat KRG as a separate country
  to dodge the internal double-clearance trap. FX parallel gap (~11–17%) is the larger, more persistent cost line than the tariff.
- **LEAP lesson named:** Panadol «نسندك في أهم اللحظات» (31/40) proves the category sells **presence in the family's important
  moments, not the molecule** — Dabur out-flanks on authenticity of care (natural + halal + heritage).
- **Bureau selection = the entry decision** (holds registration + MoH relationship + margin + de-facto brand ownership).
