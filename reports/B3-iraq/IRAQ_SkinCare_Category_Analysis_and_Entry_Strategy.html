C1 — The ask (executive summary)

Macro anchor (§M8, §M11, T17): Skin is ranked BUILD — a patient, ritual-led, salon-anchored compounding play. Enter cosmetic-lane (COSQC CoC), halal, value-to-mid; served two-door from RAK (Umm Qasr / federal) and Turkey (Ibrahim Khalil / KRG). Halal baseline + cosmetic CoC + whitening-ingredient discipline + FX + bureau selection are the gates.

The play in one screen. Four truths shape the skin strategy. First, the category has already forked: the mass shelf splits cleanly into a bleach lane (تبييض/تفتيح — Pond's White Beauty, Glow&Lovely, whitening serums: crowded, price-warred, ethics-and-regulatory-exposed) and a glow lane (نضارة/إشراقة — Nivea even-tone, Vaseline radiant, K-beauty: rising, aspirational, clean-conscience). Even Unilever renamed Fair&Lovely → Glow&Lovely to survive the backlash. Dabur wins by owning the glow lane with a real hero ingredient, not by joining the bleach fight. Second, rose water is the wedge: ماء الورد is a genuine, pre-sold Iraqi ritual (daily toning, bridal prep, hammam, even culinary-crossover trust), Gulabari has authentic Damask-rose provenance and Gulf distribution — yet in Iraq the branded, packaged "clean rose-water toner" shelf is empty, held only by Nivea's Aqua Rose mask and fragmented Iranian rose water. Third, the salon is the market-maker: Iraqi women are heavy grooming users, the bridal (عروس) economy is high-ticket and multi-day (henna night), and the salon/bridal tier carries both the fattest margin (50–100%+) and the endorsement power — so the go-to-market is salon-and-bridal-led, not pharmacy-price-led. Fourth, the binding constraints are trust and FX, not shelf price: a compliant CoC unit sits price-disadvantaged against grey Turkish/Iranian stock, so Dabur defends with authenticity (Arabic label, halal, "no mercury / no hydroquinone"), salon advocacy, and authenticated Instagram-COD supply — turning the counterfeit-ridden whitening adjacency into a trust asset. The move is a build: appoint a compliant cosmetic bureau; lead with Gulabari rose water as the natural-glow hero; win the salon/bridal artist; seed Instagram/TikTok; price value-to-mid (above artisanal, below MNC premium). Reputation Bet (C18): by 30 Jun 2028, Gulabari-led rose-water/natural-toner SKUs hold real shelf + salon presence in ≥2 of {Baghdad, Erbil, Basra}, cleared as cosmetics with halal + "no mercury/HQ" trust marks, sold on نضارة (glow), never تبييض (bleach) — or Dabur's rose equity stays a diaspora curiosity while Nivea and the Iranian rose trade keep the lane.

C5 — Whitespace & the strategic bet (DEEP THINK)

The map has two crowded poles and an empty middle. Pole A — MNC mass + whitening giants: trust and reach, but foreign, whitening-tainted, no authentic herbal. Pole B — Iranian informal + local artisanal: authentic and cheap, but unbranded, inconsistent, non-compliant, no QC. The hole is the branded, halal, QC'd, value-to-mid rose-water "natural glow" house — the authenticity of B with the trust/branding/CoC rigour of A.

DEEP THINK — why the middle, not the poles. Dabur cannot out-media Nivea/L'Oréal on the whitening front nor out-cheap the grey Iranian rose trade; both poles are structurally defended. But neither pole can hold "branded and authentic and halal and QC'd rose-water glow" — the MNCs have no hero ingredient, the informal trade has no brand or compliance. Strategist read (opinion): the durable win is ownership of the authentic-natural-glow identity, industrialising the rose ritual with brand trust and compounding it through the salon/bridal advocate — an annuity the poles' business models forbid them from contesting.
