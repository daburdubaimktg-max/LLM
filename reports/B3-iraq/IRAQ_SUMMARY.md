## The country thesis in one line
Iraq is a **regional-brand activation, not a cold entry and not a plant build** — Dabur's Vatika/Amla/**Meswak** are already
established Arabic-script "Arab-market" brands (Meswak = the Arab world's #1 oral brand) but reach Iraq only via
grey/traditional-trade/OpenSooq and are **absent from organized retail**. The work is **distributor/bureau appointment + equity
conversion**, gated by **FX (dollarized dinar + parallel gap), COSQC CoC + Arabic/Sorani labelling, and a three-market split**
(Shia south / Sunni center / KRG-Sorani). Halal is a **baseline positive** (the biggest China-flip); India origin is
**neutral-to-asset** ("the regional Vatika/Amla you know," #1 pharma supplier, medical-tourism trust).

## Cross-category re-derived flips (no China transplant)
1. **Regional-brand activation**, not entry or local-make — the third distinct archetype in the programme (vs Kenya cold-entry,
   Nigeria dormant-factory).
2. **Halal = baseline positive/expected** across all four categories (the biggest China-flip) — certify, don't bolt on.
3. **Three markets, dual language** — Shia south / Sunni center / KRG-Sorani; separate customs (Umm Qasr federal vs Ibrahim
   Khalil/Turkey KRG), calendar (Arbaeen, Nowruz), and creative (Sorani).
4. **Demand engine = pharmacist/salon + social-selling + COD**, not shelf/broadcast; <20% banked.
5. **FX, not tariff, is the cost wall** — no VAT, but dollarized dinar + parallel gap (~11–18%) + sanction/banking chokepoints;
   price at parallel. Cosmetics carry a high Decision-957 duty band (~20–30%); supplements ~5%.
6. **India origin neutral-to-asset**; deep cultural right-to-win (miswak, henna, black-seed, rose-water, olive native).
7. **The claim/lane firewall** is decisive across categories — cosmetic vs medicated (Dabur Red, Honitus, anti-acne) and glow
   vs bleach (تبييض kill-switch) decide both regulatory speed and reputational risk.

## Unique Iraq activation asset

activation (foot-balm, ORS/glucose, honey, tonic) that **no MNC or regional brand owns**, under strict Ashura tone discipline
(service/CSR, never a festive sale). The single most defensible first move in OTC, and a halo for the whole portfolio.
