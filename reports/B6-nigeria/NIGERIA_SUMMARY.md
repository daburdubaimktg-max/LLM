## The one-line thesis
**Nigeria is not a market entry — it is a ~20-year manufacturing incumbency to re-accelerate.** Dabur runs
African Consumer Care Ltd with a herbal-toothpaste factory in Festac, Lagos; the FX crisis that broke GSK,
Haleon and Unilever-homecare (all retreated to third-party distribution in 2023) *rewarded* the Indian-origin
localizers who make locally — Dabur, Godrej, Tolaram. Dabur is already on the winning side of that pattern;
what is missing is coherence and force, not a beachhead. The recurring right-to-win across all four categories
is the same: **use the Festac local-make cost engine to collapse an import price wall, own the herbal/Ayurvedic
territory no incumbent credibly holds, and win the halal North.**

## The China-flips re-derived for Nigeria (never transplanted)
1. **Not entry — re-acceleration** of a manufacturing incumbent (unique in the programme).
2. **Entry lane = local make, already built** — FX eased to ~2–3% by 2026 but the naira (−65% since 2023) makes local production about cost, not availability; Festac already answers it. Retail-pack soap is import-*banned*.
3. **Halal flips POSITIVE/near-mandatory** in the 12 sharia states — the mirror of the China no; Miswak (oral), chebe/henna + halal (hair/skin), halal wellness (OTC).
4. **India origin = neutral-to-asset** via the industrial diaspora (Indomie/Tolaram, 200+ Indian firms, 2nd-largest employer) — firewalled against a counterfeit-Indian-drug meme by "Made in Festac + NAFDAC + herbal."
5. **Two-nation GTM** — halal North (Hausa radio, Sallah, Sabon Gari, cash) vs aspirational South (Afrobeats/owambe, fintech, IG/TikTok).
6. **The recurring price-wall correction** — imported Dabur SKUs sit at 2.5–4× local benchmarks (toothpaste ₦3,650 vs Close-Up ₦1,050; Amla ₦16k vs local ₦3–7.5k); the Festac cost engine is the fix across categories.
7. **NAFDAC discipline is the moat** — "claim = classifier" (OTC), bleach-free by design (skin), cosmetic-not-drug claims (hair/oral).

## The single gating unknown (carried to every deck)
The **live scale and capacity of the Festac plant** — deferred to the W0 AFCC audit rather than faked. It gates
all sizing and is the shared falsifier: if the plant is idle or quality-capped, the "already over the wall"
premise collapses and Nigeria reverts to a Kenya-style import-plus-distributor build.

## Portfolio chassis (M11)
One operating chassis serves all four: **the Festac local-make engine** (cost + MANCAP + NAFDAC), **one
two-nation GTM** (halal North / aspirational South), **one distribution spine** (Onitsha/Kano/Balogun wholesale
→ PMV/duka + the formalising CWC layer, plus the Odomos health-halo and, for hair, the salon/braider engine),
**one equity thread** (trusted, standardised, NAFDAC-clean *herbal* — the territory no incumbent owns), and
**one FX posture** (naira-price via local blend, never imported premium). Oral leads and funds the chassis;
OTC and Hair build on it; Skin rides as a passenger.
