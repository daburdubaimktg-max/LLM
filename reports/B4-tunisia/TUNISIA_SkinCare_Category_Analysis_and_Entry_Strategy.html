C1 — Category verdict & thesis

Verdict: FLANK-NARROW — "send the jar, keep the heroes home." This is the deck where Tunisia says no, and the strategy is to listen. Three of the four SKU families that led Dabur's skin entries in Morocco and Algeria FAIL the Tunisian evidence test. Fem depilatory fails on price structure: Veet is not exiled here — it is saturated (GMS + the whole para mesh, Nair alongside) at 9.99 TND in Aziza and 14.8 at Carrefour/Géant, a fraction of Algeria's 2,700–4,500 DZD umbrella, and beneath it sits halawa, the living sugar-paste tradition with its own salons and training academies — there is no arbitrage left to flank. Gulabari fails on heritage ownership: rose water is a LOCAL category with four-plus named Tunisian brands at 8–12 TND (Bio Orient 12.05, Naturbio 8.00) and, above it, the prickly-pear-seed-oil tier — Tunisia's own liquid gold at 1,800 TND/litre wholesale — owns the premium-natural register; an Indian rose water enters that fight as a commodity, not a story. OxyLife fails on regulation and register: the formal para shelf has functionally excluded hydroquinone (zero branded SKUs on any .tn platform, against Morocco's openly-sold HiQuin and Expigment — a structural contrast this research measured directly), brightening runs on EU-permitted actives at clinical prices (SVR Clairial 58–68, Muriac 44.69), and blanchiment is a named, critiqued social phenomenon in Tunisian academic discourse — the deck's glow charter is therefore stricter here than anywhere in B4. What SURVIVES is the humble jar: Herbolene, entering not as Algeria's category creation but as a displacement flank — Tunisia already has a local generic-vaseline pharma segment (Arrazi 30g at 3.489, Cytolnat Hydra Vaseline 100ml at 16.50) and a genuine Unilever Vaseline present ONLY as a 35ml tube (2.263), which leaves the family-size branded jar with a herbal story unoccupied between them. One jar, one coffret rider on the Hammam Laaroussa occasion, and the discipline to launch nothing else: the narrowness is the strategy (strategist read on agent evidence, 7 Jul 2026).

C2 — Market size & growth

The one hard number is sector-level: 1.77 billion TND of cosmetics revenue, 1.6% of GDP, ~20,000 jobs (La Presse, 21 Feb 2025 — a feature whose own headline, «entre domination des monopoles et enjeux internationaux», names the market's concentration problem). The ~40% skincare split (≈708M TND) and the ~8% growth rate come from an unattributed secondary source and are used here as SHAPE, not fact [!] Euromonitor purchase item. What the shelf itself proves: a para-dermo tier deep enough that La Roche-Posay alone lists 101 SKUs on one platform (Pharma-shop.tn) with serums to 168.50 TND; a mass tier where Nivea Creme 150ml spans 7.90–12.65 across channels; and a counterfeit shadow economy large enough that customs seized ~53,000 fake cosmetic units in 2026 to date (18,000+ in January alone [!] TAP primary) and the state relaunched its National Council against Counterfeiting in April 2026. The deposit rule completes the picture: every imported jar now carries 100%-cash-up-front financing, visible on shelf as permanent discount theatre. A market this concentrated, this counterfeit-anxious and this financing-squeezed rewards exactly two things — honest pricing and provable authenticity — which is the same conclusion the OTC deck reached from the honey crisis, arriving here through cosmetics customs data instead (strategist read).

C5 — Positioning space & the open territory

Read as a grid, the shelf leaves exactly one branded cell open. Vertically: the therapeutic ointment register (Arrazi at ~11.6/100g-equivalent, Cytolnat at 16.5/100ml) sits above; the tiny cosmetic tube (Vaseline 35ml at 2.263 ≈ 6.5/100ml) sits below; the general cream (Nivea 7.9–12.65/150ml) sits beside. Horizontally: no product in any of those cells carries a plant story, a family format AND a mass price simultaneously. Herbolene 115–140g at 8–12 TND — aloe and herbs in the jar, winter-family framing, honest list price — occupies the intersection: cheaper per gram than Cytolnat by two-thirds, bigger than the Unilever tube by 3–4×, and differentiated from Arrazi by the one claim (plants) the pharma register never makes. The second open space is not a shelf cell but an OCCASION: Hammam Laaroussa is provisioned piecemeal while a documented coffret-gifting culture runs parallel (Fête des Mères dermo boxes) — a branded hammam coffret that bundles Dabur's jar and oil with LOCALLY-SOURCED savon noir, kessa and rose water (bought from the heritage tier, credited on the box) merges the two without claiming a ritual that is not Dabur's to claim (strategist read).
