C1 — Category verdict & thesis

Verdict: WEDGE — "the word is crowded, the clove is free." Tunisia's oral shelf is the programme's most pre-empted miswak market: where Algeria offered a five-brand siwak field and Morocco a two-siwak heritage, Tunisia presents a THREE-BRAND miswak fight already running at the mass price tier — Colgate Herbal Miswak (official, 100ml, Sangour-listed), Lilas Extreme Miswak Soin Complet (75ml at 2.68–3.28 TND, three-retailer triangulation 7 Jul 2026) pouring from SAH's brand-new 25,000-tonne Azur Cosmetics plant (Zaghouan, onstream end-June 2024, 120M TND, ~1,000 jobs — businessnews 2 Jan 2025), and Dabur Miswak itself, grey-imported in three SKUs at Sangour (Gold 120g+50g, Charbon Actif, Fresh Gel 40g+20g) with echoes at Wamia and the herboristerie tail. Entering "the miswak brand" into that fight would be a me-too against a domestic plant's cost base. The wedge instead has two arms: (1) formalise the grey — Dabur Miswak becomes the official rider on the hair deck's distributor architecture, differentiated on multi-herb complexity and visible grams (120g+50g against incumbents' 75ml), never on out-miswaking Lilas; and (2) lead with the absentee — Dabur Red, clove-forward and confirmed UNDISTRIBUTED in Tunisia, enters the empty 4–11 TND band (the scrape found nothing between Colgate's 3.99 kids' tube and GUM Kids at 11) on the one oral hook Tunisia already owns: قرنفل — the clou de girofle every Maghreb household knows as the toothache remedy. The register is locked secular-natural per the macro flip: the evidence pass found ZERO sunnah framing in any Tunisia-domestic miswak copy — heritage here is an ingredient, not an act of piety (strategist read on agent evidence, 7 Jul 2026).

C5 — Positioning space & the open territory

Map the ladder and the strategy writes itself. The mass floor (2.20–3.99) is a two-local war with Colgate riding shotgun — Signal's 47-year plant against Lilas's brand-new one, with the miswak word now planted at 2.68 by the domestic and at an uncaptured price by the American. The credential ceiling (11–20) is European, pharmacist-counselled and discount-free. Between 4 and 11 TND the adult shelf is empty — the scrape's only tenant is a kids' SKU at the top edge (GUM Kids, 11). That vacancy is not an accident of sampling: the locals price at their cost base, the Europeans price at their credential, and nobody has built a brand whose story JUSTIFIES the middle. Dabur Red's clove-forward, 13-herb formula is precisely such a story — a remedy register (not a piety register, not a chemistry register) that the household already believes, priced at 6.5–9 where it reads as "serious but mine." The grey Miswak line rides separately at the mass tier's top edge (3.5–4.5, grams-led), holding Dabur's flag in the miswak fight without pretending to win it (strategist read).
