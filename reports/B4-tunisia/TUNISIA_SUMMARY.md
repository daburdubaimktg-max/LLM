## Cross-cutting Tunisia findings (portable to Libya and beyond)

1. **The failed-entry archive is a strategy document.** Dabur's own 2013–2017 Enfidha venture — and its still-open liquidation — did more
   to define the entry mode than any market datum. Check the group's own corporate history FIRST in every remaining market; the cheapest
   research is the company's filings.
2. **Financing rules now beat tariff rules.** The BCT deposit list taxed every import twice while the Agadir lane sat at 0% — and its
   shelf signature (universal was/now theatre) handed an honest-list entrant free differentiation. Read the central bank before the
   customs code; Libya's FX architecture will decide more than its tariff book.
3. **Proof is a product feature, not a promotion.** The certificate-as-COGS doctrine (honey QR, batch traceability, skin authenticity
   architecture) converts a 77%-conscious-fakes market from threat to moat — and the state's own enforcement wave (seizures, bans, the
   relaunched anti-counterfeit council) blows at the compliant entrant's back.
4. **The anti-transplant method now rejects whole portfolios, not just concepts.** Tunisia killed Morocco's Fem storefront play AND
   Algeria's Fem ¼-price flank AND Algeria's Herbolene category-creation thesis (re-derived to displacement) on named shelf prices —
   while re-deriving Algeria's "enter as food" conclusion through entirely different regulatory machinery. Same method, opposite answers,
   both correct: the method is the finding.
5. **Registers bifurcate — check the shelf against the street.** Vernacular says tfal, the shelf sells «Ghassoul»; the pious word siwak
   trades in secular-natural copy; the premium local oil is named "the Indian one." Every naming rule needs both a street test and a
   shelf test before it hardens into doctrine.
6. **Crises are briefs with dates.** The 6-Jul-2026 honey exposé (published the day before the OTC deck) converted directly into entry
   architecture — but only because the origin liability (India named) was confronted with judo rather than denial. Monitor the press
   DURING research, not just as background.
7. **Restraint is falsifiable.** The skin bet's discipline clause (any hero SKU appearing without an evidence-change memo breaks the
   bet) turns "we chose not to launch" from a footnote into an auditable commitment — the governance answer to the perennial
   "just list it, the truck already runs" pressure.
