C1 — Category verdict & thesis

Verdict: BUILD — "govern the grey, claim the olive, ride the curls." Three findings set the play. (1) The footprint correction: the macro's "zero commercial presence" is amended — Vatika is retail-live across 6+ Tunisian e-tailers, including a dedicated Géant Drive brand page (15+ SKU depth: oils across argan/onion/cress/cactus/coconut/olive/garlic/almond/black-seed, creams at 9.75–10.75 TND, a 400ml anti-chute shampoo) plus Sangour, Drest (oils 11.20–14.00), La Mode, Wildkard, Wamia — all grey-import, no official distributor found, no pricing control. Real organic pull exists (plausibly diaspora-fed estimate based on French ethnic-retail adjacency); the entry is a governance-and-extension job on Morocco's pattern, not Algeria's blank page. (2) The olive space is open at the top: Tunisia's own olive-cosmetics champion-in-waiting (Oliss/CHO — the Terra Delyssa group!) prices its olive shampoo at a VALUE 5.59 TND, leaving the heritage-premium cell unclaimed; the risk is commoditisation (the kitchen bottle is free), so Vatika's olive SKU re-anchors on a BENEFIT hook — the concentrated, hammam-ready, tfal-compatible format — never the ingredient claim alone. (3) The curl movement is mature and mid-priced: Kamaana (2021, 35–42% annual growth) and Zynia (packs at 46–66.50 TND) built a masstige curl band on a community infrastructure that predates them (Tunisian Big Hair, ~51k, since 2015) — Vatika's curl-compatible formats enter UNDER that band (12–28 TND) with movement-respect rules, growing the category the pioneers educated. All of it rides the macro's architecture: Gate Zero first, Agadir lane, small-lot cadence, French-forward register, halal pocketed.

One strategic surprise sharpens the whole deck: Dabur is not even the only Indian ayurvedic house already trading here — Trichup (Vasu Healthcare) runs a full range through Alwosta.tn, a specialist natural/ayurvedic e-tailer. The lesson cuts two ways: the Indian-herbal register has proven Tunisian demand beyond Dabur's own grey flow; and the specialist-channel route Trichup chose is exactly the niche trap Vatika's mainstream ambition must avoid — the research shows local challengers (Lilas, Sensea, Oliss, Zynia) blanket para AND GMS simultaneously, and the deck follows their map, correcting the macro's para-first default for mass-positioned SKUs (strategist read).
