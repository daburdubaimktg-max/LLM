THE DECK IN ONE PARAGRAPH: Italian oral care (€732.5M, 2023) is oligopolistic at the top (Mentadent 25%, Colgate 21%, AZ 16% — the top eight hold ~90%) and UNIQUE at its middle: Antica Erboristeria holds 8.7% on a pure natural-herbal story, Pasta del Capitano has held ~5.5% on 120 years of heritage, and Marvis turned toothpaste into Florentine giftware — no other market in this directive structurally rewards story-led oral brands like this one. The corridor diagnostic returns its fourteenth finding: CEILING-PROVEN — the natural seat is not empty (UK), rail-bound (France) or rule-walled (Germany); it is OCCUPIED BY A LIVING WINNER whose share MEASURES the demand, and the seat beside it — the second tradition's — is open. Antica Erboristeria is Italian herbalism's paste; Dabur Red/Meswak/Neem is Ayurveda's, a different tradition with its own botany, its own story and its own diaspora floor. And Dabur arrives with the directive's most channel-diversified category cell already trading: 8–10 Amazon.it SKUs at 4.2–4.6★ (the EU's warmest oral corpus), the Concessionaria pharmacy layer across 8+ e-commerce sites, Shankara's specialist shelf, and eBay's halal bulk packs — plus the "sold by Dabur Europe" trace that makes this deck the mandate question's sharpest test. One number needs surgery first: the grey Meswak at ~€9.49/100g is the EU's most overpriced (France pays €5.07/100g, Germany €2.29/100g on the rail) — the governed ladder lands at €4.49–4.99/100g-class [AUTHORED], and the EU price architecture finally gets its third coherent column.

A framing note on this deck's place in the set: Italy's oral deck is the mandate question's proving ground, and its lessons will echo through the OTC deck (the same agent architecture serves both wings) and the eventual Spain set (whose note shows Meswak and Red already on amazon.es — the same wing forming one market over). The deck therefore writes its governance designs (T10d's mandate architecture) at reusable resolution: performance gates, parallel channels, optioned physical scope, drafted exits. What Italy's oral wing pioneers, the directive's remaining formal-channel encounters inherit.

C4 · THE CORRIDOR DIAGNOSTIC — FOURTEENTH FINDING: CEILING-PROVEN

The UK oral corridor offered a seat beside a DORMANT comp (Euthymol — proof by fossil); Italy offers a seat beside a LIVING one. Antica Erboristeria's 8.7% is not a historical curiosity but a current, competitive, multinational-owned (Henkel-heritage) natural franchise — and its share MEASURES what Italian shoppers will give a herbal paste: roughly one lira in eleven. That measurement transforms entry maths: the natural seat's ceiling is not a guess (France's unsized rail) nor a wall (Germany's rule) but a PROVEN number with a living demonstration of how to earn it (story, botany, heritage packaging, GDO+pharmacy dual presence). The open question is the second seat: Italian herbalism's paste exists; Ayurveda's does not — a different tradition, differently botanized (miswak, neem, tulsi vs sage, myrrh, rhatany), with a diaspora floor the incumbent lacks and a claims grammar the Ministry's own supplement guidance already speaks. CEILING-PROVEN corridors prescribe respectful parallel entry: never attack the prover (its success is the category's permission slip), price adjacent, and let the tradition's difference do the differentiation.

The prescription's third clause — let the tradition differentiate — has a practical corollary the playbook enforces: the second tradition must arrive COMPLETE, not SKU by SKU. The prover's bay works because it presents a coherent botanical world; a lone Meswak tube beside it would read as an import, but Red + Meswak + Neem + Basil + Clove arriving as a FAMILY reads as what it is — another tradition's full shelf. The census shows the family already exists in-market across scattered channels; move 4's build assembles it into one visual, one grammar, one bay-ready block. Assortment is the argument.
