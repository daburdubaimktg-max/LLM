A framing paragraph for the set's closer: this deck completes the directive's European skin quartet, and the four decks read together as the programme's single best exhibit of the anti-transplant discipline. A programme that transplanted would have shipped one "global rosewater playbook" four times; this one derived four answers from four market structures and can defend every difference with named evidence. When the board asks what the methodology buys, the rose water quartet is the one-page answer. And the quartet now travels: Spain, Brazil and Mexico each hold their own established rose-water answer, and each will receive its own derivation — the fifth, sixth and seventh movements of a composition whose theme never repeats.

C4 · THE CORRIDOR DIAGNOSTIC — SIXTEENTH FINDING: ICON-ANCHORED

Set the three European rose-water geometries side by side and the diagnostic's discriminating power shows plainly. France's wall was CERTIFICATION — impersonal, institutional, arguable only by paper. Germany's structure was PRICE-BY-SHELF — impersonal, economic, navigable only by tier choice. Italy's anchor is AFFECTION — personal, generational, and untouchable by any argument Dabur could make: Acqua alle Rose is the bottle in the nonna's bathroom, the Carosello jingle's residue, the pharmacy's familiar green. Affection-anchored categories punish challengers uniquely (attacking the icon reads as attacking the memory) and reward ADDERS uniquely (a second story that honours the first inherits its category-warmth). The prescription writes itself as the deck's title: two rose cities. Florence bottled Europe's rose water in 1867; Kannauj has distilled India's for centuries; the pairing frame lets Gulabari borrow the category's affection while claiming only its own address — and the icon's marketers, if they ever notice, can only agree, because the frame compliments them by construction.

The instrument's rule stands: geometries are derived from each market's evidence, never transplanted from Italy's. But the icon-anchored shape's discovery here means the derivations will begin with better questions — is the incumbent loved or merely present? is its material affection, paper or price? — and the pairing prescription waits, tested, if affection answers. The instrument, like every instrument in the kit, earns its keep by asking better first questions — never by pre-writing answers.
