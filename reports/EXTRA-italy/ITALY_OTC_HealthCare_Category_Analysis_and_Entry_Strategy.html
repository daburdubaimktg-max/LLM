The corridor diagnostic returns its fifteenth finding: WHITE-LABEL-HELD — the middle's occupants are anonymous, and anonymity is the one moat the original displaces by existing. The prescription executes the macro's option D: notify the flagship through an Italian OSA, land it in the erboristeria beside the €33–37/500g look-alikes (whose Dabur attribution the census could not even confirm — the look-alike economy is ALREADY monetising the reference product's absence), and let 1884 do what no white-label can answer. The three postures were never a ranking of markets' worth but a ranking of their weather, and Italy's execution will retroactively serve its siblings: the trade-education machinery built here translates to France's herboristeries and (post-key) Germany's Reformhäuser; the erboristeria's reorder data becomes the EU flagship business case's first primary numbers; and the white-label displacement, if it converts as modelled, becomes the template for every market where anonymous supplements harvested Ayurvedic demand first.
