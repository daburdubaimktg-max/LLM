## What Congo-Brazzaville taught the programme (doctrine bank additions)

1. **The plumbing paradox** — currency calm and payment hostility can coexist; financing-beats-tariffs arrives here via
   domiciliation/repatriation mechanics, answered by zero-XAF architecture (prepaid EUR), not by hedging.
2. **The two-column waterfall matured into the batch instrument** — four applications, three different outcomes: hair/oral
   (viable-A / unviable-B → G1 decides), honey (Scenario B KILLS the SKU → pivot pre-designed), soap (viable in BOTH →
   the only lead SKU independent of the customs ruling). The instrument now distinguishes price-point risk from viability risk.
3. **The street-conversion move** — where informal channels sell medicine illegally, food SKUs can be sold by the same
   network legally: the programme's first positive-sum answer to a grey market (OTC deck).
4. **The false-friend series** — bâton de manioc (cassava dish, not chew-stick) and «maquillage» (the bleaching practice,
   not makeup): local lexical review is now a named creative gate, not a nicety.
5. **Measured-absent vs unmeasurable registers** — Miswak PARKS here (register measured and absent) vs Libya where it
   WAITED (register unmeasurable). Different epistemic states take different verbs.
6. **The untouchable-ceiling series grew to five** — la SAPE (accompany at the chair) and nkisi (never borrow
   healer vocabulary) join Kamaana, Jabal Akhdar, hammam/dalka.
7. **Voluntary standard as asset** — where the state has no hydroquinone ban, exceeding the regulatory floor by choice is
   a PR asset — told institutionally, FIREWALLED from consumer ads (never lecture the shoppers).
8. **WhatsApp 60–70% of informal-retail revenue** — independently reproduced in three decks; promoted to programme-grade
   for Congo. Printed-price catalogue frames are the discipline.
