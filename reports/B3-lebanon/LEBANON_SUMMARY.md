## Cross-cutting Lebanon findings (portable to Yemen / B4)

1. **The barbell is the market.** Currency (fresh-dollar vs LBP), not just income, splits the consumer; the mass middle is hollowed; every deck's winning move
   bridges it with affordable small-pack USD pricing. Portable test: in any crisis economy, ask what the *remittance-funded* shopper accepts and the *have-not*
   can reach — the answer is one SKU, not two.
2. **The gray/parallel channel is both signal and poison.** Lebanon oral's six unauthorized Mazen listings prove unpaid demand AND pre-commoditize the brand at
   $1.42. Rule derived: monitor gray stock as a free market test; never launch the gray-anchored SKU; probe with an un-anchored line instead.
3. **Local heroes are untakeable; incumbent MNCs are donatable.** Beesline/Khan Al-Saboun (pride) vs Unilever/P&G/L'Oréal/Beiersdorf (BDS-exposed). Dabur's
   repeatable position: "third brand on the natural shelf" — behind the local heroes on pride, ahead of every Western brand on origin — taking share downward
   from the exposed mass tier, never sideways from the locals.
4. **Register discipline is quantified strategy, not compliance.** The OTC LEAP table prices a religious mis-step at 13 points (34 secular vs 21 hadith-coded)
   in a ⅓-Christian market; the skin deck bans تبييض outright in a glow culture. Creative governance gates belong in the playbooks.
5. **Formats beat heritage where the canon is native.** Dabur cannot out-authenticate the grandmother (honey jars, flower water, loose herbs die) — but wins
   with the same ingredients in formats the canon doesn't make (Honitus sachets, dosed digestives, cosmetic-grade toner as a test). "Front the familiar, carry
   the active" (argan front / amla active) is the same law in hair.
6. **Operations before marketing.** Two of four categories start with distribution repair or route-building (skin's replenishment fix; OTC's droguerie/importer),
   not creative. In a COD, cash-dollar economy the binding constraint is stock and terms — every marketing dollar waits behind availability.
