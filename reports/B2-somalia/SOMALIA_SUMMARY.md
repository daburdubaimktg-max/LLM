## The country thesis in one line
Somalia is a **Gulf-re-export-fed, cash-light mobile-money, halal-total, diaspora-shaped import frontier** — the **maximum contrast with
Ethiopia**: where Ethiopia is an FX-gated, local-make-or-stay-out wall, Somalia **inverts the lane** — a light landed-cost stack (~10% flat
duty, no VAT/excise machinery), no manufacturing base, and the **RAK-UAE hub sitting exactly on the existing >80%-via-Dubai re-export rail**,
so **import is the natural Phase-1 lane and Dabur's RAK plant a structural first-mover advantage**. Demand is **halal-total and
ritual-rich** (caday↔Meswak, qasil↔Vatika, foox/malmal↔traditional medicine, rose water↔Gulabari), settlement is **USD + mobile money**
(EVC Plus/Zaad/Sahal, ~89% penetration) on **hawala trade rails, not LCs**, and the market is a **three-zone, clan-plumbed** structure
(Mogadishu/Bakaara-Hawiye · Hargeisa/Waaheen-Isaaq · Bosaso-Darod) requiring **zone-specific distributors**, not one national agent. India
origin is **neutral-to-positive**; RAK/Dubai-made provenance is an **aspiration asset**; halal is the **maximum China-flip** (a hard no in
China, the total baseline here).

## Cross-category re-derived flips (no China transplant)
1. **Entry lane = Gulf re-export IMPORT, not local-make** — the exact opposite of Ethiopia. The light landed-cost stack + no manufacturing base
   + the RAK hub on the existing Dubai rail make **import the natural Phase-1 lane**; Dabur's RAK plant is a structural first-mover advantage.
2. **India origin = neutral-to-positive; RAK/Dubai-made = aspiration ASSET** (Gulf-halal provenance, not naked Indian Ayurveda).
3. **Demand engine = traditional trade + diaspora + mobile-money (payment, not discovery) + radio/mosque/WoM** — no live-commerce; beauty/skin
   is the exception (intensely social/creator-led on IG/TikTok/FB).
4. **Price = barbell in USD** (sachet mass + remittance/diaspora premium), settled on mobile money (EVC Plus/Zaad/Sahal), not shilling.
5. **Halal = TOTAL non-negotiable baseline — the maximum China-flip.** Single Islamic activation calendar (Ramadan + 2 Eids + weddings) vs
   Ethiopia's dual religious cue.
6. **Three-zone, clan-plumbed distribution** (Hawiye/Bakaara · Isaaq/Waaheen · Darod/Bosaso) with **separate customs/regulatory regimes** (Federal
   / Somaliland / Puntland) — a decisive structural constraint across all four categories: appoint zone-specific distributors, plan three
   regulatory tracks for OTC/cosmetics.
7. **The channel splits by category** — Oral/Hair are dukaan-and-wholesale led; **OTC is pharmacy-led** (pharma wholesalers → pharmacies); **Skin
   is cosmetics-shop-and-salon led**. Adeeg.com is a real but narrow window (honey + personal care; **zero medicinal OTC; no Dabur SKU natively
   listed in any category** — the recurring depth finding: Dabur has **awareness/toehold, not shelf**).
