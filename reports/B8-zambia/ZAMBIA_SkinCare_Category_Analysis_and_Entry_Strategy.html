The consumer decode in one story

A June Saturday in Chilenje. The house wakes cold and dry; after the wash, the mother works a fingertip of jelly over three children's faces and arms — the same jar that did her own hands, the baby's legs last night, and the eldest's scalp before yesterday's school photo. The jar is nearly out. At the kantemba she weighs the note in her hand against the two options that exist: the trusted blue one and a loose scoop from the tub behind the counter. There is no third jar — no herbal one, no aloe one, nothing that says more-than-jelly at a kwacha or two above the blue. On the radio at the neighbour's, the Bemba presenter is reading out something about an oil for the scalp — new this season, from a tree she half-knows — and it occurs to her, in the way shelf decisions actually occur, that if THOSE people made a jar, she'd try it.

That last sentence is the entire deck: the relay's warmth converting a category the wedge never touched, one household's logic ahead of any campaign. The one-house-two-jars mechanic exists because she does; the value-plus cell exists because her note has exactly that much stretch in it; and the third jar's absence is the open cell drawn in T4, standing in a real queue on a real cold morning.

DEEP THINK — the promotion's audit: does Herbolene deserve the lead? The case rests on three legs and the deck kicks each. Leg one — CATEGORY CENTRE: the daily jar is Southern Africa's skin economy; true, but centrality means incumbency density — the throne plus a racing multinational plus a possible local landlord; the lead's cell is open, not empty. Leg two — REGISTER FIT: Movit's expansion proves herbal-PJ sells; true, but proof-of-register is also proof-of-competition — the differentiation must carry (aloe-first, lineage-backed, radio-told), and differentiation against a value rival is won at the shelf, not in a deck. Leg three — RELAY ECONOMICS: the shared route wants a same-price-world skin SKU; true and the strongest leg — a Gulabari-led entry here would demand a build the relay can't fund. Net: the promotion stands, provisionally, with the M9 velocity read as its confirmation hearing and a demotion path written (if Herbolene under-runs, the skin file shrinks to R1-reach economics and waits for the batch's later learning). Kill-test: if the fortnight finds Trade Kings' jar ubiquitous at kantemba prices below viability, the value-plus cell may not exist — the entry re-derives before the manifest, not after.
DEEP STRAT — the one-house-two-jars mechanic. The hair wedge and the skin lead share a truck, a stall, a chair and — decisively — a HOUSEHOLD: the same woman buys the amla oil and the family jar, often in the same week. The mechanic: (1) the wholesale bundle — wedge-oil cases ship with Herbolene cases at terms that make stocking both easier than one; (2) the chair's second jar — the braider starter kit's second wave adds Herbolene (scalp under braids + the client's hands — one chair, two uses); (3) the radio's second sentence — Herbolene's introduction rides the season after the wedge's, from the same presenter voices ("from the people who brought the amla oil" — earned by then); (4) the shelf adjacency — one brand block at the kantemba, two jars deep, the tree on both. Fails if: the jars' price worlds diverge (the artifact's resolution watches this). The refusal inside it: no forced bundling at the consumer end — the household assembles its own pair or it isn't a pair.
