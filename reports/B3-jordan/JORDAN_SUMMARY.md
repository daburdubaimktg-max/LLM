## Cross-cutting Jordan findings (portable to Lebanon / the Levant)

1. **The boycott is a structural tailwind, banked quietly** — India/RAK origin is BDS-clean across all four categories; Western MNCs (Colgate, Nivea, L'Oréal,
   Beiersdorf) and French retail (Carrefour → exited) bleed share to local + boycott-safe brands. Never campaign on origin; let it be a quiet advantage.
   **Nuance:** Jordanian Dead Sea brands (Rivage) *benefit*; only Israeli Ahava is a BDS target — never conflate them.
2. **Tax geography decides category posture.** Toothpaste (HS 3306) = 16% GST only → oral is economically clean. Skincare (HS 3304) carries a **25% non-recoverable
   cosmetics excise** on top of GST → skin is structurally defensive. Confirm HS-line excise scope before any P&L is finalised.
3. **The ritual is always native — brand it, don't invent it.** Hair = حمّام زيت hammam zeit (hot-oil); Oral = سواك siwak (Sunnah); OTC = الطب النبوي tibb nabawi
   (black seed); Skin = ماء الورد rosewater (Damascus rose, UNESCO 2019). Dabur's right-to-win is strongest where its hero SKU's name IS the ritual word
   (Meswak↔siwak; Gulabari↔rosewater).
4. **De-fairness / de-Ayurveda re-derivation held.** Drop "Fairness" (reframe to radiance/إشراقة); never lead "Ayurveda" (reads foreign in a ~97% Muslim market —
   lead natural/tibb-nabawi/Sunnah instead). Halal is total baseline for ingestibles/oral, less decisive in skin (natural + alcohol-free is the sharper trio there).
5. **Warm-start risk inversion.** Because Dabur is already distributed (hair/skin since 1981), the constraint is not access but **activation** — climbing off the
   value grocery shelf into pharmacy/salon/creator tiers (Hair), resolving the importer-of-record (Oral, OTC), and JFDA registration (OTC). The traps are
   complacency and over-reach, not entry.
