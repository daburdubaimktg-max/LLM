South Sudan — Country-Entry Foundation (Macro)

The wave's hardest file, one corridor from Dabur's own rail: 92% poverty, a currency in collapse, a pipeline hostage to someone else's war — and Nairobi, where Dabur East Africa and its Kenyan distributor already sit, three border posts up the road that carries 90% of everything Juba consumes

M1 · THE THESIS. South Sudan is the East wave's inversion of the Seychelles on every axis — the queue's poorest large market after its richest small one — and the macro's honesty starts with its evidence: no fact in this file is CAPTURED; every fetch returned 403, and one of the world's least-indexed consumer markets rests here on search-index snippets in English and Arabic, graded and flagged throughout. What the index does support is stark: ~11.5–12.2M people (the estimate range held, not averaged), GDP per capita ~US$470, 92% below the national poverty line (World Bank, 2025) after five consecutive years of per-capita decline; an economy ~80%-linked to oil whose main pipeline runs through Sudan's civil war and ruptured in February 2024 (FY24 GDP −27.6%; production later recovering 61k→156k bpd); a currency at ~SSP 4,800/USD with FX reserves of US$27 million — effectively zero — and de-facto dollarization (~80% of deposits in FX; m-Gurush sells USD-only wallets); a politics suspended between Machar's treason trial and a first-ever election that the index cannot confirm has happened. The addressable market is Juba plus the trader class plus the aid economy — and one structural fact reorganises the whole entry: ~90% of imports move through two points (Nimule/Elegu and Juba airport), Uganda supplies 40.6% of imports, South Sudan is an EAC member — and Dabur's OWN East Africa infrastructure (Dabur EA, Nairobi; Unisel Ltd, the sole Kenya distributor; Amla on Kenyan shelves everywhere) sits at the corridor's other end with EAC tariff preference in principle. The species is provisional SEED-ABSENT, OWN-RAIL-ADJACENT — the first market in the programme where the rail one corridor away is Dabur itself. The verdict this macro defends: WATCH-AND-SEED, the CAR grammar at Juba scale — zero fixed cost, a Juba-coverage clause in the existing Kenya/Unisel relationship, an instrumented seed (Amla small formats + one self-proving balm, per the latency scale this market inherits verbatim), JIT Supermarket (Indian-owned) as the modern-trade door and Konyo Konyo wholesale as the volume node, USD terms throughout, both futures pre-written, and a re-review dated to the election question resolved plus corridor security stable. Juba rewards the patient wholesaler too — and Dabur's wholesaler is already in Nairobi.

The plan's demand model therefore counts households, not heads: the salaried civil-service layer (chronically in arrears but periodically paid), the NGO/UN payroll and its local staff economy, the remittance-receiving households the m-Gurush international rails serve, and the trader class itself. M6 · The lulu file — the one sourcing story with women's names on it

The note's most genuinely documented consumer-adjacent asset is not a market but a supply chain: lulu — Vitellaria nilotica, the East African shea whose softer, higher-olein butter is marketed internationally as premium — grows across Bahr el Ghazal and Equatoria, and its processing economy is real and women-owned: the USAID-backed Lulu Livelihoods Program (from 2001) built 36 Lulu Works centres with ~850 women earners; Lulu Life trades on a cooperative model; Ubuntu Company Ltd sources from Central Equatoria women's cooperatives; an Oxfam/BMZ project modernizes shea-oil production; fair-trade lulu body butter retails on Etsy (all ID). For Dabur this is the sourcing mandate's South Sudan entry: a parked, dated sourcing file — nilotica lulu as a premium ingredient story for GLOBAL formulations, purchased from named cooperatives at fair-trade terms, IF quality systems and logistics ever clear the bar. It is explicitly NOT a Y1 motion, NOT a selling story for the local market (their butter, their economy), and NOT conflict-adjacent romance — the DRC's no-war-narrative rule carries whole. The file pairs with the honey watch and opens at the same annual review.
