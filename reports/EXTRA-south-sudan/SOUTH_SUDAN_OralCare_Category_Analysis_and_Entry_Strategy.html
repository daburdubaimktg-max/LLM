THE FORTY-SIXTH CORRIDOR FINDING — HERITAGE-SEVERED. The miswak's claim to this market is older and better-documented than anywhere in the queue's western run: Salvadora persica is native to Sudan, Sudanese dental surveys found lower caries prevalence among miswak users than toothbrush users, and the WHO endorsed the practice twice (1986, 2000 — all ID). But the research date's map matters more than the botany's: partition left the miswak's cultural geography overwhelmingly north of the 2011 border. South Sudan's population is ~6.2% Muslim; the siwak tradition lives here mainly in northern heritage communities and the Darfuri/Somali trader networks — the same networks that run the consumer-goods stalls. The finding names what no prior market showed: a political border can sever a product's heritage from its market — the tradition's homeland is next door, and the market inherits an echo carried by its trading minority. Three consequences bind the deck. First, the heritage claim is REAL but not THIS market's to be sold on: Meswak's copy stays botanical and functional («the toothbrush tree»), never national or devotional — the severed heritage is the neighbour's culture-space and the trader community's private inheritance, not a mass positioning asset. Second, the affinity map's DIVERGENT cell runs its second application exactly as built: the Muslim trader gate will buy the corner warmly; the ~94% non-Muslim street decides sell-through; wholesale enthusiasm is logged as bias, stall telemetry rules. Third, the spectrum's verdict stands where the macro pre-read it: PASS CONFIRMED — no oral price room exists at 92% poverty, Colgate's re-export default owns the visible shelf, and the one exception is the CAR grammar's second member: an order-capped Meswak mini corner on the hair seed's carton, three endings pre-written, the Kampala reference shelf's toothpaste row audited on the same day as hair's.

DEEP THINK · The objections, answered

"The miswak evidence is the best in the queue — refusing to use the heritage seems wasteful." The evidence is real and the deck uses every part of it that this market owns: the botany (the tree panel), the function (the tradition's implicit endorsement of the active), and the trader community's private recognition (which needs no copy at all — the gate knows the tree on sight). What the deck refuses is the part the border moved: the NATIONAL story, which belongs to Khartoum's culture-space, and the DEVOTIONAL story, which belongs to the gate's faith and not to a tube's label. A true story deployed by the wrong owner curdles — the street hears a foreign brand wearing the neighbour's clothes, and the gate hears its inheritance merchandised. The finding's rule: check the claim's ownership before its truth.

"Three markets, three oral passes — is the spectrum just a pass machine for poor markets?" The spectrum passed the Seychelles into a three-SKU house with a race clock — its verdicts track the axes, not a temperament.
