C1 · Category frame — light paper, a priced ceiling, and a borrowed experiment

Botswana's wellness architecture is the batch's friendliest. PAPER: BoMRA registers complementary medicines (~BWP 400 cited consultancy-sourced [!] fee direct), but complementary/OTC items sold through general traders need only a trade licence (WTO notification) and cosmetics face no pre-market registration — three lanes, all light, with the one discipline that matters being CLAIMS (a therapeutic reading re-classifies into the medicines track; the deck's copy rules make that impossible). HONEY: a net-import market (~13% self-sufficiency), quality-inconsistent local supply (62% standards compliance), an FAO national strategy to 2027, Zambian and SA import lanes running, and the batch's rarest gift — a CAPTURED ceiling: Goldcrest 500g at P64.95, Fours Cash & Carry, itself a multi-origin blend foursonline.co.bw [!] re-confirm; Farming First; FAO/Xinhua 2024. THE TONIC: the CMA-adjacent slot is equally empty here, and — the deck's organising fact — the Namibian pilot exists: by the time Botswana's wellness wave could launch a tonic, the pilot's two-quarter read (designed exactly for regional reuse, floor-not-ceiling caveat attached) will have reported. Botswana therefore runs NO tonic experiment of its own: it inherits the answer, launches on a positive read via the champion desk and the grocers, or defers on a negative one with the caveat noted. Demand context: diabetes prevalence LOW and dated (2.3%→2.7%, 2007→2014), NCDs ~46% of deaths and rising post-2030 — the register is GENERAL wellness (energy, family strength, immunity within claim rules), never disease-specific, never crisis-framed STEPS via PMC [!] current print.

The claims discipline — the light paper's one hard wall

Light lanes tempt loose language, so the discipline gets its own section-adjacent paragraph. The re-classification trap works mechanically: a "supports immunity" on a foodstuff label, a "helps manage" in a Facebook caption, a "clinically" anywhere — and the SKU migrates from the trade-licence lane to the medicines track, taking its stock, its listings and its calendar with it. The deck's wall has three courses: the barred list leads with the trap's vocabulary in both languages; counsel reviews every rendered consumer-facing line BEFORE production (a day's work on a short list); and the champion desk's own compliance team is briefed as a second net (retailers police claims here too — a gift, used). The rule's spirit, stated once: in a market kind enough to offer light paper, the discipline is not to lean on the kindness.

DEEP THINK · Inheritance versus evidence, argued honestly. The objection to the protocol writes itself: Botswana is not Namibia — richer per head on paper, poorer this cycle, different diaspora, different chains — so why let Windhoek's till data govern Gaborone's launch? Because the alternative is worse on every axis the objection names: a local pilot would cost a year and a budget to answer the SAME question (does the claim-free food-format tonic convert non-diaspora Southern-African wellness shoppers?) on a smaller, noisier stage; the differences the objection lists are SECOND-ORDER to that question (the format either translates or it doesn't — the diaspora delta is why the caveat exists); and the protocol's limits already concede everything worth conceding (directional only, local sizing, the override). The deeper principle the deck stakes: a programme that builds instruments and then re-runs every experiment locally has built souvenirs, not instruments. Botswana is where the batch finds out which it built — and either answer improves the next forty markets.
DEEP STRAT · The wellness arc, closed with the batch. Seven OTC decks: Zambia's dossier-as-product, Angola's three-gate wait, Mozambique's two clocks, Namibia's foodstuff lane and pilot, Botswana's light paper and inherited read — plus the honey taxonomy running through all of them to its six-posture menu. The arc's transferable law, stated once for the B8-SUMMARY: OTC entry design is PAPER DESIGN — every verdict in the arc was set by the regulatory lane's shape and the trust environment's depth, never by demand (which was real everywhere and decisive nowhere). The corollary the programme carries forward: research OTC paper FIRST in every future market, because it is the cheapest fact to find and the only one that reorders everything else. And the honey coda: from Bu Saif to Fours Cash & Carry, fourteen markets taught the programme that honey is never one question — it is a sourcing question, a shelf question, a protection question and a timing question wearing one jar, and the menu now holds all four answers priced.
