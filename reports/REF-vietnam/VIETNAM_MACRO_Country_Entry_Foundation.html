M1 · Demographics — a 100-million market that is urbanizing, ageing and trading up at once

So what: Vietnam is not a "young frontier market" anymore — it is a fast-ageing, below-replacement-fertility, 42%-urban market whose commercial centre of gravity is two metros (HCMC, Hanoi) and whose demand base is a middle class doubling from ~13M to ~26M people between 2023 and 2026. Entry sequencing writes itself: win HCMC first (brand-open, trial-prone, remittance-fed), prove in Hanoi second (conservative, promotion-responsive), and let rural GT — 45% of FMCG spend and growing faster than urban — be a Wave-2/3 distributor play, not a launch bet.

M2 · Macroeconomics & affordability — premium-mass has real runway; sachets still rule the rural door

So what: 8% growth, 3–4% inflation, a crawling 3–5%/yr VND slide and a middle class doubling: Vietnam supports a premium-mass (masstige) price architecture in metros — the P/S-anchored floor is very low, but urban consumers already pay VND 130–166k for a 50g Pond's jar and VND 161k for a 650ml Sunsilk. The entry unit is NOT forced down to sachet/LUP as in FX-rationed Africa — but the rural 45% of FMCG runs on sachets and sub-VND-35k tubes, so the portfolio needs a two-altitude pack ladder from day one of Wave 2. Price in VND with a 3–5%/yr depreciation buffer; the AED (USD-pegged) supply base insulates better than INR or TRY.

M3 · Politics, geopolitics, trade & legal — a warm door for India, and a duty chessboard that favors RAK-UAE

So what: Vietnam is politically stable, pro-FDI and specifically warm to India (ties elevated to Enhanced Comprehensive Strategic Partnership in 2026, $25bn trade target) — no origin-political risk. The real M3 finding is the duty chessboard: there is no bilateral India–Vietnam FTA, only AIFTA (Form AI); but the UAE–Vietnam CEPA entered into force 3 Feb 2026, eliminating tariffs on 98.5% of UAE-origin lines — which makes Dabur's RAK-UAE plant the presumptive supply base for Vietnam, with the India lane reserved for authenticity-hero SKUs. Second finding: Vietnam is strict first-to-file on trademarks with documented squatting risk — filing classes 3/5 (plus 21/30 for oral/health formats) is the single most urgent Phase-0 action.

IP & trademark — the clock is already running

Vietnam is strict first-to-file; squatting of foreign marks is a documented, growing problem (Tilleke & Gibbins 2025). The 2022 amended IP Law (eff. 2023) added bad-faith as a refusal/cancellation ground (Arts. 96, 117), but the burden of proof sits with the brand owner and enforcement is mostly administrative. Grey sellers are already trading on "Dabur", "Vatika" and "Amla" names (§O2) — every month unfiled is squatter option-value. Phase-0 action: confirm/extend Madrid designations and national filings for DABUR, VATIKA, DABUR RED, DABUR AMLA, GULABARI, HERBOLENE, HAJMOLA, HONITUS in classes 3, 5, 21, 30 — status was not verifiable from this environment (WIPO/ iplib returned 403) re-validate at branddb.wipo.int + ipvietnam.gov.vn.

M4 · History, religion, values, cohorts & occasions — a herbal-literate culture with its own medicine cabinet

So what: Vietnam already believes what Dabur sells — but in its own words. The culture runs a dual traditional-medicine system (thuốc nam / thuốc bắc), washes hair in bồ kết, fades scars with nghệ (turmeric), and 88% say they'll pay more for natural. Ayurveda can slot into the existing mental model as "the Indian sibling of thuốc bắc" — credible, but NOT self-selling: the emotional heartland is the Vietnamese grandmother's remedy, so Dabur's claims must be translated into Vietnamese heritage language and paired with modern proof. Religion imposes no category constraints (halal is a non-issue — Islam ≈0.1%); the commercial calendar is Tết-centric (~20% of annual FMCG revenue) with two women's days and a mega-sale e-com ladder.

M5 · Media, digital, news & social — a TikTok-first discovery machine under a brand-new trust regime

For a proof-led herbal entrant this is a tailwind: documentation-heavy brands win the new compliance game, and mass-KOC seeding (not mega-KOL bets) is both cheaper and safer.

The 2025–26 trust reset — the single most important media fact

The Kera-candy scandal: Miss Grand International Nguyễn Thúc Thùy Tiên, Quang Linh Vlogs and Hằng Du Mục sold 56,000+ boxes of "1 gummy = a plate of vegetables" candy (lab: 0.51g fibre/100g); fined VND 140M (Mar 2025), arrested (Apr–May 2025), sentenced to 2 years' prison 19 Nov 2025 (Tuổi Trẻ, VnExpress). H1 2025: 7 celebrities/KOLs investigated for ad violations. The regulatory response now defines the playbook:

  • Amended Advertising Law (passed Jun 2025, eff. 1 Jan 2026): KOLs/KOCs must verify advertiser credibility and product dossiers, disclose sponsorship before and during content, and may not endorse products they haven't personally used — administrative fines, civil liability, criminal exposure (Tilleke & Gibbins).
  • Cosmetics ad pre-approval ABOLISHED — Circular 03/2026/TT-BYT (eff. 15 Feb 2026) moves to post-market surveillance; claims capped by ASEAN Cosmetic Claims rules (no therapeutic claims: "moisturizes/brightens" OK, "treats eczema" banned).
  • Functional-food/OTC advertising still pre-registered (tightened after the fake-milk/Kera wave) — the OTC deck's claim ladder starts here.

TV/OTT and payments: TV ad market ~$992M and declining but still the #1 channel for rural FMCG reach (Kantar/Vietnam Briefing) — relevant only from Wave 2; streaming ~70M subscribers H1 2025 (FPT Play, VieON, TV360 vs Netflix).
