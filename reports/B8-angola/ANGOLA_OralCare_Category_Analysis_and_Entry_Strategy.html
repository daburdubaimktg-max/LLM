C3 · Brand-by-brand teardown

Colgate — the kingdom, publicly priced. The ladder IS the market: 290–730 Kz small tubes anchoring daily purchase, saver twins at the trading-house tier (Bharat Mart), whitening at the top. Teardown lesson: you do not under-price a king whose bottom rung is already at the street's floor; you enter BESIDE the ladder with a story its rungs don't tell. The herbal column is that story, and it is empty shelf taxonomies, Jul 2026.

Signal (Unilever) — the absent second. No confirmed Angola retail hit in public search — a genuine anomaly given its Lusophone-Africa strength. Either it exists below search visibility (candonga-only) or Unilever ceded the country to Colgate's logistics. Both readings matter: the first means informal-channel shelf data is invisible to desk work (humility for every table here); the second means the #2 slot is STRUCTURALLY OPEN — the deck's biggest prize if true [!] W0 ask #3 — the deck plans for open-but-verify.

Couto — the heritage ghost. Porto, 1932, hydroxyapatite variants, colonial-era familiarity. No Angola retail evidence found, but the same Lisbon diaspora lanes that move Vatika could move Couto tomorrow. Lesson: the Portuguese-heritage register is a live threat lane — if Couto (or a copycat) activates it, the "trusted old-world paste" position closes. Another argument for speed [!] W0 ask #4.

Oral-B — the diaspora premium. Reseller-tier presence only; irrelevant to the value war, useful as the brush-aisle reference (see the brush-bundle question, C9).

The pharmacy imports (Elgydium tier) — the fear-priced therapeutic. Present at Mecofarma-tier pharmacies for the burden's symptomatic edge. Not competed with year 1; their price umbrella is noted for year 3.

Self-teardown — the second container states its own bet. Herb'l arrives with no equity, no parcels, no diaspora trail — everything hair had, oral lacks. The entry therefore bets the RELAY: that a proven route (distributor, listings, candonga relationships, hologram rails, partner invoicing) cuts oral's launch cost to the 45–55% index the programme measured in Zambia's design — Angola is the experiment's second instance, and this deck's economics are built at the index's conservative END (55%) with the honest note that the index itself is still being measured upstream. C5 · The fluoride gate — this deck's first commandment

Angola publishes no fluoride mandate, no toothpaste standard, and no fluoridation policy that public search can find [!] the gap itself is the finding — W0 regulatory pull. Meanwhile its clinical literature describes a population where untreated caries is the NORM by age 30. This deck therefore writes itself a commandment it will not cross: no fluoride-free SKU ships as the family staple in a 72.9%-untreated-caries market without an affirmative, documented decision — dental-professional consultation on record, formulation options reviewed (the international Herb'l range spans fluoride and fluoride-free variants — selection is an INTERNAL formulation/regulatory verification item, worked with public evidence only here), and ARMED's position confirmed. The herbal story is the position; fluoride is a formulation fact inside it. If the gate resolves fluoride-mandatory (by regulation or by conscience), Herb'l launches fluoridated with the herbal register intact; if genuinely open, the decision is still taken as if the dentist were in the room. A value brand whose buyers ration tubes owes them teeth that last — citizenship before premium was never only a pricing law.

DEEP THINK · Why the second container waits — argued once, against the strongest counter-argument. The case for launching oral IN the first container is real: consolidation saves freight, the buyer meeting covers two categories anyway, and every month of delay is a month Couto or a Colgate flanker could close the herbal column. The deck still says no, for three reasons that outrank all three. First, EVIDENCE SEQUENCING: hair's M6 read-out is the cheapest oral market research Angola will ever produce — real velocity on the same route, same partner, same streets; launching blind alongside it burns the information a season's patience buys. Second, FAILURE SEPARABILITY: if both categories launch together and stall, the post-mortem cannot tell route failure from category failure, and the programme learns nothing it can bill to Mozambique — the relay's whole value is that its instances are legible experiments. Third, the FLUORIDE GATE is not schedule-compressible: rushing the one decision this deck calls a commandment to catch a boat is exactly the improvisation the file's governance exists to prevent. The counter-argument's honest residue is the Couto clock — and the deck answers it with speed WITHIN the sequence (listings at M9, not M12) rather than abandonment of the sequence.
