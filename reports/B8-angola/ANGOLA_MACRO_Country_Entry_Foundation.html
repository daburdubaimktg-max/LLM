M1 · Demography — the youngest market in the programme, in Portuguese

Angola's population is CONTESTED across sources — ~40.2M (2026 projections) against census-trend readings nearer 34–37M [!] INE 2024 census finals — the file's first re-validation — but its shape is not: median age 16.7, 44.4% under fifteen, the youngest market in this programme, urbanising toward 80% with Luanda at ~8.5M — one in five Angolans in one city. Portuguese is near-universal in urban commerce (71%+ speakers; sufficient for national packaging), with Umbundu (23%, the central highlands), Kimbundu and Kikongo as radio-localisation layers. Religion: Catholic 41% + Protestant/Evangelical 38%, with documented syncretism — Christian practice coexisting with kimbanda (traditional-healer) consultation, which this file fences from all commercial use exactly as its Zambian sibling fenced the ng'anga's world. Hair is near-universally Afro-textured — structurally a Black-hair-care market in the Nigeria/DRC grammar, NOT a South Asian oiling culture; the demand architecture in every deck flows from that fact.

M2 · Economy — oil's child, inflation's tenant, the kwanza's good year

Oil is ~94% of exports and ~60% of fiscal revenue; growth reads 1.9–3.1% for 2025 by source [!] range carried; inflation runs 15.7–17.2% — easing but double-digit, eroding imported-FMCG purchasing power faster than local staples. The kwanza sits ~919/USD with an UNUSUALLY stable 2026 — a stability this file refuses to extrapolate (the structural exposure to oil is unchanged; the plan prices at a 1,000–1,100 buffer band planning rule). The Luanda paradox persists: one of the world's historically costliest cities for imported goods sitting atop a mass market whose minimum wage is itself CONTESTED between 32,181 and 70,000 AOA/month [!] decree check — a 2× spread that changes every pack-size decision. The consumer read: a hyper-young, price-brutal mass market with a thin imported-premium crust — the steepest wealth gradient in the batch.

T2b · The two-economy wallet — who actually buys, and with what

Angola's macro numbers describe two economies wearing one currency. The first is salaried, banked and small: public-sector and oil-adjacent formal employment, paid by transfer, shopping the Kero and Candando aisles at posted prices. The second is the candonga economy — the zungueira (street vendor, from the Kimbundu ku zunga, to circulate) selling from a basin balanced on her head, her customers paid in cash daily or not at all. The informal share of employment is put at roughly 80% in most recent readings [!] INE labour survey — re-pull the exact informality series, which means the second economy is not the residual of the market; it IS the market, and the first economy is its premium annex. Every ladder this file's category decks author must price for the second wallet and merely remain visible to the first.

M3 · Market access — the forex gate

The tariff wall is ordinary (10.9% average, 2–50% range; cosmetics HS lines unconfirmed [!] AGT pauta pull); Angola grants NO preferences to anyone (SADC member but outside its FTA — MFN for all). Port of Luanda has improved (4.4-day vessel waits post-concession) though clearance friction persists. The real wall is the FOREX GATE: commercial banks informally cap USD sales (~$1,000/week per customer against a nominal $120k/year threshold), the parallel market runs 30–35% above official, and an importer's problem is not landing goods but PAYING for them — repatriating margin or settling an overseas principal on time. The structural consequence outranks every other finding in this file: Angola's entry is a corporate-structure decision before it is a marketing decision — local-currency invoicing through a distributor/JV who owns the forex problem, versus direct export-and-repatriate which imports it. The candidate partner class is documented: Lebanese trading houses (entrenched since the late-1980s, customs-and-forex fluent) and the Bharat Mart-type Indian retail beachhead [!] named-entity search queued.

M4 · Regulation — ARMED's perimeter, thinly mapped

ARMED (Agência Reguladora de Medicamentos e Tecnologias de Saúde, under MINSA) licenses the pharmaceutical establishment chain INCLUDING cosmetics shops (the RLEAF regime names them), runs the new SIREMA digital registration system for medicines (cosmetics-dossier extension unconfirmed [!]), and — per 2022 reporting — lacked its own QC laboratory, implying manufacturer/exporting-country certificates carry registration weight [!] confirm — the RAK/India COA question. A local pharmacist-of-record plus a MINCO import licence read as near-certain gates [!] local counsel. The lightening register: NO Angolan restriction was found at all — against regional peers' bans — while an active informal trade in «creme clareador» products (~15,000 AOA, social-media sellers) runs visibly uncompliant. This is T15b's SIXTH condition: UNREGULATED — no law, no stated posture, a live grey trade — and the discipline binds by rule precisely because nothing else here binds anyone. Counterfeit context is severe (a dated sample study found 53.3% medicine non-conformity; ~50% circulating-counterfeit estimates; the DRC border the entry point) — anti-counterfeit rails are a launch PRECONDITION, not an enhancement.

The consumer decode in one story — a Rocha Pinto Saturday

Take one Saturday in Rocha Pinto, the hillside musseque twenty minutes from Luanda's glass towers. A mother of four starts at the candonga stall two alleys down, where a zungueira she has bought from for six years sells her a 200g pot of Skala creme — Brazilian, pink-lidded, its label promising vitamina e óleos naturais in the Portuguese of the telenovela she watched last night. She pays in kwanza notes whose street value both women silently index to this week's parallel dollar. The pot will do four jobs: her own twists, her daughters' braids, the baby's dry shins, and a fingertip for her husband's razor bumps — one jar, one price, four bodies. Toothpaste is a decision she makes monthly, not weekly: the smallest Colgate at 345 Kz when the month is hard, the 830 Kz tube when it is not, and she can recite both prices without looking because in a 15–17% inflation economy every household runs a live price board in its head. Medicine she buys last and most carefully — from Mecofarma when the child's fever frightens her, from the stall's blister-packs when it does not, knowing some of those blisters are counterfeit and buying the fear-priced genuine article only when it matters most. Honey she knows from her mother's province, sold roadside in reused bottles; a foreign jar of it would puzzle her. No SKU in this story is Indian. That is the entry's whole geometry in one shopping trip: the CODE she trusts is Brazilian and available, the LADDER she recites is Colgate's and priced, the CHANNEL she uses is the zungueira's and margin-driven, the categories she is not ready to buy from a stranger — medicine, honey — are exactly the ones this file defers. The decks that follow are that Saturday, systematised.

DEEP THINK — is Angola worth entering at all this cycle? The honest case against: the forex gate taxes every dollar out, inflation runs double-digit, the population number itself is contested, three of four categories defer or wait, and the batch has friendlier files (Namibia, Botswana) queued behind it. The case for entering anyway, which carries narrowly: (1) the DEMAND SIGNAL — Angolans already pay Lisbon freight for Vatika; unserved demand at 3× landed cost is the loudest proof this programme ever gets; (2) the CODE ARBITRAGE — the Brazilian register's trust is transferable to Dabur's botanicals NOW, while the natural-hair current builds; waiting cedes the lane's Indian seat to Emami or a Marico; (3) the STRUCTURE, once built (local-invoicing partner, counterfeit rails, PT panel), is the batch's Lusophone template — Mozambique inherits it whole, which halves the next file's cost. The synthesis: enter NARROW (hair first, oral second, everything else gated), through a partner who owns the forex problem, sized so that a total write-off costs one container and a year. Kill-test: if no partner emerges willing to invoice locally at workable terms within two quarters of the shortlist's first meeting, Angola re-times to the batch's tail and Mozambique's file leapfrogs it — the queue is a strategy, not a promise.

The bet's shape, before its text. Six prior macro bets in this programme wagered on launches; this one wagers mostly on GATES — that a partner will be found or the market honestly re-timed, that boards will be rebuilt from streets rather than proxies, that two categories will launch narrow while three stay deliberately unshipped, and that a deferred verdict will be decided by evidence rather than by a container's spare capacity. A bet whose clauses are mostly discipline clauses can still fail loudly: the partner search can drift past its kill-date, the deferrals can leak, the parallel rate can eat a ladder between authorship and shelf. It is falsifiable at every joint, which is the only quality a reputation bet is required to have.
