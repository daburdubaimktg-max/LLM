THE ASK: Yemen OTC is a BUILD-narrow whose winning formats are small — because the honest price maths says so. The demand system is the strongest in the batch: the remedy canon is alive and war-strengthened (herbalists' trade boomed as drug prices rose 300%+; honey, black seed, fenugreek and myrrh are daily practice, not nostalgia), respiratory symptoms drive 80% of self-medication episodes (60% of Sana'a patients took an unprescribed antibiotic in the prior 15 days), aid is collapsing (2025 HRP 25% funded; 453 facilities closing — the paid self-care market is structurally growing), and the Prophetic register is native (the Jordan playbook travels — unlike Lebanon). But the flagship format fails the note: a Honitus 100ml syrup lands at ~$2.70–3.25 (≈4,400–5,300 YER-new / 1,450–1,750 old) through the official +57.44% pharma stack — 2–3× over the qat-note hero band. The deck's answer is a format inversion: (1) LEAD = Honitus lozenges (honey-ginger; Arabic pack copy already live on Dabur's UAE site) at 100–150 YER-old per piece / 400–500 per 4-strip — pocket-change tier, dukkan-sellable loose by the piece (the dukkan already splits cigarette packs), heat-stable, no cold chain, and plausibly food-track clearable (the regulatory swing [!]); the syrup enters beside it as the pharmacy "family bottle" without volume expectations. (2) The original play — Hajmola on the post-تخزينة occasion: qat's GI burden is documented (constipation to 80.8% in a college cohort; obstruction a recognised surgical entity) and the demand is proven by natural experiment — when Aden banned qat in 1957, laxative sales fell 90%, recovering with the ban's lift; no product anywhere is branded on the occasion; a 4-tab sachet retails at ~100 YER-old with >40% dukkan margin — sellable at the qat stall itself, beside the sweet tea. (3) Third wave only: the branded tibb-nabawi line (black-seed + honey formats — the lane does not exist in Yemen, but attar loyalty makes it slower money than acute cough and the daily qat occasion). Guardrails: never claim against Sidr (sell cough-function, never honey-quality — the sacred pyramid stands), never moralise the chew (post-session register only: "بعد المخزن… هاضم"), seal everything (counterfeit medicines ~30% of supply; under half of ~13,000 pharmacies licensed — the أصلي program extends), and route by zone: lozenges/sachets north-first via the Hodeidah food track; syrup south via Aden's cleaner pharmacy track, with the GAFTA origin decision (RAK-UAE cert = 0% vs Indian-origin 25%) worth more than any marketing line. Category verdict (macro board): BUILD-narrow.

C1 — The ask (executive summary)

Macro anchor (YEMEN_MACRO decision board): OTC ranked BUILD-narrow — the herbal lane only, gated by dual regulators and the aid-distorted wallet. This deck sharpens "narrow" into a format law: in a qat-note economy, the small formats are the strategy — the lozenge beats the bottle, the sachet beats the jar, and the occasion (post-takhzina) beats the category.

The play in one screen. Four truths define the build. First, the canon is the market: honey for cough and wounds, black seed by hadith, fenugreek daily on the saltah (its popularity explicitly linked in food-culture sources to "a nation of qat-chewers and the digestive problems associated with the drug's long-term use"), myrrh for mouth and gut — all supplied loose through the عطارة, with no branded tibb-nabawi packaged lane found anywhere in-country. Dabur does not have to create belief; it has to package it at note-money. Second, the acute need is respiratory and the cultural answer is already honey: with 80% of self-medication episodes driven by respiratory symptoms and WHO itself endorsing honey as a cough demulcent, Honitus is "the pharmacy version of what your grandmother makes" — but the honest waterfall shows the 100ml bottle misses the note, so the lozenge leads: piece-priced, dukkan-native, loose-sellable, heat-stable, with Dabur's Arabic copy already written. Third, the qat-digestive occasion is the batch's most original open position: the 1957 Aden natural experiment (laxative sales −90% under the qat ban) proves the demand mechanically; the GI literature quantifies it; the folk answer (fenugreek) confirms consumers already self-treat it; and no brand on earth has claimed the post-session moment — Hajmola's candy-tablet format, tamarind-sour profile and ₹-sachet economics fit it like a glove at ~100 YER-old. Fourth, trust is the multiplier: with ~30% of legitimate-looking medicines illicit and most pharmacies unlicensed, a sealed, holographed, halal-marked, أصلي-QR'd pack from a GCC-traded house is itself a claim. The moves: (1) lozenges north-first via the Hodeidah food track, dukkan strips + loose-piece selling; (2) syrup south via Aden's pharmacy track as the family bottle; (3) Hajmola post-takhzina within six months — qat-stall distribution, never moralising; (4) the tibb-nabawi line third, built on the first two SKUs' distribution; (5) GAFTA origin certificates decided before anything ships. Do not lead the syrup, do not claim against Sidr, do not moralise the chew, and do not let the drug track capture what the food track can carry. Reputation Bet (C18): by Ramadan 2027, the lozenge holds the note tier in the north and Hajmola owns a occasion nobody else has named — or the formats were right and the tracks were wrong.

C2 — Category frame, size & the self-care structure

Yemen's consumer-health frame is a collapsed formal system leaning its whole weight on self-care. Medicines are 80–90% imported (India the dominant supplier — 95% of tracked shipments); recorded pharma imports (~$26M/month) are implausibly small against 36–40M people, meaning most flow is unrecorded; local industry covers ~10% (Shaphaco the GMP champion); no reliable OTC market-size figure exists — the deck publishes only a flagged range (total pharma incl. informal ~$450–700M; OTC share est. 25–35% [!] — estimate based on regional analogues). The system shocks compound: the Dec 2024 airport strike put ~500 medicines into shortage in the north; the aid system is failing (25%-funded HRP; 453 facilities closing; 40% of facilities non/semi-functional) — every failure shifts another household from clinic to dukkan-and-pharmacy self-care.

C4 — Demand engine: the canon & the occasion (Module — consumer truth)

Two demand chambers again — the acute cough and the daily chew. The cough chamber: respiratory symptoms drive 80% of self-medication; the culturally correct first answer is honey (WHO-endorsed, hadith-sanctioned, grandmother-practised); the war's shortages made the pharmacy answer expensive and the counterfeit answer dangerous. The chew chamber: qat's GI toll is measured (constipation 15–18.4% in national survey data and to 80.8% in a college cohort; delayed gastric emptying; haemorrhoids; obstruction as a recognised surgical entity) and historically proven as a demand engine by the 1957 Aden ban — laxative sales fell 90% and recovered with the ban's lift; the folk self-treatment already exists (daily fenugreek, explicitly linked to the chewers' digestive problems); the post-session moment (بعد المخزن — the sweet tea, the come-down window) is an un-branded national ritual.

The Sidr discipline, restated for OTC. This category sails closest to the sacred rock. Honitus contains honey; the temptation to borrow Do'ani prestige ("with the goodness of Yemeni honey…") will recur in every creative review — and must lose every time. The pyramid's logic: Sidr IS medicine in the consumer's mind, priced accordingly, gifted accordingly, stored as savings; a $0.30 lozenge claiming that mantle would read as either fraud or insult. The lane that works is function-first: "relief for the throat, honey-and-ginger, clinically tested" — honey as ingredient, never as provenance; relief as the promise, never honey-quality. The same discipline protected the hair deck (leaf, never honey) and holds harder here.

C8 — Territories / where to win first

Territory logic follows the formats and the tracks rather than the population map alone. The lozenge's first geography is the northern dukkan web — Sana'a, Ibb and Dhamar — because that is where the three drivers stack: the heaviest respiratory self-medication burden, the strongest remedy canon, the old-rial purchasing power that makes a 100-YER piece a true reflex buy, and the Hodeidah food-track entry that keeps the piece economics intact. The syrup's first geography is the southern pharmacy spine — Aden and Taiz — where SBDMA-Aden registration is cleaner, GAFTA certificates are routine, and the Grand-Vit precedent proves the family-bottle purchase exists. The Hajmola wave is not a geography at all but a channel overlay: the qat stalls of every city, north and south, entered through the same wholesale relationships that stock the dukkans beside them — which is why it launches second, riding routes the lozenge has already opened. The attar-dense port hinterlands (Tihama, Mukalla) wait for wave 3, when the branded canon line needs the adjacency.
