## Cross-cutting Yemen findings (portable to B4)

1. **The war did the segmentation.** Two currencies, two customs regimes, two retail cultures — every deck's architecture is zone-first, category-second.
   Portable test: in any fractured state (Libya next), design per-zone P&Ls with independent kill-switches before choosing SKUs.
2. **The state can be your category manager.** The northern import-substitution enforcement (ban → grace → confiscation → 404s) is an exogenous shelf-
   clearing event with a clock; the strategy is to arrive as the compliant, non-American, GAFTA-origin answer while the vacuum is fresh — never to
   celebrate or advertise the politics that made it.
3. **The qat-note is the real price law.** Where a daily discretionary ritual anchors the wallet (~$1–2.50/day south), FMCG pricing is not benchmarked
   against competitors but against the ritual's own note. Pieces and sachets clear it; bottles and jars don't — the format law follows from the wallet, not
   from preference.
4. **Sacred ingredients have a hierarchy.** Sidr honey is untouchable (never out-honey a Yemeni) but the Sidr LEAF is open; the miswak is the ritual's
   word and the Tihama grows the tree. Rule: source the story one step below the sacred apex — leaf not honey, tree not relic — and function-first always.
5. **أصلي (genuine) is the scarcest attribute in a war economy** — counterfeits name-check Vatika specifically. The scratch-QR + WhatsApp verification
   program is not brand furniture; it is the moat, shipped at launch in every category, and its Arabic word does the advertising.
6. **Religion is infrastructure, not positioning.** Tibb nabawi is trade-level truth north (mosque-mat channel, fitrah clock, sunnah framing) and an
   occasion register south — the zone-split religious-space rule (trade north / occasion south) repeated in all four decks. The fitrah frame turned skin's
   hardest category (depilatories) into an observance aid — the batch's cleanest demonstration that the frame, not the product, is the entry.
7. **Dormant equity beats new equity.** Every deck found Dabur assets already trading (Vatika live, Herb'l live, Siwak-F holding a slot, Fem gray-flowing
   as "الأصلي الاماراتي") — reactivation economics (repair, re-role, re-price) beat launch economics in every waterfall. Audit before you build.
