THE ASK: Yemen hair is the programme's purest LEAD — a reactivation-deepening with a state-made opening and a race against one clock. Vatika already holds the widest hair range of any brand on Yemen's largest e-commerce platform (~15+ SKUs across BOTH zone storefronts: styling cream 70/140/210ml from 1,768 YER ≈ $1.09, five hammam-zait variants at 4,820, shampoos 400–900ml to 8,670), with the Arabic-ritual localisation already done (garlic, henna-olive, black-seed variants; the real حمام زيت naming) and Amla trading beside it. Three findings define the play. First, the P&G vacuum: the Houthi ban on American goods (enforced from ~Jul 2025) stranded Pantene, Head & Shoulders, Herbal Essences and Pert in the ⅔-population northern zone — and the Ansarollah boycott apparatus itself publishes an alternatives list naming Sunsilk among the approved substitutes: Unilever is the state-blessed filler of the vacuum, and beating it to consolidation is the deck's clock. Second, the sidr bridge is an open first-mover slot: the weekly سدر wash is Yemen's own foundational hair ritual — Sunnah-endorsed, Hadhrami- prestige, anti-dandruff-credible, the same tree as the sacred Do'ani honey — currently sold only as trad powder (Sitt Al-Kul, 1,500 YER) and by tiny attar brands; no major brand anywhere in Yemen/KSA/Gulf has a sidr variant. A Vatika Sidr (شامبو/حمام زيت بالسدر اليمني) would be the first mainstream codification of Yemen's own ritual. Third, the أصلي (genuine) equity: counterfeiting is state-level news in both zones and Vatika is the name-checked faked brand — its own Sana'a listings carry "الاصلي" in the title; a genuine-assurance seal + QR program converts the fake problem into a trust moat. The moves: (1) press the northern vacuum now (two-zone consignments per the macro design — Hodeidah north / Aden south, never cross-ship); (2) fill the missing rung — a 90–100ml oil at the "qat-note" price (~1,600 new-YER / ~$1) between the $1.09 cream and the $2.31 300ml, plus shampoo sachets (FOB clears with ~2× headroom); (3) launch Vatika Sidr with a Hadhrami-leaf sourcing story; (4) seal the genuine (أصلي program); (5) defend the south on value vs Pantene at 8,092. The GAFTA question is the margin swing (~$0.30/unit, 13% of shelf — fund the squeezed dukkan margin with it, not price cuts). Category verdict (macro board): LEAD.

C4 — Demand engine: the ritual map (Module — consumer truth)

Yemeni hair care is a living ritual system, not a benefit market — and the system is documented in unusual ethnographic depth. The foundational wash is sidr: leaf powder of the same Ziziphus tree that gives Do'ani honey, used weekly, endorsed as Sunnah practice, credited with anti-dandruff and anti-lice properties, with Hadhrami leaf carrying the prestige grade. Around it: the Mu'aqassa (المعقّصة) — the professional female braider who worked the عجفة circular braid, scented with mahlab and saffron to hold fragrance until the next wash; henna (Yemeni bridal henna is an export prestige item — "حناء عروس اليمن"); native oils (saleet/juljul sesame, black seed) beside naturalised Indian imports (coconut, amla); and the hammam — Old Sana'a keeps 14 working Ottoman-era steam baths with gender-scheduled days, Thursday the bridal day when brides are oiled after steam.

The sidr-bridge feasibility verdict — first-mover confirmed. The scan for existing sidr hair products found only small natural/attar brands (Rowa Care KSA, The Old Tree, Sabbar — all boutique) and no sidr variant from Unilever, P&G, L'Oréal, Marico, Emami or Dabur anywhere in Yemen, KSA or the Gulf. The slot is open, and it is more than a flavor: sidr is simultaneously the weekly ritual, a Sunnah-legitimated practice, the Hadhrami prestige grade, an anti-dandruff claim with native credibility, and the same tree as the national honey treasure — a five-layer semantic asset. The discipline in claiming it: pair the launch with the أصلي program and a real sidr-extract percentage plus a Hadhramaut/Tihama leaf-sourcing story (authenticity policing is fierce in the land of sidr); and tell the leaf story, never the honey story — borrowing Do'ani honey's sanctity for a shampoo would trip the macro's never-out-honey rule. Launch as a hammam-zait + shampoo pair so the ritual (weekly deep treatment) and the modern format (regular wash) each get a vehicle.
