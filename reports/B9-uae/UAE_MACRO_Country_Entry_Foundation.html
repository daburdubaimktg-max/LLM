M1 · Demographics — five consumer nations under one flag, and Dabur's base is the biggest

So what: the UAE is a federation of diasporas under an Emirati cultural canopy: ~11.3M people of whom Emiratis are ~11.5% and South Asians are ~62% (~7.1M) — meaning Dabur's home-market loyal base is the demographic majority while the prestige codes are set by the Khaleeji minority. This is the only market in the 42-country program where the entrant starts as the incumbent of the largest cohort. The strategy question is therefore not "how to enter" but "how to climb": from the South Asian mass shelf into the Emirati/Arab premium shelf, and from grocery into pharmacy.

M7 · Regulatory spine & supply lanes — cheap, fast, and already half-built

The claim decides the lane (one therapeutic claim → MOHAP drug territory at 10–100× cost). Supply is dual-sourced and duty-advantaged: RAK's 160M-unit plant plus CEPA-0% imports, while L'Oréal pays the 5% CET and Unilever/P&G run bigger regional plants. Binding constraint: speed-to-shelf and portfolio ambition, not regulation.
