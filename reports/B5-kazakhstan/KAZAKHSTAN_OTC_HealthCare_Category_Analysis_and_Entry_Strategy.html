C1–C2 · Category frame — a trust-channel market with a marketplace overlay

Kazakh OTC wellness runs on the same БАД architecture as Russia — state registration (СГР) via the Health Ministry's quality-control committee, structure/function claims only, the EAEU recognition premise — with two local torsions. FIRST, the CHANNEL: pharmacy counters dominate (medicines were only ~4.8% online in 2022; chain pharmacies hold 76.3% of a KZT 667.7bn market), so the trust game is won at the counter and Kaspi provides the REACH overlay rather than the spine — the inverse of Russia's marketplace-led structure stat.gov.kz; pharmnewskz 2025. SECOND, the CLOCK: БАД digital marking goes mandatory ~September 2026 (Order 199-НҚ, 57 codes) — every wellness SKU in this deck must be marking-enrolled within roughly fourteen months of this file's date pharmreviews/gov.kz 2026. Inside this frame sits the finding that shapes everything: Chyawanprash is already a Kazakh shelf reality — Kaspi lists Dabur regular, sugar-free, "Тройной иммунитет" and 1kg SKUs plus Madhuvaani Honitus (a Dabur honey-based cough jam — the brand's honey CONCEPT already trades here); satu.kz shows 132 Chyawanprash-tagged listings with confirmed prices (Dabur 1kg ₸7,395–9,380); and the specialist layer (Ganga ₸4,000–4,800/500g, Avita, Moya Indiya, omshankar) carries the depth kaspi indexed; satu/gipermart Jul-2026 [!] Kaspi prices 403-blocked.

C3 · Brand-by-brand teardown — five entries, one self-read

1 · Evalar — the incumbent that came along

Evalar's Kazakh operation (dedicated evalar.kz storefront, biosfera.kz and apteka listings, full assortment) shows the Russian БАД champion treating KZ as a home-adjacent market evalar.kz accessed Jul-2026. The Russia teardown's core transfer holds: Evalar owns pharmacy-coded natural trust and the single-herb format; the multi-herb ritual jar remains unoccupied by it. New here: Evalar's cross-border strength means its Kazakh shelf standing was NOT earned locally — a subtle opening, because "trusted Russian brand" reads differently to the Kazakh-first shopper cohort the re-Kazakhification trend is growing.

2 · Baidyanath — the value cousin Russia never showed

Baidyanath's Kesari Kalp at ₸2,800/500g (Veda Life) is the specialist channel's value option — an Indian heritage house undercutting Dabur by ~35% in the same aisle vedalife.kz. Lesson: the specialist niche has its own internal ladder, and Dabur's ₸4,000–4,800 sits at its TOP. The formalized entry defends that premium with the only currencies that justify it — the СГР number, the marking code, the brand's global scale — or watches the niche's value floor erode it.

3 · Himalaya — the real rival, fourth market running

Himalaya's KZ wellness presence (satu listings, kz.iherb storefront, the mir-vostoka importer) is again more institutionally built than Dabur's satu/kz.iherb. The race framing from Russia's OTC file applies with better odds and the same instruction: the СГР filing date is the single most consequential calendar entry in this deck.

4 · The kumys/shubat tradition — the incumbent that isn't for sale

The deck's most important non-competitor: fermented mare's and camel's milk occupy the "daily family resilience" slot in Kazakh culture with doctor-endorsed, COVID-era-reinforced standing lada/liter/bizmedia 2023. Chyawanprash does not displace kumys any more than it displaced burdock oil — it JOINS the household's wellness repertoire beside it. The copy consequence is architectural: the jar is "the Ayurvedic tradition's answer to the same instinct" — two old cultures agreeing that daily tonics work — and the panel's tone check ensures the comparison honours rather than appropriates. The nomadic frame belongs to Kazakhstan; Dabur is a respectful guest in it.

5 · Honitus — the accidental scout

Madhuvaani Honitus — a Dabur honey-based cough/cold jam — is ALREADY listed on Kaspi, unbriefed, unpromoted kaspi indexed. Strategically this is the honey window's scout report: a Dabur-branded honey-format wellness product cleared whatever informal path exists and found buyers. The honey pilot (T9b) does not start from zero; it starts from a live SKU whose G1 velocity read is the window's first datum. The teardown lesson: sometimes the market runs the pilot before the strategist designs it — read the accidental experiment before funding the deliberate one.

C5b · Consumer decode — three cabinets

The pharmacy loyalist. Karaganda, fifties, her medicine cabinet is the Soviet-adaptogen shelf plus whatever the провизор recommended last winter. She has never opened Kaspi for health products and never will; the jar reaches her at the counter or not at all, and its passport there is the СГР number and the pharmacist's familiarity with the brand — which is why the pharmacy pitch leads with paper and per-ritual math, not with Ayurveda. Her Kazakh-Russian bilingualism runs Russian-first in health contexts; the label obliges both.

The dual-heritage mother. Almaty, thirty-eight, keeps kumys in the fridge when she can get good quality, gives the children vitamin D because Instagram said so, fasts some of Ramadan. The jar's "two-traditions-agreeing" frame is built for her — the Ayurvedic tonic beside the nomadic one, neither displacing the other — and the honey gift-pack meets her twice a year: Ramadan (the religious-wellness valence, handled by the triple gate) and New Year (the secular gifting peak). She is the pilot's primary read in both windows.

The student and the studied. The ~10,000-strong Indian student cohort buys Chyawanprash from the specialist shops as a taste of home — the existing demand's visible core. But the decode's point is who stands beside them at the till: Kazakh classmates, colleagues, neighbours who ask what the jar is. The diaspora is the channel's seed, never the message (the dignity rule, standing); the entry's growth cohort is the curious local the seed introduces. The specialist shops' protected status keeps the seed alive while the mainstream build runs.

The price architecture question, argued in place

Move 3 deserves its reasoning shown, because the cross-channel spread is the deck's most genuinely open commercial call. The evidence: satu's 1kg band (₸7,395–9,380) implies ~₸3,700–4,700 per 500g-equivalent; the specialist shops charge ₸4,000–4,800 for the 500g; and the pharmacy shopper's reference frame (Evalar's trust-priced boxes, vitamin regimens) has never been asked the question at all. Three architectures compete. RATIFY HIGH (500g at ~₸4,400): honours the specialist precedent, protects their margins under the protected-specialist rule, but asks the pharmacy's first-time buyer to pay enthusiast prices. REPRICE MID (the deck's provisional ₸3,900): a designed step BELOW the specialist band for the mainstream channels, with the specialists compensated by supply terms rather than matching — defensible, but it opens a visible cross-channel gap the census's MAP discipline must manage. LADDER BY PACK: hold the 500g at specialist parity and make the 1kg the mainstream value story (its per-ritual math is the pitch's best line anyway) — the least channel-conflict, the most SKU complexity. The deck provisionally models REPRICE MID, flags the decision as G1's second deliverable (after the route call), and binds whichever architecture wins to one rule: ONE national logic, written down, with every channel's price derivable from it — because the fastest way to lose a trust channel is to let it discover it paid more than the app.
