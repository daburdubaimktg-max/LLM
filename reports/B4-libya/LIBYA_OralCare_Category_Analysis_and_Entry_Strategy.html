C2 — Market size & growth

Sizing honestly: no Libya oral-care market value exists publicly, and — unlike hair — no defensible LYD price band can be triangulated: the research returned exactly two price signals, a 260-LYD Splina herbal paste (Malaysian MLM direct-selling bundle — an outlier by construction) and an unverified TikTok claim of a 5-LYD Colgate; the deck declines to average nonsense and instead pegs provisional targets to the Egyptian mass range (~EGP 20–75 ≈ US$0.40–1.50 per 75–100ml) landed through the ~5.5% stack [!] pharmacy price sweep = task #1. What IS measurable is need and structure. Need: the dental burden is among the worst documented in this programme — 64.7% pooled permanent-teeth caries (17-study meta-analysis, 2026), 78% of six-to-seven-year-olds with decayed primary teeth (mean dmft 3.7), 57.8% at age 12, 98.4% in southern Libya, with the majority of child caries UNTREATED (Tripoli cross-sectional) — self-care is not a lifestyle here, it is the health system's fallback. Structure: coastal water is naturally fluoridated (~0.66 mg/L; Benghazi fluorosis low-to-moderate), so fluoride is table stakes, not news; the pharmacy is the category's shelf; and DSM.ly — a structured online oral-care marketplace run by the country's most followed pharmacy brand — proves organised demand exists at national-delivery economics (free over 50 LYD) (strategist read).
