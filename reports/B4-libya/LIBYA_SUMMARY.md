## Cross-cutting Libya findings (portable to the programme)

1. **Silence gates; it never licenses.** The set's defining discipline: where evidence was absent (the siwak register, Veet's price,
   the ingredient list, honey's ladder), the SKU waited for a named file with an owner — "we don't launch into silence." Tunisia
   rejected on evidence of presence; Libya gates on absence of evidence; both are the same method facing different darkness.
2. **The seal can BE the strategy.** In a market with documented lead/mercury counterfeits and flour-filled fake medicines,
   "official, sealed, printed price, from the authorized distributor" is not trade dressing — it is the purchase reason, worth COGS
   (serialisation) and margin (the distributor's 15% risk-transfer rate). Every future conflict-adjacent market gets this doctrine
   pre-built.
3. **Reforms are weather; structure for the climate.** The FX convergence is real, IMF-blessed and months old. The entry banks none
   of it: prepaid terms, small lots, zero dinar receivables, a same-week print at every shipment. If the reform holds, the structure
   costs little; if it relapses, the structure is the survival.
4. **A Facebook page is a national presence.** Bioderma, Avène, Opalescence and a Marico grey-marketeer all run Libya AS Facebook
   pages; DSM.ly is a pharmacy that became a marketplace. In COD-cash markets without card rails, the official storefront-page IS the
   flagship store — and the fabricated-influencer catch (three AI-suggested handles discarded) hardened the rule: build creator lists
   from direct platform search only, never from search-engine summaries.
5. **Untouchable ceilings, one geometry.** Three registers scored 33–34 and were deliberately un-grabbed (the ritual registers in
   hair, the Jabal Akhdar terroir in OTC, the hammam/dalka glow in skin) — all answered by accompany-salute-co-sign, never claim.
   The batch's most portable creative law.
6. **Re-derivation produced a third answer.** The jelly question now has three: Algeria creation, Tunisia displacement, Libya
   creation-with-a-blind-spot. The method's value is precisely that it keeps finding new answers to the same question — and the
   blind spot itself (the 403-hidden mass tier) was priced as the first spend rather than papered over.
7. **Bad data gets rejected in print.** The set's governance ledger: a 2009 "70% diabetes" figure rejected with reasoning; a price
   band refused on two unusable points; conflicting Volza slices carried openly; vendor claims flagged as vendor claims; a macro
   number narrowed (Modhela's 45%) on portfolio evidence. The reports say what is known, what is inferred, and what is silence.
