The Seychelles is an ENTER market — the macro's verdict, three decks now executing it — and precisely that success creates this deck's temptation: an entering brand wants to land its whole portfolio, and small markets flatter the wish (one more carton line, the same doors, why not?). The skin file answers why not, with the note's own words: this is Dabur's weakest entry lane — no diaspora pull equivalent to hair's or the flagship's (the parcels carry no meaningful skin signal), an authentic local natural-coconut incumbency occupying exactly the register Dabur's natural skincare would claim (Mahé Bleu's coconut-argan body ranges; the artisanal oils — the SAME shrine hair's finding mapped), a mass body-lotion tier presumed held by SA-supplied Vaseline/Nivea-class lines at duty-free landed costs, and a premium tourist tier better served by boutique local brands than by any mass entrant. The finding names the discipline the whole queue will reuse: market entry and category entry are separate decisions — in a small market, prune the portfolio's weakest lane at the landing, because presence everywhere dilutes the launch attention the strong lanes are spending to found the brand's name. The deck therefore executes a governed HOLD in an entering set: no skin lane ships in Y1; the audit's skin module rides the same census day anyway; two options are specified and priced CAR-style (a Vatika body lotion for the mass question; a Gulabari niche row for the diaspora's private rose-water practice — the rose instrument's new reading, below); and the activation door is the Y2 review, where hair's crossover read, oral's house read and OTC's three-lane read will say whether the brand's name has earned a fourth conversation. DEEP THINK · The objections, answered

"Holding skin in an ENTER market looks inconsistent — the macro said enter." The macro said enter the MARKET; the finding separates that from entering every category, and the three strong lanes are executing the macro's verdict at full commitment. The inconsistency would run the other way: a skin launch with no parcel proof, no diaspora pull and a sacred incumbent would spend the set's scarcest resource — the doors' and the town's attention at landing — on the portfolio's weakest case. Entering well IS the selective act; the prune is the entry strategy's sharpest edge, not its hesitation.

"The Gulabari niche row seems too small to specify at all. Why carry it?" Because it costs one table row and honours two instruments. The private-storehouse reading is new to the rose instrument's range, and the row is its operational form: IF the community's own pull surfaces (the swap rule's trigger), the spec is ready, priced and correctly doored — a week from decision to shelf. Small honest rows are how a programme serves small honest demand without inflating it into strategy; the alternative — ignoring a real community practice because it is small — would be the atlas's first act of rounding a community away.

"Two whole-category holds in three sets (CAR, here) — is the programme getting hold-happy?" The two holds share grammar and nothing else, and the contrast is the instrument's health check. CAR held skin because the market couldn't fund any entry (poverty, blindness, a fenced pocket unmapped); the Seychelles holds skin because the ENTRY is too valuable to dilute (wealth, focus, a sacred incumbent).
