Seychelles — OTC Health Care: Category Analysis & Entry Strategy

Where the local jar is sacred, the format is the door: honey-as-medicine belongs to the islands' own GI-bound jar — so Dabur's honey arrives inside a cough remedy the local chain doesn't make, and Chyawanprash walks the queue's first fully documented regulatory door

THE FORTY-THIRD CORRIDOR FINDING — REMEDY-FORMATTED. The Seychelles OTC file holds two assets no market in this queue has paired before: a living herbal culture — the «bonnonm dibwa» bush-healer tradition (CAPTURED as attested Kreol vocabulary) and the «tizann» infusion habit — and a fully documented regulatory door: the Ministry of Health's Medicines Regulatory Unit runs import permits through the Trade Division at Maison Esplanade, no licence fee, six-month permits renewable once, designated-port clearance (Trade Portal PDF — CAPTURED, Feb 2025). Between them sits the file's one sacred object: local honey, scarce, prized, Varroa-free, "possibly four times more medicinal" in the islands' own telling, and heading for the country's first Geographical Indication. Verdict #34 (the macro) already split the jars — the local jar keeps the medicinal crown; the imported jar takes the table — and this deck deposits the finding that completes the split: where a local ingredient owns its sacred meaning, the entrant carries that ingredient inside a FORMAT the local chain does not make. Honitus — honey made into a cough remedy, with herbs the tizann culture already respects — borrows the islands' honey-as-medicine belief WITHOUT contesting the jar that owns it: the local chain presses jars, not syrups, and a cough syrup's offer is its function, not its provenance. The deck's architecture follows: Honitus leads the remedy lane through Behram's counter (warmed by oral's kit); Chyawanprash walks the MoH door as the queue's first fully-papered flagship entry; the everyday table jar follows verdict #34's register-separation discipline — and the entire plan runs through the classification split (W0 #2) before anything health-claimed ships, because the friendliest door in the queue is still a door, and doors are walked in order.
