C2 · Demand architecture — dry country, saturated shelf, one gap

The need is not in question anywhere in this country: Namibia is the batch's driest market — high year-round UV, desert aridity, and a documented public-health sun-protection concern — so intensive skin care is a CLIMATE fact before it is a category. The shelf, however, is served: the four-jobs jar economy that defined Angola and Mozambique exists here too (the north's decants, the value tubs), but the salaried band's version is already branded — Vaseline for the base job, Ingram's for the functional-cream job, Nivea for the lotion job. The gap the claim architecture targets: NOTHING on the value shelf is herbal, and nothing speaks the intensive dry-climate register as its whole identity — Ingram's says camphor-heritage, Vaseline says everything-and-nothing, Payless says cheap. Herbolene's entry says herbs for hard weather (working form) — a functional difference the aisle can read at arm's length, priced INSIDE the value band it differentiates from. The north's version stays the batch's standard: the sealed value tub against the decant, wage-anchored, wave 3, Oshiwambo-labelled. And Gulabari's demand question is answered by the shelf itself: somebody already buys rose water at N$49 here; the entry's work is one facing, one price, one story better told.

The consumer decode in one scene — the winter wind and the shelf's answer

June in Windhoek: the east wind off the Kalahari drops humidity to single digits, and every skin in the city knows it — knuckles crack, heels split, lips go first. The Clicks shopper answering this is not experimenting; she is provisioning against weather, the way her mother did, with the blue tin for the base and Ingram's for the hands because that is what has always worked. Her purchase is a HABIT with a season attached — the hardest kind to interrupt and the easiest kind to ADD to, which is precisely the claim's design: not "replace what works" but "the herbal jar for the hard months", one more provisioning item in a ritual the climate renews every year. Up in Oshakati the same wind means the same cracked heels answered by the decant and the smallest tub — the wage board's version of the same provisioning logic. And in Kunene, the same climate is answered by a practice this deck honours by never mentioning it again after this sentence. One weather system, three answers, and the deck's whole architecture is knowing which two it may serve.

The worked example — the claim's read, run end to end

The claim's test inherits oral's machinery, so the worked example shows only what differs. Pre-launch: the value-shelf benchmark is pulled (comparable new value-tier listings' velocities, chain data), the ×0.7 factor applies, and the legibility test runs — twenty shoppers, arm's length, one question: "what does this jar do?" If fewer than fourteen answer with something like the claim, the pack redesigns BEFORE print, not after failure. Launch: one facing beside Vaseline, the talker up, the drift-checked tag. The read at M12: at or above benchmark, R2 unlocks and the claim has earned its band; below it, the ONE permitted re-brief runs — the claim restated, not the price dropped, because a price drop on a claim product concedes the claim — and a second miss triggers the pre-written retreat: the south goes quiet, the north's R4 proceeds on its own untouched logic, and the CLAIM-DIFFERENT posture's memo to Botswana carries the negative honestly. DEEP THINK · The claim, stress-tested against the shelf's silence. The deck's whole southern bet is that a CLAIM can earn ~40% over the base jar on a shelf where the two incumbents win by silence (Vaseline) and by memory (Ingram's). The honest case against: value shoppers at this tier buy by habit and price tag, and claims are premium-tier furniture. The case for, argued from the shelf itself: Ingram's IS a claim — camphor, functional, ninety years old — proving this exact shopper pays for a named function at value prices; the gap is that no one has named a NEW function since. The dry-climate register is that new function with the country's weather as its witness. And the test is cheap and bounded: two quarters, one benchmark, one permitted re-brief, and a pre-written retreat (the north's R4 survives any southern verdict, because its logic never depended on the claim). The deck bets the claim but budgets the silence.
DEEP STRAT · The set closed, the ledger written.
