THE BANNER. The question flagged in Italy — is Brazil's cronograma capilar the strongest bridge in the corridor series? — is answered: yes, and it is more than a bridge. The corridor diagnostic returns its twenty-fifth finding: RITUAL-KITTED. The cronograma capilar is a consumer-administered treatment CALENDAR (hydration → nutrition → reconstruction, cycled weekly) so culturally mature that incumbents sell it as kits with named steps — Novex's "Kit Cronograma Capilar Para Cacheadas" is the category's operating system rendered as a product. Italy's bridge was a claims vocabulary (translation the only toll); Spain's was a salon's calendar (the chair as gatekeeper); Brazil's is a NATIONAL SELF-ADMINISTERED RITUAL with named oil moments — umectação and pré-poo — currently served by kitchen oils (coco, rícino, abacate) and incumbent blends. RITUAL-KITTED corridors carry the series' friendliest prescription: the toll is SCHEDULING, not education — earn a named place in a calendar the shopper already keeps (amla as the umectação's new active, "o óleo novo do cronograma"), sell in the ritual's own formats and vocabulary, and let the novelty-hungry content culture (TikTok Shop Brasil: 102× first-year growth) do the introduction. Two cautions govern: the Lola bar — this market punishes cultural tourists, so nothing ships that reads as translated, and the creator lane is the fluency-acquisition machine before it is a sales channel; and the litigation fence's hair rule — Dabur touches NOTHING in the relaxer/progressiva/chemical-straightening class in Brazil, ever, under this programme (the Namaste docket + the formol history make that class radioactive twice over). The entry is the oil step, done fluently, at a founding's scale.

C4 · THE CORRIDOR DIAGNOSTIC — TWENTY-FIFTH FINDING: RITUAL-KITTED

The neighbours fail in an instructive order. DEEP THINK · Three objections answered

Objection 1 — "If the corridor is the series' deepest, why is the entry so small? The finding argues for ambition; the plan is a founding." Because depth of corridor and right-to-scale are different assets. The corridor guarantees that a FLUENT entrant with a real active gets carried; it guarantees nothing about an entrant's fluency, and the Lola bar says fluency here is the scarcest input an outsider can claim. The founding's size is the price of buying fluency honestly — a creator bench, a listening phase, kill numbers — before betting real capital. The corridor will still be there in eighteen months; a torched first impression would not be recoverable at any budget. Depth justifies ENTERING; only earned fluency justifies scaling.

Objection 2 — "Amla-as-new-active assumes the ritual wants new actives — maybe the kitchen oils persist because the step is deliberately cheap." Partly true, and priced: the R$34,90 sits close enough to kitchen arithmetic that the story only has to earn a R$20 gap, and the sensitivity table's contraction branch exists for the miss. But the evidence for ingredient-hunger is the incumbents' own behaviour — Novex sells baobab-story kits, Lola built R$500M partly on ingredient narratives, and the content culture's daily genre is literally testing new things on camera. The step has a cheap FLOOR and a story-priced CEILING already occupied by local brands; the question is whether amla's use-evidence clears the bar those brands set. That is a testable question, and the founding is its test.

Objection 3 — "The fence refuses the progressiva-aftercare play that Spain proved — isn't that leaving the directive's own instrument unused?" Deliberately, and the reasoning is stated rather than buried: Spain's aftercare screen found a real seat here (the progressiva economy's calendar has the same unbranded weeks), but the seat sits INSIDE the product class the litigation fence radioactivates — a Dabur oil marketed as straightening aftercare is a Dabur product marketed into the relaxer economy's orbit while the corporate family defends ~5,400 relaxer cases. The screen's finding is recorded, the file is dated, and the trigger is legal's — not marketing's. An instrument that finds a seat the fence forbids has still worked; knowing WHERE you refuse to sit is half of what the screens are for.
