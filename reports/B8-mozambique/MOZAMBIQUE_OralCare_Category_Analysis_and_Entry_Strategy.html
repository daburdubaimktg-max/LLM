C2 · Demand architecture — a low-caries country westernising its diet

The clinical evidence forces this deck to re-derive its health story rather than inherit one. Zambia's file ran on gingivitis prevalence; Angola's on 72.9% untreated caries; Mozambique's numbers say something different: DMFT is LOW by global standards — 0.60 at age 12, 1.94 even at 35–44 — and the gradient runs the telling direction: urban caries prevalence (1.89) significantly exceeds rural (1.33) multi-province WHO-methodology study, n=722; Maputo adolescent study DMFT 0.9 [!] publication dates confirmed at source. Read strategically: this is not a country drowning in untreated decay; it is a country whose teeth are still protected by a low-sugar rural diet and endangered exactly where incomes rise — Maputo first. The honest positioning is therefore not "distribution is the health intervention" (Zambia's law — true there, unearned here) but "keep what you have": an affordable everyday paste for a population whose oral health is an asset being slowly spent by urbanising diets. That framing suits a herbal value brand unusually well — protection and daily habit, no fear, no therapeutic theatre — and it survives the wage arithmetic: at the agri floor (MZN 6,688/month), a 45-MZN entry tube is 0.67% of monthly income, the most affordable considered purchase in the portfolio.

The unconfirmed seconds (Aquafresh/Sensodyne/Signal) — the map's soft cells. If the walk finds them, the mid band is crowded and Herb'l leans harder on the herbal story; if not, Mozambique is ANOTHER structurally-open #2 market like Angola read. Both branches pre-planned; neither assumed.

Meswak-in-South-Africa — the shadow sibling. Dabur's own Meswak already trades in SA retail (Nature's Pharmacy, Holistic Organix tier) — one corridor away. This is an asset and a hazard: the asset is proven regional supply; the hazard is UNGOVERNED arrival — a grey parcel of Meswak reaching Nampula before the four gates open would test the register law without a strategy behind it. The deck's answer: the grey-watch protocol (quarterly scan; the written no-fuel position on file — Angola's instrument, second use).

The chewing-stick tradition — the rural incumbent, respectfully read. Regional SSA literature assumes mswaki/stick use in rural areas; no Mozambique-specific evidence was captured [!] flagged proxy. The deck does not romanticise it, does not campaign against it, and does not claim it as heritage kinship (that would be the curandeiro fence's cousin) — it simply prices the slow lane so a first tube is affordable when a household chooses one.

Self-teardown — the second passenger, third market. Herb'l arrives with zero equity into the batch's hardest category and thinnest wallet — every reason to ride, not lead: the door's shelves, hair's route decision at M9–12, the same relay economics Zambia designed and Angola re-ran (index target ~55%, still a measured hypothesis — this becomes instance THREE, and the B8-SUMMARY gets three data points instead of two). Failure shapes: (i) the wallet fails oral specifically (mitigation: oral's rungs are the portfolio's cheapest absolute prices); (ii) Colgate's rebuilt ladder leaves no honest notch (mitigation: the herbal story differentiates where price cannot); (iii) the north file leaks early via grey Meswak (mitigation: the watch protocol + gates).

The consumer decode in one scene — the tube that waits for month-end

A Matola household, last week of the month. The Colgate tube — the small one — has been rolled from the bottom for days, and the decision it represents is not whether to brush but WHEN to buy: this week if the machamba's greens sold well, next week if not. Her mother, in the village, brushed with a stick her whole life and kept most of her teeth; her own children, raised nearer the city on sweeter bread and Fanta, will not be so lucky — the urban-rural caries gradient in the national study is this family, measured. When she does buy, she buys at the banca whose owner she knows, from a carton that came through a Baixa wholesaler, and she can feel by weight whether a tube is honest. Into this scene the deck's design speaks precisely: a 50g tube priced inside the week-she-can, a yield card that respects arithmetic she already runs, a herbal clause that names its leaves without promising miracles, and — up in Nampula, where the same scene ends with different prayers — a file that refuses to address her neighbour's register until it can do so with a certificate rather than a costume. Nothing in this deck is cleverer than this scene; it is all engineering around it.

The channel doctrine's quiet corollary

One operational note deserves its own paragraph because three future files will reuse it. Because the door's wholesale layer already serves the 63 markets, oral's street activation is not "build a vendor network" (Zambia's cost) or "enter the trays railed" (Angola's) but simply "add a carton to an existing delivery" — the cheapest street entry the programme has designed anywhere. The corollary: the vendor RELATIONSHIP belongs to the wholesaler, not to Dabur, which means the tray-economics audit (spread ≥ her Colgate margin) must be verified through the wholesale price sheet rather than assumed from the shelf price — a one-page addendum to the door agreement, drafted at W0, or the launch's cheapest leg becomes its blindest.

C16 · What does not transplant

Angola's fluoride COMMANDMENT arrives in lighter clothing — the caries context differs (72.9% untreated there, low DMFT here) so the gate keeps its timing (before print) and loses its alarm; method constant, evidence different, posture adjusted. Angola's published-ladder luxury does not exist here — the ladder must be WALKED before it can be designed into, which reorders the W0 fortnight. Zambia's citizenship-order transplants whole. The GCC Meswak playbooks still do not transplant — what crosses the border is not a playbook but a QUESTION, processed through four gates; if the file opens, its execution will be designed fresh for Nampula, not copied from Dubai. And the deck flags one export the batch has not seen: the REGISTER-SEPARATION rule (two open columns, never blurred) — Namibia and Botswana won't need it, but the programme's remaining Muslim-minority markets will.

DEEP THINK · The gated file tested against both its critics. The critic on the left says: gating Meswak is cowardice — the north is Muslim-majority NOW, the certifier exists NOW, and a competitor (or the grey market) can serve the register while Dabur scores gates. The critic on the right says: the file shouldn't exist — the register law said Herb'l for Christian-majority Africa, Mozambique is 69% Christian, done. The deck answers both with the same fact: REGISTERS ARE REGIONAL HERE. The right critic's blanket rule would have the programme treat Niassa's 61% Muslim majority as a rounding error — analytically false and commercially wasteful. The left critic's speed would ship a faith-register product without external certification, correct channels or a security review — the exact register incident the programme has spent ten markets refusing to cause. The gates are not hesitation; they are what taking BOTH populations seriously looks like in paperwork. And the file's dormant-brief discipline (nothing drafted before gate 2) is the enforcement mechanism: a strategy that cannot ship early because it does not yet exist.
DEEP STRAT · The worked launch — one fortnight, third catalogue, first column. M12, assuming the read-outs land clean. The whole fortnight's incremental infrastructure cost: printing, freight allocation, one buyer lunch. That is the relay's third instance as lived detail — and the reason its measured index matters enough to report three times: if ~50–55 holds here, in the batch's poorest market, the B8-SUMMARY can write the relay into the programme's standing method with numbers behind it, and every future country set prices its second, third and fourth categories at the passenger rate by default.
