MOZAMBIQUE · MACRO — THE WARM DOOR AND THE SECOND ENGINE

A 300-year-old Indian trading community, a policy-stabilised metical, ZaZiBoNa paper and the poorest wallet in the programme — the Lusophone twin-engine's second cylinder, which turns out to be a different machine · All MZN numbers at ~64 MZN/USD plan band 64–72 unless flagged · Portuguese copy panel-gated (panel SHARED with Angola) · 6 July 2026
FOCUS · One line: assess Mozambique as Dabur's fifth Batch-8 entry — a market whose door is the warmest in Africa (Maputo's Gujarati/Ismaili merchant class has run urban commerce for a century) and whose wallet is the thinnest in the programme (~US$623 GDP/capita, ~95% informal employment), where the winning shape is warm-door-first, notification-track wellness, third-lane oils, a gated northern Meswak question the programme has never faced before, and a 12th honey verdict no prior market produced.

M1 · Demography — young, rural, plural, and further from Lisbon than Luanda is

Population ~36.6M (2026 projection off the 2017 census — the constant is SOFT and the file plans mid-band [!] INE census 2027 release = re-validation #1), median age 16.6 — statistically the programme's youngest tie with Angola — but only ~42% urban against Angola's ~68%: Mozambique is a MAJORITY-RURAL market and will stay one for decades (ISS African Futures models ~49% urban only by 2043). The single metro-scale demand cluster is Maputo–Matola, realistically 2.5–3M combined; Nampula (~1.1M) anchors the north, Beira (~0.7M) the centre city-proper vs metro definitions vary materially [!] INE ground truth. Language is layered: Portuguese official but a minority first language; Emakhuwa (north, ~26%), Changana/Tsonga family (south), Sena/Ndau (centre) carry daily life — a national campaign in Portuguese alone under-covers the rural majority, and the shared Angola panel therefore gets a Mozambican EXTENSION brief (Changana + Emakhuwa) rather than a copy-paste ethnolinguistic map, standard references, Jul 2026.

Religion is the batch's new variable: Christian ~68.6%, Muslim ~21.3% (Afrobarometer 2025) — but the split is a MAP, not a blend: Niassa ~61% and Cabo Delgado ~54% Muslim-majority, Nampula ~40%, while the south and centre are Christian-majority. For the first time in Batch 8, halal is commercially REAL — regionally: a functioning national certifier exists (Comissão Halal de Moçambique, est. 2005), a Muslim radio station is nameable (Rádio A Voz do Islão), and the Meswak question reopens under strict re-derivation rules (O-audit). Traditional healing is institutionalised at a scale no prior market showed: AMETRAMO, the government-recognised curandeiro association, counts 20,000+ registered members of an estimated 70,000+ practitioners, with 9,000+ integrated into official health committees AIM News Sep 2024; academic sources — the fence law applies absolutely, and this file adds the refusal in advance: the research itself suggested an "Ayurveda-as-natural-medicine bridge", and the programme's answer is NO — the fence exists precisely because the resonance is real (M5).

M2 · Economy — the thinnest wallet, the steadiest currency

GDP per capita ~US$623 nominal (2025) — the programme's poorest market, full stop; ~US$1,495 PPP softens the reading, not the shelf mathematics. Inflation closed 2025 at ~3.2–4.4% and is climbing gently (April 2026 print 4.41% on floods and fuel) with the policy rate at 9.25% BoM Mar 2026; Club of Mozambique; Trading Economics [!] latest print before pricing. The metical sits at ~MZN 63.9/USD, policy-stabilised since mid-2021 — the anti-Angola: no parallel market crisis, no import-dollar queue found in public sources — but the stability is ADMINISTERED, not structural: the metical shed ~48% over 2014–24, the IMF flags 2026 devaluation risk tied to LNG-linked fiscal financing, the prior IMF programme lapsed April 2025 with no successor confirmed, and the Mozal smelter's loss strains the current account IMF Art. The file therefore plans at a 64–72 MZN/USD band with a written repricing trigger — lighter machinery than Angola's council, same discipline. M3 · Market access — no forex gate, a corridor question instead

Mozambique's access architecture inverts Angola's. FX: no acute scarcity found — the entry does NOT need a local-invoicing partner for currency reasons (it still wants a strong partner for channel reasons — M7). Ports: THREE real gateways — Maputo (south + SA corridor), Beira (centre + Zimbabwe/Malawi/Zambia transit), Nacala (north/Nampula, deep-water) — and the choice is strategic, not logistical trivia: the warm-door community concentrates in Maputo and Nampula, which argues Maputo-first with a Nacala option when the north opens. Duties: consumer goods commonly ~20% + 16% VAT, cosmetics carrying a Specific Consumption Tax (ICE) as "non-essential"; critically, SADC-origin goods enter preferentially while India/UAE-origin do not — so a South-Africa repack/blend step could legally cut landed cost versus direct RAK shipment, a supply-chain option NO prior batch market offered trade.gov tariff guide; PwC [!] HS-line verification for toothpaste/ hair oil/honey/supplements = W0 pull #2; SA-repack economics = a named study, not an assumption. Pre-shipment inspection is mandatory. The Cabo Delgado insurgency closes that province to any commercial plan in this file's horizon — the security fence is absolute and stated once.

M4 · Regulation — ANARME's split path, and paper that may already exist

The regulator is ANARME (Autoridade Nacional Reguladora de Medicamentos, under Lei 12/2017), ISO 9001:2015-certified and aligned to the SADC regulatory strategy. The commercially decisive finding: ANARME runs a SPLIT PATH — medicines and herbal products require full registration (AIM), while cosmetics, nutritional supplements, disinfectants and devices go through a lighter "notificação" (notification) pathway per the 2008/ 2009/2017 ministerial dispatches ANARME indexed content; MTaPS — the exact supplement/herbal line is THE swing fact [!] manual ANARME pull = W0 task #1. If Chyawanprash and the supplement line ride the notification track, Mozambique's OTC opens QUARTERS earlier than Angola's — the two Lusophone files' opposite OTC verdicts (wait vs fast-follow) hang on this one classification. Pharmacy retail was re-regulated in 2025 (Resolução 18/2025: Type A/B/C licensing, mandatory Director Técnico — a pharmacist-of-record, same structural requirement as Angola's). Mozambique appears on ZaZiBoNa's active-participant lists (with ANARME as representative) — meaning Zambia's dossier discipline and possibly its assessments carry here — but sources hedge between active and non-active status PMC7704514; TMDA [!] the batch's highest-leverage regulatory confirm: one email to ANARME. Counterfeit pressure is real (Operation Afya seizures; 9,000 counterfeit cough-syrup bottles in one action) though the environment reads less totalising than Angola's 50% estimate — rails remain a launch precondition, now programme-standard Interpol 2021–23.

M5 · Health & culture — malaria's country, AMETRAMO's country

Mozambique carries ~3.5% of global malaria cases with childhood prevalence near 40% — health here MEANS malaria first, and immunity/recovery framing lands on lived experience. HIV prevalence ~12.5% (INSIDA 2021). Diabetes DOUBLED between STEPS waves (2.9% in 2005 → 7.4% in 2014/15, ages 25–64) while awareness and treatment FELL — a fast-growing, under-managed NCD base PMC9701039 [!] check for a ~2024/25 third wave. The cultural system this file must respect: the curandeiro/nyanga world is not informal here — it is ASSOCIATED, carded, and integrated into health committees (AMETRAMO, M1). The macro researcher's own note suggested Ayurveda could ride this "natural medicine" trust as a message bridge; the programme's law says the opposite and this file states it as Mozambique's first commandment: the deeper the traditional-medicine system, the higher the fence — Dabur positions as internationally manufactured, quality-assured, REGISTERED wellness, distinct from and never comparable to curandeiro practice; no imagery, vocabulary, or event adjacency, ever. Beauty norms carry TWO pulls: Brazilian telenovelas (since 1986; salon clients citing novela looks — the Angola mirror's cultural half exists here) and South African retail gravity (Shoprite assortment) — but crucially, the Brazilian RETAIL infrastructure that made Angola's code commercially ownable does NOT exist here: no Skala/Salon Line shelf presence confirmed. The code is in the air, not on the shelf — a different strategic object (O-audit).

M7 · Retail — Shoprite's spine, Recheio's catalogue, and 63 markets in one city

Modern trade: Shoprite (since 1997; Maputo, Beira, Xai-Xai, Chimoio, Nampula, Dondo, Macia — the only confirmed multi-province chain), Recheio Cash & Carry (self-described largest grocery operation, claiming 400k+ monthly online shoppers [!] self-reported, with the catalogue whose captured SKUs anchor half this file), Spar via Premier franchise (two Maputo SuperSpars confirmed). E-commerce splits INVERSELY to Angola: here GROCERY e-commerce (Recheio, Shoprite online, izyshop, Bazara) is more mature than pharmacy e-commerce (folded into multi-category apps: Moza Go, Mozabuy, Trago, Deliva, Kwika) — so honey and skin launch digitally through grocery rails while wellness waits for its paper. Pharmacies: state-owned FARMAC (28 or 40 stores — sources conflict [!]) explicitly retails vitamins and supplements; private chains Farmácia Calêndula and MEDIPLUS are Maputo-anchored. And beneath all of it, the real market: 63 markets in Maputo city alone (33 informal), the dumba-nengue street economy, ~70% of Maputo households active in informal trade — the batch's most informal-dominant channel structure ResearchGate/academic; Trade.gov FMCG guide.

The consumer decode in one story — a Xipamanine Saturday

A Saturday at Xipamanine, Maputo's great informal market. A mother moves through stalls where ~70% of the city's households earn or spend: she buys a palm of loose coconut oil decanted into her own bottle for her daughter's tranças next week; a Colgate tube — the smallest — from a stall whose supply came through a Baixa wholesaler whose grandfather came from Diu; honey she skips this month (the branded jar at Recheio is a town trip and a splurge; the roadside bottle is cheaper but she trusts it less since her neighbour's was watered); and for the baby's dry skin, the blue tin everyone uses. On the minibus home a TikTok plays braids content from Brazil dubbed in Portuguese; at home the novela runs on Miramar; if the baby's cough worsens she will visit the curandeiro her family has used for twenty years, and the clinic only if he says so. Every element of this file is in that Saturday: the informal channel's totality, the Indian wholesale layer already inside the supply chain, the unbranded oil the third lane must beat on trust, the honey trust-gap a sealed jar answers, the media that costs nothing, and the healing world the brand must never touch. The decks that follow are that Saturday, engineered.

M11 · Decision board, wave design, what does not transplant

DEEP THINK · The twin-engine premise, tested and revised. Angola's DEEP STRAT designed a Lusophone twin engine — one partner structure, one panel, one register doctrine for two countries. The Mozambican evidence keeps the shared ASSETS and kills the shared ENGINE: the two markets are complements, not twins. Angola has the code but no warm door; Mozambique has the warm door but no code infrastructure. Angola's OTC waits on an opaque regulator; Mozambique's likely fast-follows through a notification lane. Angola needed a partner for CURRENCY reasons; Mozambique needs one for CHANNEL reasons. Forcing one structure across both would replay the transplant error the programme exists to prevent — inside its own machinery. The revision, stated once for the B8-SUMMARY: the twin engine becomes a shared toolkit — the PT panel (extended with Changana/Emakhuwa), the private law (verbatim), the register doctrine (adapted), the classification reasoning (transferred, re-filed) — bolted onto two separately-shaped entries. What survives of the twin premise is exactly what was cheap to share; what died was what was expensive to force.

Reading the file as one sentence

Mozambique is the programme's purest white space behind its warmest door, wrapped in its thinnest wallet — a market entered house by house through a century-old trading network, priced at the batch's lowest ladder, papered through a notification lane its Lusophone twin never had, and fenced three ways in a country where the healing world is an institution, the lightening lane has no law, and the door itself must never become the advertisement.

The bet's shape, before its text.
