C3 · Brand-by-brand teardown — five entries, one self-read

1 · The US import wave — the real incumbent

The category's growth engine is American: Solgar with an official-representative storefront (~1% share, +67%), California Gold, NOW Foods, Natrol, Solaray moving through the specialist e-pharmacy layer pharmnewskz; evitamin/biotus listings. Teardown lesson: the aspirational register here is LAB-SCIENCE AMERICAN, not Russian-natural (Evalar's register, unconfirmed here anyway) — and Chyawanprash cannot and should not out-lab it. The flank is the one the US brands cannot claim: TRADITION with a local bridge (Ibn Sina), a FORMAT no capsule matches (the daily family spoon), and per-ritual economics that undercut a Solgar regimen honestly. The US wave also proves the big fact: Uzbek pharmacy shoppers PAY for imported wellness — the willingness is evidenced, the register is contestable.

2 · The specialist e-pharmacy layer — the crowded shelf that isn't a rival

Evitamin, Biotus, Vitaline, PharmaClick: a dense online-vitamin sub-segment that looks like competition and is actually INFRASTRUCTURE — multi-brand shelves hungry for assortment, the exact channel Chyawanprash's existing sellers already use. Lesson: these are listing targets, not competitors; the entry's e-commerce motion is a six-account sweep (the hair deck's mesh logic) through doors that are already open to imported wellness.

3 · Evalar — the incumbent that didn't show

The batch's standing БАД benchmark has NO confirmed Uzbek storefront — a striking absence given its KZ depth [!] likely via general importers. The empty aisle's discipline — what leading permits and what it doesn't

One paragraph on posture before the decode, because an empty aisle tempts a specific arrogance. Leading means Dabur writes the category's price architecture, its claims vocabulary, its shelf placement logic — the privileges this deck exercises. It does NOT mean the market owes the category attention: with no rival spending against the same aisle, every som of category education comes from Dabur's own line, and the aisle can stay empty of CUSTOMERS as easily as of competitors. The discipline: the entry rides EXISTING demand vectors (the pharmacy listings' proven buyers, the import-wave's evidenced willingness, the Ramadan gifting current) rather than manufacturing new ones, and the media plan's patience — long-form heritage content compounding slowly — is matched to a category that must grow at the trust channel's pace, not at a launch calendar's. Empty aisles reward the patient and bankrupt the theatrical; the deck budgets like the former.
