C3 · Brand-by-brand teardown — five entries, one self-read

1 · Burdock oil — the anchor, third market, cheapest edition

The Slavic slot exists here as everywhere post-Soviet, at the batch's lowest absolute prices (~13k/50ml). The inherited frame runs unchanged for the apteka shopper. What's new: at THIS wallet, burdock's cheapness is a harder ceiling — the ×2.5–3.5 multiple that worked in Russia and Kazakhstan translates to a per-ml discipline here (the 200ml at 55–62k is ×4.5–5 per bottle but ×2.2–2.5 per ml against burdock's 50ml) — the deck prices per-ml and says so on the card, because the value-literate shopper does the division anyway.

2 · The usma tradition — the native incumbent that isn't a competitor

Usma is the deck's deepest asset: a living practice (fresh leaf juice, family-taught), a commercial register (manufactured usma oils on Uzum), and — the finding that shapes the entry — an ingredient VATIKA ALREADY USES. The teardown lesson mirrors burdock's from Russia but goes further: Dabur doesn't just join this belief, it can legitimately claim membership in it, because the formulation is real. The conduct bar: usma belongs to Uzbek women's culture, not to Dabur — the register is "Vatika honours the usma tradition your family taught you," never "Vatika improves it"; the double gate tests exactly this line in both scripts.

3 · The Russian/CIS mass tier — the incumbent shelf

Russian mass brands and Turkish-manufactured lines hold the modern shelf's middle without a single named champion in the evidence [!] Euromonitor paywall. Lesson: the mid is CONTESTED but UNOWNED — no L'Oréal-subsidiary fortress (Kazakhstan), no distracted champion (Russia). For a patient value-builder, an unowned middle in a 39M-person market is the batch's best volume geometry.

4 · Parachute — the scout on Asaxiy

Marico's coconut oil is listed on Asaxiy — the only confirmed Indian-oil rival presence asaxiy listing. Lesson (third market): Parachute's single-ingredient commodity story travels ahead of brand-building everywhere — and its presence VALIDATES the Indian-oil proposition at Uzbek prices while leaving the branded, multi-benefit, heritage lane empty. The G1 read prices the validation.

5 · Himalaya — absent, and the absence audited

No Himalaya hair listing was found on any Uzbek platform — the macro's lead extends to this category, and the macro's three-hypothesis discipline (true lead / indexing mirage / rational abstention) travels with it. The hair-specific note: Himalaya's anti-hairfall oil was the contested cell's incumbent in Kazakhstan; here the cell is EMPTY, which means Amla's Uzum listings — if the census confirms them healthy — are the category's only Ayurvedic-oil incumbency. Defending an empty cell is cheaper than contesting one: the deck's tempo argument.

The two anchors, reconciled — why this deck prices off burdock and localises off usma

A design note the two-culture shelf forces: the deck uses DIFFERENT traditions for its two core jobs, and the separation is deliberate. PRICING anchors on burdock because burdock is a PRICED, pharmacy-shelved, per-ml-legible commodity — the shopper's arithmetic reference — while usma's fresh form has no stable retail price and its manufactured form is too new to anchor anything. LOCALISATION anchors on usma because usma carries the MEANING — family, inheritance, identity — that burdock, an imported Soviet-era commodity, never had. Getting this backwards would be expensive both ways: pricing off usma would build on sand; localising off burdock would speak Russian to a heart that remembers Uzbek. The two-anchor rule (price off the priced tradition, localise off the loved one) is the deck's cleanest generalisable lesson, and it travels to every two-culture market the batch has left.

The remittance calendar, priced into the plan

One operational novelty the decode surfaced deserves its mechanics spelled out: Fergana-belt purchasing power arrives MONTHLY WITH THE TRANSFERS, not with local paydays — and a Moscow construction season means the flow itself is seasonal (strong spring-autumn, thinner deep winter). For a value-tier oil whose buyer stretches for it, promo timing against the transfer rhythm beats promo timing against retail convention: the pharmacy-floor promotions and the bazaar cargo's push weeks should track remittance peaks (and the ruble's health), while the Tashkent/Uzum calendar runs on ordinary retail seasonality. The deck flags this as a HYPOTHESIS with a cheap test — two promo cycles timed each way in matched pharmacy clusters, one quarter, till data — because the programme has never run a remittance-timed trade calendar before, and if it works here it travels to Kyrgyzstan (remittance-geared harder still) with evidence instead of theory.

The usma conversation, worked — what the inheritor panel actually hears

The membership gate's step 2, staged concretely so its stakes are visible. A rented room in Andijan, eight women across three generations; on the table, a fresh usma leaf, a bottle of the manufactured usma oil from Uzum, and the Vatika SKU whose INCI verified in step 1. The moderator — a local researcher, not a Dabur employee — starts with THEIR practice: who taught you, when do you use it, what does the fresh juice do that matters. Only after the tradition has been described by its owners does the Vatika bottle enter, with one honest sentence: this international product contains usma extract, and the company wants to know whether saying so respectfully is welcome or not. Then the moderator reads the three candidate lines — the membership line, the heritage-respect line, the plain functional line — in both scripts, and the women mark each: fine / uncomfortable / offensive, with the why recorded verbatim. Their verbatims, not the scores, decide: if the discomfort clusters around OWNERSHIP ("this is ours, why is a company saying it"), the membership register dies regardless of averages; if it clusters around FRESHNESS ("extract isn't usma"), the copy pivots to honesty about the modern form; if the room warms ("my daughter won't sit squeezing leaves — good that something keeps it alive"), the register has its licence. Eight women, one afternoon, repeated once in Tashkent — and the batch's most celebrated finding either earns its copy or teaches the programme why it shouldn't.

The small-pack architecture, argued in place. Move 4's value rung deserves its arithmetic shown, because it is the deck's answer to the wallet and the batch has not needed it since Africa. A 45ml Amla at ~16,000–18,000 сум sits INSIDE the value band (15–25k), reads per-ml at roughly ×1.4–1.6 against burdock's 50ml — a stretch the dorixona mother can justify on a strong remittance month — and converts the trial barrier from a 58,000-сум commitment into a pocket decision. A value rung that is asked to be profitable AND to recruit is being asked to fail at both; the deck asks it to recruit.

DEEP THINK · Membership as a strategy, examined before it is celebrated. The Vatika-usma finding is the kind that wins internal applause — a true formulation claim meeting a living tradition — and precisely therefore it needs the cold reading. Three ways it fails. APPROPRIATION READ: the inheritor cohort hears a foreign company claiming their grandmother's practice — the gate exists for this, and its 12-of-15 threshold is deliberately high. DILUTION READ: usma's power is FRESHNESS (the leaf, the season, the ritual); a shelf-stable extract may read as the tradition's ghost, not its member — the panel probes this specifically, and the fallback register (heritage-respect without membership) is pre-written. TRIVIALITY READ: the extract is real but minor in the formulation — then the claim is technically true and commercially hollow, and the formulation verification (step 1) exists to catch exactly the gap between INCI presence and honest prominence. If all three reads clear, the register ships as the batch's best localisation; if any fails, the deck loses a headline and keeps its integrity — the trade the programme makes every time.

The Bollywood halo, sized precisely. One asset the macro documented deserves its hair-specific sizing: the durable Indian-cinema affection gives Dabur brand-India WARMTH no Turkish or Korean rival owns — but warmth is a door-opener, not a purchase driver, and the deck sizes it accordingly. Where it works: the brand story's opening beat in long-form content (the YouTube heritage film, the Telegram channel's origin posts), where "the India you already love" lowers the guard for the functional argument that follows. Where it fails: as a purchase reason on a card — nobody oils their hair out of nostalgia for Raj Kapoor. The halo therefore gets content-budget presence and zero claims-space, and the LEAP table's 13/20 score is the discipline made visible: two full points below the functional leads, exactly where a halo belongs.

DEEP STRAT · The two-culture shelf as the batch's preview. Uzbekistan's hair file is the first place the batch's two inheritances — the Slavic-CIS commercial layer and the Turkic-Islamic cultural layer — stand on ONE shelf at equal strength, and the deck's answer (run both registers, never blend them, gate the native one harder) previews the design problem the batch's remaining files will meet in different mixes: Kyrgyzstan more Slavic-layered, Azerbaijan more Turkic, the Caucasus pair differently again. The never-bundle rule, born in Kazakhstan's oral file for faith-and-function, generalizes here to CULTURES: a brand that speaks two traditions speaks them separately, each to its own audience, each through its own gate — and the shelf where they meet (the pharmacy counter, the Uzum card) lets the SHOPPER choose which conversation to have. That is the architecture the batch carries forward.

Programme perspective. The 108th file adds an instrument the programme has needed since Batch 8's Africa: a formal method for the moment a brand's OWN product already participates in a local tradition. The programme has honoured traditions from outside (the otjize standing negative, the healer-world fences, the banya guest rules) and borrowed adjacent beliefs carefully (beside-burdock, the two-traditions-agreeing frame); the membership register is the third posture — a claim of participation, available only when the formulation makes it TRUE, and gated by the tradition's own inheritors rather than by the brand's enthusiasm. The four-step gate (verify, cohort, double gate, standing bar) is deliberately heavier than anything the programme asks of an ordinary claim, because the failure mode — a multinational wearing a grandmother's ritual as packaging copy — is the exact class of harm the conduct doctrines exist to prevent. If the gate clears here, the instrument travels wherever Dabur's formulations genuinely meet native ingredients; if it fails here, the failure is cheap, recorded, and more valuable than a lucky pass.
