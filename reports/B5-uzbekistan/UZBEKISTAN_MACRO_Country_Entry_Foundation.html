M1 · Demography — the batch's youngest, densest, most traditional market

Uzbekistan is Central Asia's demographic centre: 38.24M (administrative, 1 Jan 2026) against a 39.05M preliminary first-census figure (Feb 2026) Stat.uz; Gazeta/Spot.uz Jun-Jul 2026 [!] final census release governs; median age ~27–28 (~46% under 25) — younger even than Kazakhstan and a full generation younger than Russia. Urbanisation is genuinely CONTESTED between definitions (47.9% vs ~37–40%) [!] census breakdown resolves — and the uncertainty matters, because the population's centre of gravity is not a metro belt but the Fergana Valley (Fergana + Andijan + Namangan; Fergana province alone ~4.06M at 613/km²) plus Samarkand province (~4.21M): dense, traditional, observant, bazaar-served agrarian regions. Tashkent (~3.24M) is the only true modern-retail-plus-e-commerce market. Language is a TRI-SCRIPT problem unique in the batch: Uzbek is the sole state language, historically Cyrillic, officially Latin since 1993 with the transition accelerated in 2025 (state media fully Latin from Oct-2025); Russian remains the practical retail/e-commerce default on shelf today Caspian Policy Center; retail photography evidence. The pack rule this file adopts: Russian + Latin-Uzbek as the near-term default, Cyrillic-Uzbek fallback for rural/older/Fergana bazaar trade — and every Uzbek line through the double gate in BOTH scripts.

M3 · Entry lane — the national build, priced honestly

The lane's governing fact: Uzbekistan is an EAEU observer, not a member — its parliament concluded in 2024 that observer status "best serves" the country — so the EAC declarations and СГР certificates the batch built for Russia and inherited in Kazakhstan are not valid here. Everything registers nationally: cosmetics through the O'zstandart/Technical-Regulation-Agency conformity system (regmed.uz handling cosmetics registration), supplements through the Ministry of Health's 45-working-day БАД permit (Resolution 131/2016), medicines — not Dabur's lane, but the ecosystem's tone-setter — through UzPharm-Control's ~155-day cycle with new mutual-recognition fast tracks lex.uz/regmed/uzpharm-control Jul-2026. Three lane facts sharpen the design. FIRST, the grey door is legally CLOSED: personal-import caps since 20 July 2025 (4 jars of capsules, 0.5kg powders, 3 perfume bottles per crossing) mean diaspora/parallel channels cannot scale — commercial registration is the only route, which conveniently is also the route where Dabur's institutional advantages live. SECOND, India's standing is the batch's warmest: a structured India-Uzbekistan pharma/nutraceutical corridor launched 2025–26 framed around a $7–10bn Eurasian health market, India already supplying ~12% of imported medicines, and Uzbekistan targeting $10bn domestic pharma output by 2035 dunyo.info/eurasiatoday — regulators here are COMFORTABLE with Indian dossiers in a way no other batch market has evidenced. THIRD, duties: India pays MFN rates (the CIS FTA excludes India-origin goods) and this pass could not pull the exact ad-valorem line — [!] tarif.customs.uz pull is W0 work.

M5 · Health & culture — the strongest bridge in the programme

Three cultural facts make Uzbekistan Dabur's most naturally receptive batch market, and one binds its conduct. FIRST, Ibn Sina (Avicenna) was born near Bukhara (~980 CE) — the Canon of Medicine's author is a source of NATIONAL PRIDE, actively curated (Bukhara's medical institute, heritage tourism), and his herbal-humoral medicine is a genuine intellectual sibling of Ayurveda's framework. "A sister tradition to Ibn Sina's medicine" is the strongest indigenous-heritage bridge available to Dabur anywhere in this programme — and it must be built respectfully: the panel's tone gate applies, and the contested rose-attar-invention lore stays OUT of copy (flagged legend-adjacent by the research) centralasia.club/ziyouz/aromo Jul-2026. SECOND, Bollywood affinity is durable and evidenced — 200+ Indian films imported to the USSR vs 41 American (1954–91), the Tashkent festival's Bollywood tradition, and 2020s sources confirming the affection outlasted the Union — the India-familiarity question Kazakhstan's file flagged as a gap is answered YES here. THIRD, living traditional-medicine culture: tabib healers, herbal remedies, and the usma/basma/henna beauty tradition (the hair deck's lever). The CONDUCT bind: ~90%+ Muslim and more observant than Kazakhstan means the TRIPLE GATE binds harder, the Miswak/honey religious registers carry more weight AND more risk, and one specific bar is recorded now: исирик/harmal (Peganum harmala) fumigation is live folk practice whose alkaloids are banned as psychotropics in three neighbouring states — no Dabur asset ever references it; the receptivity it signals is claimed through safe registers only.

The remittance household, decoded

One consumer portrait the batch has not met before, and the file's wallet math lives in it: a Namangan household whose eldest son works construction outside Moscow. His transfers land monthly through the banking apps; the family's discretionary spending breathes with the ruble and with Russian migration policy; the mother shops the bazaar weekly and the dorixona when someone is unwell; the daughter studies in Tashkent and orders from Uzum. This household holds all three of the file's channels in one kitchen — bazaar, pharmacy, marketplace — and its brand decisions run on TRUST FIRST, price-per-use second, novelty a distant third. A Dabur jar that earns the dorixona's nod and survives the bazaar's price scrutiny reaches her; a marketplace-only launch reaches only the daughter. And the volatility is structural: a ruble shock or a Russian migration clampdown tightens Fergana's wallets within a quarter — which is why the T3 dashboard carries remittances as a demand-volatility line and the entry's forecasts run scenarios, not points.
