WATCH-FRAME (binding — read with the macro). Sudan is in the fourth year of civil war (SAF vs RSF, since April 2023). The country-level verdict is WATCH + pre-position for recovery, not a live launch; this deck is a recovery-window readiness assessment. The OTC/pharma system is war-broken (essential-medicine availability ~45%; median prices 39–187× international reference; medicine reserves under 20% of pre-war; >90% imported), and much of the formal chain has been replaced by a smuggling economy. A formally-registered launch is not viable now. Every "first move" is a no-regret pre-position, and the macro's reputation/FX gates apply in full — plus a category-specific one: the gum-arabic sourcing red flag (a live war-finance commodity, RSF-controlled — kept at arm's length here).
THE ASK: OTC is a self-treatment economy by necessity — with the formal system collapsed, Sudanese self-treat coughs, digestion, immunity and pain with honey (عسل), senna (سنامكي), black seed (حبة البركة), hibiscus (karkadeh) and gum arabic, inside a ~97% Muslim, tibb-nabawi (Prophetic-medicine, طب نبوي) belief frame that makes herbal self-care religiously sanctioned, not fringe — and the war is deepening the habit (91.2% of hypertensives use herbs; ~20% started during the crisis; ~22% who couldn't reach a clinic turned to herbs). That makes Sudan arguably more defensible for herbal OTC than most Dabur markets. And Sudan is not just a market — it is the naturals source: the world's dominant gum-arabic supplier (~80%), a top-3 senna exporter, the origin of karkadeh, and a frankincense (luban / Boswellia) source. Dabur's portfolio maps onto this with unusual precision — Dabur Honey + Honitus (honey cough) + a senna/mint digestive (Hajmola/Pudin Hara) as the Tier-1 trio, Chyawanprash and Boswellia/Shallaki as education-led Tier-2/3. But the real incumbent is the loose, unbranded attarin (عطّارة) herbalist trade + local honey — cheap, trusted, war-resilient — so Dabur's wedge is branded trust + purity/testing credibility (adulteration is endemic) at a sachet price. Frame it "natural herbal halal, Sunnah-aligned," never "Ayurveda." Recovery-window verdict (macro T14): BUILD. Halal is the total baseline; keep gum-arabic sourcing strictly arm's-length until it can be certified conflict-free.

C1 — The ask (executive summary)

Macro anchor (§M6, §M11, T14): OTC is ranked BUILD for the recovery window — the deepest latent demand (a religiously- sanctioned herbal self-care system + the naturals heartland) but the heaviest-gated (NMPB registration; smuggling-based supply). Enter via the RAK → Port Sudan lane through a pharma-wholesale importer + the attarin lane; halal-total; USD/hawala; Honey + Honitus + senna-digestive Tier-1 trio; keep gum arabic at arm's length.

The play in one screen. Three truths make OTC a recovery-window build. First, the belief system is already Dabur's and it is strengthening: Sudanese self-treat with herbs because the formal system has collapsed, and they frame health through tibb nabawi — honey is Qur'anic (Surah An-Nahl), black seed is "a cure for everything but death," senna and hibiscus are everyday remedies, and the attarin herbalist is the de-facto pharmacist across the interior. The war has actively deepened this habit. Dabur's honey, honey-cough Honitus, senna/mint digestives and immunity tonic are culturally congruent with a religiously-sanctioned system — arguably more defensible here than in any other Dabur market. Second, Sudan is the naturals source, which is both an asset and a landmine: the gum-arabic, senna, karkadeh and frankincense heartland gives Dabur an authentic "made-with-what-you-grow" recovery story — but gum arabic is right now a war-finance commodity (RSF-controlled, smuggled, relabelled), so it is a powerful recovery-era hook that must wait for a certified conflict-free supply. Third, the incumbent is loose and unbranded: the real competitor is the attarin trade and local honey — cheap, trusted, but adulteration-prone — so Dabur's wedge is branded, tested, halal purity at a sachet price, the one thing the loose trade cannot offer. The move is a WATCH-window pre-position, not a launch: (1) sign a pharma-wholesale importer MoU (the FX-holding gate) + the attarin/grocery lane; (2) build a pre-cleared, halal-certified, food-claim NMPB dossier (a honey/senna product on nutrition claims enters faster than one making therapeutic claims); (3) ready a sachet-format Tier-1 trio — Honey + Honitus + senna/mint digestive — and seed the diaspora. Frame "natural herbal halal, Sunnah-aligned," never "Ayurveda." Do not source Sudanese gum arabic now, do not make therapeutic claims that trigger the heavy medicine dossier, and do not use Hindu-devotional imagery. The LEAP lesson (C13): own the "tibb-nabawi × tested-purity × halal" intersection, in real Sudanese Arabic. Reputation Bet (C18): if Dabur pre-positions the halal, sachet, food-claim trio with a Port Sudan importer and a ready dossier before a durable ceasefire, it achieves first-registered-branded-herbal-OTC status, beating GCC/Egyptian rivals to shelf within 12 months of the Khartoum corridor reopening — or NMPB stays non-functional and Sudan holds as WATCH.

C4 — Demand engine: Prophetic medicine + the naturals heartland

The demand engine runs on two rails, both positive and religiously sanctioned in ~97%-Muslim Sudan. Rail 1 — tibb nabawi (طب نبوي): honey, black seed, senna, dates, hijama (cupping), olive oil — mainstream, hadith-endorsed, overlaid with strong Sufi herbal tradition. This religiously legitimizes Dabur's honey/Honitus/digestive lines. Rail 2 — the naturals heartland: Sudan grows the very botanicals Dabur formulates around.

The naturals heartland (the crux — with a red flag). Sudan is the world's dominant gum-arabic source (~80%; hashab/Acacia senegal premium, talh/Acacia seyal lesser), traditionally used for sore throat, cough, demulcent, dysentery; a top-3 senna exporter (laxative); the origin of karkadeh (hibiscus) (its 4th-largest export crop, an everyday drink + cold/BP remedy); and a frankincense/luban (Boswellia papyrifera) source — which ties to Dabur Shallaki (Boswellia serrata), though the species differ, so position on the boswellia family story, not identity. But gum arabic is right now a war-finance commodity: RSF controls the Kordofan/Darfur acacia belt, taxes and loots it (a single Kordofan haul valued $75m), and it is smuggled via Chad/CAR/South Sudan and relabelled to defeat conflict-free certification. Any Dabur gum-arabic sourcing today carries a live conflict-financing exposure — treat "Chad-origin" post-2023 gum as suspect relabelled Sudanese product, and do not build a sourcing story on it now.

So what: the heartland is a genuine recovery-era right-to-believe — honey (cough+immunity), senna (digestive), karkadeh-adjacency and boswellia (joints) are culturally native, religiously sanctioned OTC positionings. The disciplined move is to ride the belief and the finished-product lanes now, and defer the raw gum-arabic sourcing story until it can be certified conflict-free.

Why tibb nabawi makes this the most defensible herbal-OTC market Dabur has. In most markets Dabur must argue that natural is credible; in Sudan the argument is already won, and won at the highest possible authority. Honey is not merely "healthy" — it is Qur'anically endorsed ("therein is healing for mankind," Surah An-Nahl); black seed is hadith-declared "a cure for everything but death"; and senna, dates, hijama and olive oil sit inside the same Prophetic-medicine canon, delivered through the attarin herbalist who functions as the interior's pharmacist. This means a Dabur honey or honey-cough product does not enter as "foreign wellness" — it enters as the branded, tested version of a remedy the consumer already believes God prescribed. That is a categorically stronger platform than the "modern-vs-traditional" tension Dabur navigates elsewhere, and it is why the OTC build, though gated by war and registration, has the deepest latent demand of Sudan's four categories. The disciplined reading is that the belief is not the risk — the supply chain, the registration throughput and the reputational/FX gates are. Strategist read (opinion): when the demand is religiously pre-sold, the winning brand is the one that shows up trustworthy and available — which is exactly the tested-purity, halal-certified, registered posture Dabur can build during the WATCH window while the loose trade cannot.

Payment, registration & the two gates that decide OTC. Two gates make or break the OTC build, both distinct from the other categories. The FX/payment gate is the same war-economy stack — no LCs, USD/hawala settlement with a 10–30% conversion drag, three co-existing exchange rates — but it bites harder here because the pharma-wholesale importer who holds USD holds the shelf: entry is winning that importer, not building retail. The registration gate (NMPB) is unique to OTC: the National Medicines & Poisons Board (Medicines & Poisons Act 2009) requires a full marketing- authorisation dossier for medicines, but the food/supplement boundary is loosely codified — a honey or senna product sold on nutrition claims enters materially faster than one making explicit therapeutic claims. NMPB's wartime capacity is degraded (HQ in the Khartoum war zone), so the recovery-window discipline is to keep a pre-built, halal-certified, food-claim dossier ready to file the moment throughput restores, and to sequence Honitus-as-medicine behind it. The strategic consequence is concrete: Dabur Honey and the senna/mint digestive can move on the fast food lane through the attarin and grocery channels, while Honitus and any therapeutic SKU wait for the medicine dossier — a two-speed build that lets the trust-and-cash engine run while the heavier registration clears.
