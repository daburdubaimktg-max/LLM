C1 · Executive summary — the one screen

So what. Malaysian oral care (~US$275–290M, +2.5%/yr) is a fortress market with a fault line running through it. The fortress: Colgate (~35–45% est., 64.5% household penetration) and Darlie (~20–25% est.) own cavity-science and whitening; Southern Lion's Johor plant owns the value tier; twin-packs and 25–35% promo depth make list price a fiction. The fault line: Malaysia's mouths are clinically gum-sick (94% of adults have periodontal disease, 85% caries, only 57% brush twice daily, 35.7% of men smoke, the highest sugar intake in ASEAN) while the market sells whitening — and the one product code that speaks gum-health in all three cultural languages at once is the miswak. Dabur Meswak is the world's scaled miswak-built toothpaste: sunnah-coded for the Malay majority (the siwak is fasting-safe under the Shafi'i reading that makes daytime brushing makruh — a Ramadan wedge no brand owns), Ayurveda-coded for the Indian cohort (already buying it grey at ValueBazaar), natural-trend-coded for urban Chinese/K-beauty consumers. Incumbents have reduced miswak to a flavour variant (Colgate Kayu Sugi, Safi Kayu Sugi, F&W Kayu Sugi, Mu'min BSugi); nobody has built the gum-care brand on it with clinical framing and JAKIM certification — Sensodyne, the only clinical player, has no halal cert; Mu'min, the halal leader, has no clinical equity. SUGI 2.0 takes that open triple: Meswak as sunnah-science gum care at RM7.90–9.90/170g, cosmetic-notified (weeks), JAKIM-certified (the gate), launched soft at Deepavali alongside Hair, mainstreamed through the Ramadan–Raya 2027 window with a "Ujian Gusi 7 Hari" (7-day gum test) mechanic and the unowned bersugi conversation. Ask: trademark clearance ("Meswak" vs Al-Meswak Mu'min Sdn Bhd — the deck's #1 legal risk), the JAKIM dependency confirmed on the critical path, and BIG CARING as the first pharmacy partner.

C2 · Market & sub-segment map + the Claim Ladder

So what: a slow-growing, twin-pack, promo-heavy category whose growth pockets are exactly where Dabur's equities sit — herbal/natural, mouthwash (alcohol-free mandatory for the Malay segment), and premium-problem niches (sensitivity, gum). The Claim Ladder is friendlier than it looks: fluoride toothpaste ≤1,500ppm is a cosmetic notification (days–2 weeks), so Meswak's regulatory entry is trivially fast — the real gates are halal certification and the KKLIU rule if any SKU ever makes therapeutic claims.

C3 · Competition — the fortress, the halal flank, and the open cells

So what: the shelf is a three-army occupation (Colgate science, Darlie whitening, Southern Lion value) with a permanent but sub-scale Muslim-owned flank (Mu'min, Zaitun, Safi oral). The decisive competitive fact: every incumbent has already validated the miswak as an ingredient — and none has built a brand on it. Colgate Kayu Sugi, Safi Kayu Sugi, F&W Kayu Sugi and Mu'min BSugi are all variants inside other stories. The second fact: the two premium levers are un-combined — Sensodyne has clinical authority without halal; Mu'min has halal authority without clinical. Meswak stands where they don't overlap.

C4 · Consumer & culture — need-states, cohorts, and the Ramadan fiqh map
