C1 · Executive summary

The play. Malaysia's skincare pool is ~US$787M/RM3.7bn (2024, EMR) growing 4–6% on a fair midpoint of diverging forecasts, inside a >US$2.3bn beauty market — and its structure is unique in this engagement: halal is not a differentiator here, it is the entry ticket, and one brand holds the fortress. Safi (Wipro Unza) owns the Malay-mass shelf — historically >48% of halal personal care, #1 in facial cleansers and moisturisers, its own research institute, "Selamat dan Suci" trust codes, RM1m Raya scholarship programs — at RM8–25 price points. Attacking that head-on is capital destruction. But the market has two proven side doors. First, ingredient territories are unclaimed: Safi owns gold, SKINTIFIC owns ceramide/barrier, Hada Labo owns hyaluronic — nobody owns rose, and nobody owns turmeric, despite kunyit being literally the ingredient of the Malay pre-wedding radiance ritual (mandi lulur/kunyit) and rose water being festival-and-hospitality grammar. Second, the TikTok Shop route is proven: SKINTIFIC went from zero to TikTok Sales Champion (Indonesia+Malaysia 2022) and ~US$114M SEA sales in two years, then converted virality into full Guardian distribution — beauty is TikTok Shop Malaysia's #1 GMV category (~14%, Q1 2024). Dabur's wedge: Gulabari (already selling unmanaged in Brickfields today, no official notification) rebuilt as the rose ritual + Raya gifting brand at RM12.90–14.90 (+ RM24.90 gift twin-pack), and a turmeric "cerah berseri" radiance line under Dermoviva (currently absent from Malaysia = clean-slate naming) at RM11–16 — both NPRA-notified for RM50/SKU in weeks, both JAKIM-halal-gated before Malay-market spend. Size band: US$2.5–6M cluster retail by Y3 (C6). Kill-switch: same JAKIM gate as the OTC deck — no recognised halal cert, no Malay-market launch; and never, anywhere, the word "whitening"/putih.

C4 · Consumer & culture — cerah berseri, not putih

Three consumer nations, one calendar. For the Malay majority (~60%), halal is a hygiene factor (its absence de-selects; its presence says nothing), the rising code is mesra wuduk (wudhu-friendly — water-permeable claims spreading from nail polish into skincare), and the beauty ideal has completed a generational migration: from putih (white) to "cerah berseri" (bright-radiant) with open celebration of kuning langsat (the classic golden-fair Malay complexion). Turmeric is not a discovery here — mandi lulur/kunyit is the pre-wedding radiance ritual — it simply has no modern branded format. Hijab-wearing consumers report T-zone oil and hijab-line breakouts (the maskne analogue) and favour lightweight gels — the insight SKINTIFIC's textures and Safi Shayla ride; quantified incidence is a source gap queued in the appendix. Raya is the year's beauty Everest: prep content starts 60 days out (Safi's Rejab-to-Ramadan countdown proves the media logic), gifting sets spike, and "Raya-ready skin" is an explicit genre. Chinese Malaysians (~23%) skew K/J-beauty and premium drugstore; Indian Malaysians (~7%) are the live Gulabari beachhead — it already sells in Brickfields at ValueBazaar, unmanaged.

DEEP THINK on W1 (Gulabari). Inversion: how does a rose brand die here? (1) Commodity gravity — RM3 air mawar on every Shopee page; the answer is the UAE deck's answer re-derived: sell formats and ritual, not water (toner-mist, gift twin-pack, bridal minis) and price-anchor against Garnier's rose micellar (RM20.43), not against commodity mawar. (2) "Indian brand" friction on the Malay shelf — mitigated by leading with the halal mark + ritual grammar ("air mawar untuk seri Raya-mu") and by the fact that rose is already inside Malay festive culture; the brand joins a ritual, it doesn't import one. (3) Safi response — Safi could add rose SKUs, but its architecture is benefit-led (white/gold/sensitive), not ingredient-ritual-led; a Safi rose SKU validates the code while Gulabari owns the name "rose" outright. Second-order: if the Raya twin-pack works, grocery will demand it year-round at promo depth — protect seasonality by making the gift pack genuinely seasonal (dated Raya sleeve) while the core toner runs year-round. Base-rate: SKINTIFIC proved TikTok→drugstore conversion in this exact market; Wardah proved a second halal brand can wedge in behind Safi on purpose-coded campaigns; kelulut honey (OTC deck) proved heritage-ritual premiumisation. Kill-switch: if after two quarters TikTok+Shopee GMV <RM100k/quarter or ≥70% of volume is diaspora-channel, hold Gulabari as a managed diaspora brand and re-scope.
