Claim Ladder — what can legally and credibly be said

Shampoos, oils, tonics and masks are cosmetics under the ASEAN Cosmetic Directive regime (DAV notification via a Vietnam-based Responsible Company — macro §M7: ~1–3 months, ~VND 500k/SKU official fee, 5-year validity; the new Cosmetics Decree targeted in force 1 Jul 2026 tightens CGMP and surveillance — file under current rules, build dossiers to the new standard). Disease-treatment language reclassifies the product as a drug. The amended Advertising Law (eff. 1 Jan 2026) adds influencer-disclosure (#quảngcáo), genuine-use requirements and criminal liability for false advertising — a moat for a proof-led brand, a trap for over-claimers.
