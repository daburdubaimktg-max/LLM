The Claim Ladder — what a toothpaste may say in Vietnam

Toothpaste is a cosmetic (oral-hygiene preparation) under the ASEAN Cosmetic Directive as implemented by Circular 06/2011/TT-BYT — administered by the Drug Administration of Vietnam (DAV); notification is online under Circular 34/2025 and a full new Cosmetics Decree (CGMP, tighter surveillance, recall powers) is targeted in force 1 July 2026 (macro §M7/T17). File under current rules; build the dossier to the new standard. Fluoride is permitted within the ACD annex limit (≤0.15% F, ~1,500 ppm) M re-validate the exact Vietnamese annex print per formula.

C3 · Competition — the fortress, the wounded giant, the herbal insurgents

So what: Vietnam oral care is the single most instructive M&A cautionary tale in ASEAN FMCG. Both global majors entered by buying the two local champions in 1995; Unilever kept the Vietnamese name and built a national-purpose fortress; Colgate killed its acquisition and has fought uphill for thirty years. The lesson for Dabur is structural: in this category, Vietnamese identity is a moat, foreign identity is a fee — so Dabur must enter where foreignness is an asset (herbal authenticity, like Korean Bamboo Salt and Thai Twin Lotus did), never where it is a fee (mass family paste).
