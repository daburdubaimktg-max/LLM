# COMPANY RESEARCH SKILL — PART 2
## Brand, Product, Competitive & E-Commerce Intelligence

### When to Trigger
Use when the user says "BRAND RESEARCH" or "COMPETITIVE INTEL" or when deeper product/brand-level analysis is needed after completing Part 1. Also trigger when user asks about specific brands, products, competitive dynamics, e-commerce performance, or social media campaigns.

## MODULE 8: Brand Portfolio Analysis

**For each brand in the target company's relevant portfolio:**

### Research checklist:
- Brand positioning and tagline
- Target consumer (age, gender, income, need-state)
- Product range (variants, pack sizes, price points)
- Market share (value and volume, by channel if available)
- Brand health metrics (awareness, consideration, trial, repeat — estimate from available data)
- Innovation pipeline (recent launches in last 12 months, announced upcoming)
- HCP/expert recommendation status (is it recommended by professionals?)
- Key campaigns (last 2-3 major campaigns with reach/results)

### Output format:
- Brand snapshot card (one per brand)
- Portfolio architecture map (how brands relate to each other: price tiers, need-states, consumer segments)
- Portfolio gaps and white spaces

## MODULE 9: Product Formulation & Science

**For technical/science-based products (pharma, consumer health, personal care):**

### Research checklist (from product packaging, Amazon listings, official websites):
- Complete ingredient list (INCI format where available)
- Active ingredients with concentrations
- Key differentiating ingredient vs competitors
- Patent/proprietary technology status
- Clinical evidence base (published studies, claims substantiation)
- Regulatory status (certifications, endorsements — e.g., IDA, ADA, dermatologist-tested)

### Output format:
- Ingredient comparison table across competitors:
  | Ingredient | Category | Mechanism of Action | Consumer Benefit | Typical % | Found In |
- Formulation architecture comparison (e.g., silica-based vs CaCO₃-based for toothpaste)
- Estimated raw material cost per kg (with assumptions stated)
- Gross margin estimation by product
- Strategic implications (what the formulation enables/constrains in terms of pricing, positioning, claims)

## MODULE 12: Competitive War Room

**For each major competitor (3-5 competitors):**

### Research checklist:
- Company overview (parent, size, relevant division)
- Market share in the target category
- Product range and pricing vs target company
- A&P spend (estimate from annual report or industry data)
- Recent strategic moves (launches, campaigns, price changes, distribution expansion)
- Known vulnerabilities (product gaps, quality issues, regulatory challenges)
- Likely response to target company's growth initiatives

### Output format:
- Competitor profile card (one per competitor)
- Competitive positioning map (2x2 or price-value map)
- Share of voice analysis (who's spending more on A&P?)
- Competitive response scenarios: "If we do X, competitor likely does Y within Z months"

## MODULE 13: Claims & Regulatory Landscape

**For regulated categories (consumer health, OTC, food supplements):**

### Research checklist:
- Recent regulatory rulings affecting the category (e.g., ASCI rulings, FDA warnings)
- Claims substantiation requirements (what evidence is needed for what claims)
- Current claims being made by target company and competitors
- Vulnerable claims (claims that could be challenged)
- Opportunity claims (claims no one is making that could be substantiated)

### Output format:
- Regulatory landscape summary
- Claims comparison table: | Brand | Claim | Evidence Type | Vulnerability |
- Recommended claims strategy (offensive and defensive)

---

## MODULE 14: HCP / Expert Channel Mapping

**For categories with professional recommendation (oral care, skincare, OTC health):**

### Research checklist:
- Which professionals recommend in this category? (dentists, dermatologists, GPs, pharmacists)
- How does the target company engage with HCPs? (detailing, sampling, conferences, KOL programmes)
- How many HCPs in the target market? (total universe, panel size)
- What's the recommendation-to-purchase conversion rate? (estimate)
- Competitor HCP strategies
- Digital HCP engagement (platforms, content, CRM)

### Output format:
- HCP ecosystem map (which professionals, how they interact with consumers, how the brand engages them)
- HCP engagement programme assessment (strengths, gaps, recommendations)
- Competitive HCP comparison

## MODULE 15: Synthesis & Interview Readiness

After completing all relevant modules, produce:

### 15A: 10 Things You MUST Know Before the Interview
Numbered list of the 10 most important facts/insights, each with a one-line "so what" for the interview.

### 15B: 5 Impressive Things to Say in the Interview
Specific data points, insights, or proposals that will demonstrate you've done deeper research than other candidates.

### 15C: 3 Questions to Ask the Interviewer
Questions that demonstrate strategic thinking and genuine curiosity about the business — not generic questions.

### 15D: Key Numbers Cheat Sheet
The 8-10 numbers you must know cold (revenue, growth rate, market share, key brand metrics, competitive benchmarks).

## Quality Standards

- Use web search to verify all data points — don't rely on training data for current metrics
- Provide clickable links to all online sources (Amazon listings, social media campaigns, news articles)
- Distinguish between confirmed data and estimates — flag assumptions explicitly
- Apply the "Pharmacist Test": if a pharmacy manager in a Tier 2 city wouldn't understand this insight, it's too abstract
- Cross-reference e-commerce data with financial data: does the online pricing align with the margin story in the annual report?
- Every module ends with "Strategic Implications" — what does this mean for the role/interview?
