# Common Words and Names — Phonetic Breakdowns

Pre-derived breakdowns of the 50+ most common ISKCON Sanskrit terms and names. Load whenever a familiar term appears so you don't have to derive from scratch. Each entry follows the format:

**Word** [IAST] — *syllable break* (beat count) — articulation notes

## Names of the Lord and His expansions

**Kṛṣṇa** — *Kṛṣ-ṇa* (2 beats)
- *Kṛṣ*: guttural *k* + vocalic *ṛ* (tongue vibrates near hard palate, "kri") + retroflex *ṣ* (tongue curled back, "sh")
- *ṇa*: retroflex *ṇ* (tongue curled back) + short *a*
- Final 'a' preserved. **Never "Krishn".**

**Govinda** — *Go-vin-da* (4 beats)
- *Go*: labial *g*+ long *o* (2 beats)
- *vin*: labio-dental *v* + short *i* + nasal *n* (dental)
- *da*: dental *d* + short *a*

**Viṣṇu** — *Viṣ-ṇu* (2 beats)
- *Viṣ*: labio-dental *v* + short *i* + retroflex *ṣ* (tongue curled, "sh")
- *ṇu*: retroflex *ṇ* (tongue curled) + short *u*
- **Never "Vish-noo".** Both ṣ and ṇ are retroflex.

**Hari** — *Ha-ri* (2 beats)
- *Ha*: guttural *h* + short *a*
- *ri*: cerebral *r* + short *i*

**Rāma** — *Rā-ma* (3 beats)
- *Rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ma*: labial *m* + short *a*
- **Never "Ram".** Final 'a' preserved.

**Nārāyaṇa** — *Nā-rā-ya-ṇa* (5 beats)
- *Nā*: dental *n* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ya*: palatal *y* + short *a*
- *ṇa*: retroflex *ṇ* (curled tongue) + short *a*

**Vāsudeva** — *Vā-su-de-va* (5 beats)
- *Vā*: labio-dental *v* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *su*: dental *s* + short *u*
- *de*: dental *d* + long *e* (2 beats — diphthong)
- *va*: labio-dental *v* + short *a*

**Caitanya** — *Cai-tan-ya* (4 beats)
- *Cai*: palatal *c* (= "ch") + diphthong *ai* (2 beats)
- *tan*: dental *t* + short *a* + dental *n*
- *ya*: palatal *y* + short *a*
- **Never "Chaitanya" with English "ch"** — it's the same sound but rendered *c* in IAST.

**Nityānanda** — *Nit-yā-nan-da* (5 beats)
- *Nit*: dental *n* + short *i* + dental *t*
- *yā*: palatal *y* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *nan*: dental *n* + short *a* + dental *n*
- *da*: dental *d* + short *a*

**Balarāma** — *Ba-la-rā-ma* (5 beats)
- *Ba*: labial *b* + short *a*
- *la*: dental *l* + short *a*
- *rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ma*: labial *m* + short *a*

**Rādhā** — *Rā-dhā* (4 beats)
- *Rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *dhā*: dental *d* with breath puff + long *ā* (2 beats)
- **Never "Radha" with short vowels.** Both vowels are long.
- Final ā is *not* dropped. *Rādhā*, not *Rādh*.

## Devotee names from the lineage

**Arjuna** — *Ar-ju-na* (3 beats)
- *Ar*: short *a* + cerebral *r*
- *ju*: palatal *j* + short *u*
- *na*: dental *n* + short *a*
- **Never "Arjun".** Final 'a' preserved.

**Yudhiṣṭhira** — *Yu-dhiṣ-ṭhi-ra* (4 beats)
- *Yu*: palatal *y* + short *u*
- *dhiṣ*: dental *d* + breath + short *i* + retroflex *ṣ* (curled tongue, "sh")
- *ṭhi*: retroflex *ṭ* + breath + short *i*
- *ra*: cerebral *r* + short *a*
- Note the **two retroflex sounds** (ṣ and ṭh) — tongue curls back twice.

**Bhīma** — *Bhī-ma* (3 beats)
- *Bhī*: labial *b* with breath + long *ī* (2 beats)
- *ma*: labial *m* + short *a*

**Pāṇḍava** — *Pāṇ-ḍa-va* (4 beats)
- *Pāṇ*: labial *p* + long *ā* (2 beats) + retroflex *ṇ* (curled tongue)
- *ḍa*: retroflex *ḍ* (curled tongue) + short *a*
- *va*: labio-dental *v* + short *a*
- **Two retroflex sounds in a row.**

**Pāñcāla** — *Pāñ-cā-la* (5 beats)
- *Pāñ*: labial *p* + long *ā* (2 beats) + palatal *ñ* (nasal at soft palate)
- *cā*: palatal *c* (= "ch") + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *la*: dental *l* + short *a*

**Prahlāda** — *Prah-lā-da* (4 beats)
- *Prah*: labial *p* + cerebral *r* + short *a* + guttural *h*
- *lā*: dental *l* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *da*: dental *d* + short *a*

**Hanumān** — *Ha-nu-mān* (4 beats)
- *Ha*: guttural *h* + short *a*
- *nu*: dental *n* + short *u*
- *mān*: labial *m* + long *ā* (2 beats) + dental *n*
- **Final 'n' is on its own — no vowel after.** This is a *halanta* (consonant ending).

**Vyāsa** — *Vyā-sa* (3 beats)
- *Vyā*: labio-dental *v* + palatal *y* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *sa*: dental *s* + short *a*

## Place names

**Vṛndāvana** — *Vṛn-dā-va-na* (5 beats)
- *Vṛn*: labio-dental *v* + vocalic *ṛ* (tongue vibrates near hard palate) + dental *n*
- *dā*: dental *d* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *va*: labio-dental *v* + short *a*
- *na*: dental *n* + short *a*
- **Never "Vrindavan".** Final 'a' preserved.

**Mathurā** — *Ma-thu-rā* (4 beats)
- *Ma*: labial *m* + short *a*
- *thu*: dental *t* + breath + short *u* (NOT English "th")
- *rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)

**Goloka** — *Go-lo-ka* (5 beats)
- *Go*: guttural *g* + long *o* (2 beats — diphthong)
- *lo*: dental *l* + long *o* (2 beats)
- *ka*: guttural *k* + short *a*

**Dvārakā** — *Dvā-ra-kā* (5 beats)
- *Dvā*: dental *d* + labio-dental *v* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ra*: cerebral *r* + short *a*
- *kā*: guttural *k* + long *ā* (2 beats)

**Ayodhyā** — *A-yo-dhyā* (5 beats)
- *A*: short *a* (independent vowel, throat)
- *yo*: palatal *y* + long *o* (2 beats)
- *dhyā*: dental *d* + breath + palatal *y* + long *ā* (2 beats)

**Hastināpura** — *Has-ti-nā-pu-ra* (6 beats)
- *Has*: guttural *h* + short *a* + dental *s*
- *ti*: dental *t* + short *i*
- *nā*: dental *n* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *pu*: labial *p* + short *u*
- *ra*: cerebral *r* + short *a*

**Kurukṣetra** — *Ku-ru-kṣe-tra* (5 beats)
- *Ku*: guttural *k* + short *u*
- *ru*: cerebral *r* + short *u*
- *kṣe*: guttural *k* + retroflex *ṣ* (curled tongue) + long *e* (2 beats — diphthong)
- *tra*: dental *t* + cerebral *r* + short *a*
- The conjunct *kṣ* is one breath unit: *k* immediately into retroflex *ṣ*.

## Scriptures and texts

**Bhagavad-gītā** — *Bha-ga-vad Gī-tā* (7 beats)
- *Bha*: labial *b* + breath + short *a*
- *ga*: guttural *g* + short *a*
- *vad*: labio-dental *v* + short *a* + dental *d* (halanta — no vowel after)
- *Gī*: guttural *g* + long *ī* (2 beats)
- *tā*: dental *t* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- **Never "Bagavad Gee-tah".** The *bh* has a breath; *ī* is held 2 beats; *ā* is held 2 beats.

**Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam** — *Śrī-mad Bhā-ga-va-tam* (8 beats)
- *Śrī*: palatal *ś* (soft "sh") + cerebral *r* + long *ī* (2 beats)
- *mad*: labial *m* + short *a* + dental *d* (halanta)
- *Bhā*: labial *b* + breath + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ga*: guttural *g* + short *a*
- *va*: labio-dental *v* + short *a*
- *tam*: dental *t* + short *a* + labial *m* (halanta)

**Caitanya-caritāmṛta** — *Cai-tan-ya Ca-ri-tā-mṛ-ta* (10 beats)
- *Cai-tan-ya* (see Caitanya above, 4 beats)
- *Ca*: palatal *c* (= "ch") + short *a*
- *ri*: cerebral *r* + short *i*
- *tā*: dental *t* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *mṛ*: labial *m* + vocalic *ṛ* (tongue vibrates)
- *ta*: dental *t* + short *a*

**Rāmāyaṇa** — *Rā-mā-ya-ṇa* (6 beats)
- *Rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *mā*: labial *m* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ya*: palatal *y* + short *a*
- *ṇa*: retroflex *ṇ* (curled tongue) + short *a*

**Mahābhārata** — *Ma-hā-bhā-ra-ta* (7 beats)
- *Ma*: labial *m* + short *a*
- *hā*: guttural *h* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *bhā*: labial *b* + breath + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ra*: cerebral *r* + short *a*
- *ta*: dental *t* + short *a*

**Upaniṣad** — *U-pa-ni-ṣad* (4 beats)
- *U*: short *u* (independent labial vowel)
- *pa*: labial *p* + short *a*
- *ni*: dental *n* + short *i*
- *ṣad*: retroflex *ṣ* (curled tongue, "sh") + short *a* + dental *d* (halanta)

**Purāṇa** — *Pu-rā-ṇa* (4 beats)
- *Pu*: labial *p* + short *u*
- *rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ṇa*: retroflex *ṇ* (curled tongue) + short *a*

**Veda** — *Ve-da* (3 beats)
- *Ve*: labio-dental *v* + long *e* (2 beats — diphthong)
- *da*: dental *d* + short *a*

**Vedānta** — *Ve-dān-ta* (5 beats)
- *Ve*: labio-dental *v* + long *e* (2 beats)
- *dān*: dental *d* + long *ā* (2 beats) + dental *n*
- *ta*: dental *t* + short *a*

## Devotional / philosophical vocabulary

**Bhakti** — *Bhak-ti* (2 beats)
- *Bhak*: labial *b* + breath + short *a* + guttural *k* (halanta)
- *ti*: dental *t* + short *i*

**Bhakta** — *Bhak-ta* (2 beats)
- *Bhak*: labial *b* + breath + short *a* + guttural *k* (halanta)
- *ta*: dental *t* + short *a*

**Dharma** — *Dhar-ma* (2 beats)
- *Dhar*: dental *d* + breath + short *a* + cerebral *r* (halanta)
- *ma*: labial *m* + short *a*

**Karma** — *Kar-ma* (2 beats)
- *Kar*: guttural *k* + short *a* + cerebral *r* (halanta)
- *ma*: labial *m* + short *a*

**Mokṣa** — *Mok-ṣa* (2 beats)
- *Mok*: labial *m* + long *o* (2 beats — diphthong) + guttural *k* (halanta)
- *ṣa*: retroflex *ṣ* (curled tongue, "sh") + short *a*
- Conjunct *kṣ* often pronounced as one unit.

**Yoga** — *Yo-ga* (3 beats)
- *Yo*: palatal *y* + long *o* (2 beats — diphthong)
- *ga*: guttural *g* + short *a*

**Sādhu** — *Sā-dhu* (3 beats)
- *Sā*: dental *s* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *dhu*: dental *d* + breath + short *u*

**Sādhanā** — *Sā-dha-nā* (5 beats)
- *Sā*: dental *s* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *dha*: dental *d* + breath + short *a*
- *nā*: dental *n* + long *ā* (2 beats)

**Guru** — *Gu-ru* (2 beats)
- *Gu*: guttural *g* + short *u*
- *ru*: cerebral *r* + short *u*

**Mantra** — *Man-tra* (2 beats)
- *Man*: labial *m* + short *a* + dental *n*
- *tra*: dental *t* + cerebral *r* + short *a*

**Japa** — *Ja-pa* (2 beats)
- *Ja*: palatal *j* + short *a*
- *pa*: labial *p* + short *a*

**Kīrtana** — *Kīr-ta-na* (4 beats)
- *Kīr*: guttural *k* + long *ī* (2 beats) + cerebral *r* (halanta)
- *ta*: dental *t* + short *a*
- *na*: dental *n* + short *a*

**Saṅkīrtana** — *Saṅ-kīr-ta-na* (5 beats)
- *Saṅ*: dental *s* + short *a* + guttural nasal *ṅ* (back of tongue at velum)
- *kīr*: guttural *k* + long *ī* (2 beats) + cerebral *r*
- *ta*: dental *t* + short *a*
- *na*: dental *n* + short *a*
- The *ṅ* before *k* is the guttural nasal — same articulation point as *k*.

**Prasāda** — *Pra-sā-da* (4 beats)
- *Pra*: labial *p* + cerebral *r* + short *a*
- *sā*: dental *s* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *da*: dental *d* + short *a*

**Ātmā** — *Āt-mā* (4 beats)
- *Āt*: long *ā* (2 beats) + dental *t* (halanta)
- *mā*: labial *m* + long *ā* (2 beats)

**Brahman** — *Brah-man* (2 beats)
- *Brah*: labial *b* + cerebral *r* + short *a* + guttural *h*
- *man*: labial *m* + short *a* + dental *n* (halanta)
- Note: this is *Brahman* (the Absolute). *Brāhmaṇa* (the priestly class) is different — see below.

**Brāhmaṇa** — *Brāh-ma-ṇa* (4 beats)
- *Brāh*: labial *b* + cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats) + guttural *h*
- *ma*: labial *m* + short *a*
- *ṇa*: retroflex *ṇ* (curled tongue) + short *a*

**Avatāra** — *A-va-tā-ra* (5 beats)
- *A*: short *a*
- *va*: labio-dental *v* + short *a*
- *tā*: dental *t* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ra*: cerebral *r* + short *a*

**Lakṣmī** — *Lak-ṣmī* (3 beats)
- *Lak*: dental *l* + short *a* + guttural *k*
- *ṣmī*: retroflex *ṣ* (curled tongue, "sh") + labial *m* + long *ī* (2 beats)

**Gopī** — *Go-pī* (4 beats)
- *Go*: guttural *g* + long *o* (2 beats — diphthong)
- *pī*: labial *p* + long *ī* (2 beats)

**Gopāla** — *Go-pā-la* (5 beats)
- *Go*: guttural *g* + long *o* (2 beats)
- *pā*: labial *p* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *la*: dental *l* + short *a*

## Honorific terms

**Śrī** — *Śrī* (2 beats)
- Palatal *ś* (soft "sh") + cerebral *r* + long *ī* (2 beats)
- The standard auspicious prefix. **Never "Sri" with English 's'.**

**Śrīla** — *Śrī-la* (3 beats)
- *Śrī*: as above (2 beats)
- *la*: dental *l* + short *a*

**Prabhupāda** — *Pra-bhu-pā-da* (5 beats)
- *Pra*: labial *p* + cerebral *r* + short *a*
- *bhu*: labial *b* + breath + short *u*
- *pā*: labial *p* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *da*: dental *d* + short *a*

**Mahārāja** — *Ma-hā-rā-ja* (6 beats)
- *Ma*: labial *m* + short *a*
- *hā*: guttural *h* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *rā*: cerebral *r* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *ja*: palatal *j* + short *a*

**Gosvāmī** — *Go-svā-mī* (6 beats)
- *Go*: guttural *g* + long *o* (2 beats)
- *svā*: dental *s* + labio-dental *v* + long *ā* (2 beats)
- *mī*: labial *m* + long *ī* (2 beats)

**Ācārya** — *Ā-cār-ya* (5 beats)
- *Ā*: long *ā* (2 beats) — independent vowel
- *cār*: palatal *c* (= "ch") + long *ā* (2 beats) + cerebral *r*
- *ya*: palatal *y* + short *a*
